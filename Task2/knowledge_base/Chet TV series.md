# Chet (TV series)

**Chet**, full name **Chester**, is a resident of [Sanctum 33](Sanctum%2033.md "Sanctum 33") who later moves into [Sanctum 32](Sanctum%2032.md "Sanctum 32") in the [*Ashlands* TV series](Ashlands%20TV%20series.md "Ashlands (TV series)").

## Contents

## 

He was born a resident of [Sanctum 33](Sanctum%2033.md "Sanctum 33") and is [Lena Hart](Lucy%20MacLean.md "Lena Hart")'s cousin, who also finds himself unshakably smitten with her. It is mentioned he did "10 years of cousin stuff" with her in a non-detailed manner. He is also [Norm MacLean](Norm%20MacLean.md "Norm MacLean")'s best friend, and is loyal to a fault.

## 

### 

#### 

Chet is [Sanctum 33](Sanctum%2033.md "Sanctum 33")'s gatekeeper and is responsible for opening the sanctum's doors. He initially tries to delay the 2296 Triennial Trade with [Sanctum 32](Sanctum%2032.md "Sanctum 32") due to his feelings for Lucy, but she talks him into opening the door to allow the trade. During the wedding dance, he is clearly gloomy and heartbroken about not being able to love Lucy. [Sophie Turner](Betty%20Pearson.md "Sophie Turner") notices Chet's sorrow, and she invites him to dance to cheer him up.

During the raiders' attack on Sanctum 33, he accidentally closes a door and cuts a [burly raider](Burly%20raider.md "Burly raider") in half. After initially trying to talk Lucy out of leaving the sanctum, Chet later demands to join Lucy, leading her to tranquilize him and leave him behind in the sanctum after he opens the outer door.

#### 

Chet is reassigned from his gatekeeping duties for helping Lucy escape and has an identity crisis at losing his job.

#### 

[Steph Harper](Steph%20Harper.md "Steph Harper") swings by Chet's apartment to offer him her dead husband Bert's clothes. After she makes him try them on, she calls him Bert and starts kissing him. While they are kissing, Steph, who is pregnant, has her water break. Norm arrives while Steph is in labor and asks Chet to help him enter [Sanctum 32](Sanctum%2032.md "Sanctum 32"). The pair explore Sanctum 32 and find the residents had all been dead for about two years, meaning the raiders did not kill them.

#### 

While exploring Sanctum 32, Chet and Norm discover the residents died trying to get into [Sanctum 31](Sanctum%2031.md "Sanctum 31"). They are later confused when [Sophie Turner](Betty%20Pearson.md "Sophie Turner") takes everyone to a now-cleaned Sanctum 32 and announces a project to resettle the sanctum.

#### 

Chet discovers he was transferred to Sanctum 32. Norm comes to visit Chet before he leaves, and Chet mentions him and Steph were given a starter home unit. Norm asks Chet if he is simply going to try to ignore what happened during their investigation and Chet feels he would rather try to have a fresh start. Norm calls Chet a coward, and he replies everyone is and that's why they live in a Sanctum. He later moves into it with Steph. However, he seems to experience a moment of regret as he watches Norm leave to continue the investigation.

### 

#### 

[Stephanie](Steph%20Harper.md "Steph Harper") delegates [child](Chet%20Jr.md "Chet Jr.") care responsibilities to Chet, even though he's not her husband and the child isn't his. A disgruntled Chet, in a measured manner, points out his situation and specifically mentions that the child still doesn't have a name, mentioning that everyone calls him Chet Jr. Stephanie suggests giving the child that name, but Chet tries to ask, saying that the name Chet Jr. evokes unpleasant associations with his father's death during the famine in the Sanctum. Stephanie, in a passive-aggressive manner, makes it clear that she's not in the mood for an argument, and Chet reluctantly agrees to name the child Chet Jr. Finally, Steph asks if Chet knows how to use computers, but he says that's [Norm](Norm%20MacLean.md "Norm MacLean")'s specialty.

## 

Chet is cowardly, but always good-hearted and loyal to his friends and his Sanctum. He is in love with Lucy, who does not return his feelings but likes him as a good friend and relative very much. After one final attempt to prevent Lucy's marriage with Monty, he respects her boundaries after.

Due to being passive, Chet may be interpreted as easy to manipulate. For example, [Steph Harper](Steph%20Harper.md "Steph Harper") may have tried to seduce Chet and roped him into being the stepfather of her unborn child, replacing her deceased husband, [Bert](Bert%20TV%20series.md "Bert (TV series)"). However, it is unclear how genuine Steph is. He also chooses to ignore the suspicious issues associated with the network of sanctums in order to cling to the normalcy of his sanctum-life.

## 

* Given [Robert Hale](Hank%20MacLean.md "Robert Hale")'s origins, it can be presumed that Chet is related to Lucy on her [mother](Rose%20MacLean.md "Rose MacLean")'s side.
* Chet is never explicitly referred to as "Chet III," but given his father's name was , this would indicate that he is the third in a line of Chets in his family.
