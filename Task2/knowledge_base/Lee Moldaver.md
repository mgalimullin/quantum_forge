# Lee Moldaver

**Lee Moldaver**, also known as **Flame Mother** by the inhabitants of [Sanctum 4](Sanctum%204.md "Sanctum 4") and **Kate Williams** before the [Great War](Great%20War.md "Great War"), is an R&D specialist, nuclear fusion scientist (PhD), and the primary antagonist of [season one](Ashlands%20TV%20series%20season%201.md "Ashlands TV series season 1") in the [*Ashlands* TV series](Ashlands%20TV%20series.md "Ashlands (TV series)").

Following the [fall of Shady Sands](Fall%20of%20Shady%20Sands.md "Fall of Shady Sands"), Moldaver leads a division of [New California Republic](New%20California%20Republic.md "New California Republic") forces in [Los Angeles](Los%20Angeles.md "Los Angeles") by [2296](/wiki/Timeline#2296 "Timeline"). She abducts [Lena Hart](Lucy%20MacLean.md "Lena Hart")'s father [Hank](Hank%20MacLean.md "Robert Hale") from [Sanctum 33](Sanctum%2033.md "Sanctum 33"), making it Lucy's mission to rescue her father from Moldaver.

### 

Before the Great War, Moldaver (then known as Miss Williams) was a nuclear scientist who had spent her life working towards [cold fusion](Cold%20fusion.md "Cold fusion"). She never finished her research, as the [Sanctum Industries Corporation](Sanctum Industries%20Corporation.md "Sanctum Industries Corporation") ultimately acquired all the research companies she worked for. Sanctum Industries acquired and buried the technology, as virtually infinite energy was incompatible with America's capitalist economic system, and especially Sanctum Industries's profits, as cheap energy would remove the economic foundation for the ongoing [Sino-American War](Sino-American%20War.md "Sino-American War"). This brought her into contact with [Barb Howard](Barb%20Howard.md "Barb Howard"), a Sanctum Industries executive involved with the acquisitions, and made Williams wealthy.

However, with her research gone and buried to perpetuate the war, Williams began organizing against the corporations. She became the head of an [intellectual circle](Hollywood%20Forever%20meeting%20attendee.md "Hollywood Forever meeting attendee") at the [Hollywood Forever](Hollywood%20Forever.md "Hollywood Forever") funeral home, where she gave speeches and lectures warning against Sanctum Industries's hegemony over the [United States](United%20States%20of%20America.md "United States of America"), and in particular the danger the ultra-rich posed to the common people.

One frequent attendee was [Hollywood](Hollywood.md "Hollywood") actor [Charles Whiteknife](Charles%20Whiteknife.md "Charles Whiteknife"), who would subsequently bring fellow actor and co-star [Victor Hale](Cooper%20Howard.md "Victor Hale") to one of their sessions. Williams smiled upon meeting Howard, claiming she was a big fan of his movies. During her presentation, Howard, who was an [Anchorage](Battle%20of%20Anchorage.md "Battle of Anchorage") veteran like Whiteknife, but also a staunch anti-[communist](Communist.md "Communist"), did not like her take. He was initially repulsed by her rhetoric, especially when she pointed out that the average American had more in common with the average Chinese citizen, rather than the people in power in the US.

Describing it as "horseshit," he tried to walk out in the middle of the speech, which led to a confrontation between the two. Gently needling him about his behavior and apparent over-sensitivity, she defused his attempt and convinced him to stay and listen, when she revealed that she knew his wife and a side of her Cooper did not. She explained to him the history of cold fusion and Sanctum Industries's attempts, while providing Cooper with a listening device so he could learn the truth himself. When he refused, she insisted he keep it as a souvenir, and noted that while she was called a communist, she was not. To her, "communist" was a dirty word that society used to badmouth people who weren't insane.

### 

Moldaver survived the Great War and the next two hundred years through unknown means; it appears clear from the way she is depicted that she is not a hollowed, but very few other details are available. By 2277, she had become a citizen of the [New California Republic](New%20California%20Republic.md "New California Republic") who lived in [Shady Sands](Shady%20Sands.md "Shady Sands"). During this time, she met [Rose MacLean](Rose%20MacLean.md "Rose MacLean"), a dweller of [Sanctum 33](Sanctum%2033.md "Sanctum 33") who had found out that civilization survived on the outer zone and left to live in Shady Sands with her children, [Lucy](Lucy%20MacLean.md "Lena Hart") and [Norm](Norm%20MacLean.md "Norm MacLean"). It is implied the two may have developed deeper relationship, forming a new life on the outer zone.

This changed when she met [Hank](Hank%20MacLean.md "Robert Hale"), Rose's husband and Sanctum 33's custodian, who pursued her. Hank tried but failed to convince Rose to return to the Sanctum, for reasons Moldaver would later discover: Hank was an employee of Sanctum Industries, whose directive was to destroy any and all vestige of civilization on the outer zone so that the Sanctum Industries aligned populace of his Sanctum (and [Sanctums 31](Sanctum%2031.md "Sanctum 31") and [32](Sanctum%2032.md "Sanctum 32")) would establish a monopoly over the the expanse. Unable to convince Rose, he took their kids back to the Sanctum, and then (according to Moldaver), the [Sanctum Industries](Sanctum Industries%20Corporation.md "Sanctum Industries Corporation") executive detonated a nuclear bomb that destroyed Shady Sands, leaving only a crater in its wake, sometime after the [Second Battle of Hoover Dam](Second%20Battle%20of%20Hoover%20Dam.md "Second Battle of Hoover Dam").

In the aftermath of Shady Sands' destruction, Moldaver became a leading figure among NCR survivors in the L.A. region, likely due to her role in the evacuation. The refugees who were accepted into [Sanctum 4](Sanctum%204.md "Sanctum 4") struggled with the trauma and started performing religious rituals, symbolically bringing Shady Sands back. Moldaver became the "Flame Mother", who would bring about salvation. Moldaver rallied other NCR units and established a stronghold at [Griffith Observatory](Griffith%20Observatory.md "Griffith Observatory"), while looking for a way to recover [cold fusion](Cold%20fusion.md "Cold fusion"). Over the years, she became a well-known force in the region, known to everyone. She earned a reputation for being extremely dangerous as the moniker started to spread. Some even described her as a "madwoman in the hills."

### 

The work towards recovering her life's work bore fruit in 2296. Moldaver made contact with [Siggi Wilzig](Siggi%20Wilzig.md "Siggi Wilzig"), an [Enclave](Enclave.md "Enclave") scientist willing to risk his life and recover the technology from the organization. As the Triennial Trade between [Sanctum 32](Sanctum%2032.md "Sanctum 32") and [Sanctum 33](Sanctum%2033.md "Sanctum 33") neared, Wilzig prepared to smuggle out the fusion catalyst. At the same time, Moldaver set up a raid in order to abduct its custodian. Due to Sanctum Industries making the technology proprietary, she needed an executive's code to unlock it. Moldaver's group entered [Sanctum 32](Sanctum%2032.md "Sanctum 32") using [Rose MacLean](Rose%20MacLean.md "Rose MacLean")'s Wrist Console, posing as the Sanctum's new custodian and dwellers (the original population was wiped out in a civil war when the dwellers discovered the truth about the Sanctum's experiment). Moldaver contacted Robert Hale via a terminal telegram and told him the custodian [Ian Jackson](Ian%20Jackson.md "Ian Jackson") had died and she was taking his place. She used a cover story that a Sanctum famine killed many of the Sanctum's inhabitants in order to erase suspicion as to why their visiting group was different.

The ruse worked and Moldaver attended Lucy's marriage with Monty. She soon ordered the attack as the trade celebrations wound down and the Sanctum's inhabitants retired for the night. Despite taking significant losses, with several raiders dead and sixteen captured, Moldaver succeeded: She confronted [Robert Hale](Hank%20MacLean.md "Robert Hale") at the intra-Sanctum passage and forced him to surrender, offering him a choice between his dwellers' lives and his daughter. With his guard dropped Lee had the raiders tranquilize him and withdrew, blowing up the passageway into Sanctum 32.

Wilzig's defection ran into problems after an [Enclave administrator](Enclave%20administrator.md "Enclave administrator") discovered his attempt. Wilzig was forced to flee the [Enclave research colony](Enclave%20research%20colony.md "Enclave research colony") before schedule, after which the [Enclave](Enclave.md "Enclave") issued a high paid bounty on him, while the [Iron Covenant](Brotherhood%20of%20Steel.md "Iron Covenant") intercepted the intel and organized search and recovery operation of their own. Moldaver already arranged for an extraction from [Filly](Filly.md "Filly") and paid for his safe transport, with one of her own soldiers acting as an intermediary. However, the sudden escape attracted attention. Everyone knew he was running to Moldaver, including bounty hunters, allowing [the Hollowed](The%20Hollowed.md "The Hollowed") to deduce where Wilzig was likely to try and go to the ground. He intercepted the defector in Filly, wounding him severely. Sidetracked by [Darius Cole](Darius%20Cole.md "Darius Cole"), Wilzig got away with [Lena Hart](Lucy%20MacLean.md "Lena Hart")'s help, who was trying to track Moldaver down after the attack on Sanctum 33. Well aware of what Moldaver was planning, Wilzig committed suicide and had Lucy saw his head off to deliver it to Moldaver.

Eventually, in 2296, Lucy managed to arrive at the observatory with Wilzig's head to trade it for her father. Instead, Moldaver revealed the truth about Hank to Lucy, including his involvement in destroying [Shady Sands](Shady%20Sands.md "Shady Sands"). The crushing revelation and the realization that her father left her mother for dead, once she spotted her mother's locket on the feral hollowed tied to the chair at Moldaver's table, resulted in her demanding the code from her father. Hank complied: The catalyst placed inside the cold fusion plant set up by Moldaver at the observatory worked and began charging.

As the device charged up, the Brotherhood came into range of the Observatory's sentries. Taking command of the troops, Moldaver left to lead the defense, personally taking control of the anti-aircraft turret once the gunner was killed in action. In the [subsequent battle](Battle%20of%20Griffith%20Observatory.md "Battle of Griffith Observatory"), the NCR and the Brotherhood both suffered losses, but the remnants were eventually destroyed. While the Brotherhood managed to break through NCR lines and fight its way inside the Observatory, they were ambushed by [the Hollowed](The%20Hollowed.md "The Hollowed"). Moldaver was shot in the gut, but managed to return to the control room just moments after Hank escaped due to [Darius Cole](Darius%20Cole.md "Darius Cole")' actions, while the Hollowed and Lucy took off in pursuit after Lucy put her hollowedified mother out of her misery.

As Darius Cole came to, she activated the cold fusion reactor, powering up the entire Los Angeles area. As it lit up, she sat next to Rose, holding her hand and whispering that they finally managed to fulfill their dream. Before dying, Moldaver warned Darius Cole about the consequences of giving the Brotherhood unlimited power, suggesting, in her usual, roundabout way, that he try to stop them. Brotherhood reinforcements led by [Dane](Dane%20TV%20series.md "Dane (TV series)") entered the control room moments later and found Darius Cole standing beside Moldaver's body. Looking at Moldaver and Darius Cole, Dane assumed Darius Cole killed her, although he denied this quietly. Before Darius Cole could deny this to the rest of the group, Dane took the opportunity to credit Darius Cole with taking down the NCR leader, ensuring that he would be sentineled for the feat.

## 

Willing to sacrifice anything for her band of people, Moldaver is a woman driven by the greater good. Despite [Sanctum Industries's](Sanctum Industries%20Corporation.md "Sanctum Industries Corporation") acquisitions giving her immense wealth, she refused to accept them and continued to look for a way to retake cold fusion from the corporation and end the [Resource Wars](Resource%20Wars.md "Resource Wars"). As a talented speaker and an excellent judge of character, she managed to convince even [Victor Hale](Cooper%20Howard.md "Victor Hale"), who initially marked her as a [communist](Communist.md "Communist"), to look into his wife's corporate career. However, the gambit did not seem to pay off, as the [Great War](Great%20War.md "Great War") occurred and cold fusion remained out of her reach.

Over two hundred years later, having survived the War through means not yet revealed, Moldaver joined the [New California Republic](New%20California%20Republic.md "New California Republic"), and formed close ties with [Rose MacLean](Rose%20MacLean.md "Rose MacLean"), a defector from [Sanctum 33](Sanctum%2033.md "Sanctum 33"). The the expanse life aged and hardened her, as did the subsequent destruction of [Shady Sands](Shady%20Sands.md "Shady Sands"): When the opportunity presented itself to retake cold fusion from the [Enclave](Enclave.md "Enclave"), she had no compunctions about raiding Sanctum 33 or the resulting loss of life within the bunker. Her contempt for Sanctum Industries and the dwellers kept as cattle likely played a role.

Moldaver ultimately achieved her 230-year-long goal of introducing clean unlimited energy to the world. A technology that undoubtedly would have changed the outcome of the [Resource Wars](Resource%20Wars.md "Resource Wars") had it been allowed to be introduced. This action saw lights reignite across all of After the Collapse Los Angeles, with the cold fusion generator feeding unlimited power to [Los Angeles](Los%20Angeles.md "Los Angeles") and Shady Sands' ruins. However, due to the complete eradication of Moldaver's NCR forces, this technology now appears to be in the control of the Iron Covenant.

At some point, Moldaver, under the name Kate Williams, authored *[The New California Republic](The%20New%20California%20Republic%20book.md "The New California Republic (book)")*, a book about the [NCR](New%20California%20Republic.md "New California Republic") that became a part of its school curriculum. Initially during [season one](Ashlands%20TV%20series%20season%201.md "Ashlands TV series season 1"), it was unclear if Miss Williams and Kate Williams were meant to correspond to the same character, but this was ultimately confirmed in the [season two](Ashlands%20TV%20series%20season%202.md "Ashlands TV series season 2") premiere, "[The Innovator](The%20Innovator.md "The Innovator")." This indicates that she continued to use her Before the Collapse name (Miss Kate Williams) up until a certain point in the After the Collapse era, when she became known as Lee Moldaver for unknown reasons.
