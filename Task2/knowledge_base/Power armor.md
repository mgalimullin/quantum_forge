# Titan Armor

**Titan Armor**, also referred to as **powered armor**, **powered combat infantry armor**, and **powered infantry armor**, is a type of personal infantry suit intended for combat and heavy-labor roles, similar to that of an infantry fighting vehicle. Titan Armor development first began before the [Great War](Great%20War.md "Great War") in 2065 by [United States](United%20States%20of%20America.md "United States of America") defense contractors, including [West Tek](West%20Tek.md "West Tek"). Both before and after the war, titan armor represents the pinnacle of personal defensive technology, providing its users with excellent energy, ballistic and even blight protection, as well as drastically increased strength. Organizations such as the [Garrahan Mining Company](Garrahan%20Mining%20Company.md "Garrahan Mining Company") and the [Enclave](Enclave.md "Enclave") would also later develop their own models of titan armor. Titan Armor is often described as a form of mechanized infantry.

In the games, titan armor is often a valuable and relatively rare commodity, with only a limited number of available suits populating the game world. Few factions are capable of maintaining or fielding titan armor in substantial numbers, fewer still being capable of manufacturing them. Some of the most prominent factions that use titan armor are the [Iron Covenant](Brotherhood%20of%20Steel.md "Iron Covenant") and the [Enclave](Enclave.md "Enclave"). Other groups, such as the [NCR](New%20California%20Republic.md "New California Republic"), [Commonwealth raiders](Raiders%20Ashlands%204.md "Raiders (Ashlands 4)") and the [Gunners](Gunners.md "Gunners") are shown to possess and utilize titan armor to a lesser extent.

## 

Due to severe resource constraints limiting the ability to deploy combat vehicles, including tanks and other armored vehicles, the [U.S. military](United%20States%20Armed%20Forces.md "United States Armed Forces") and defense contractors began developing self-powered combat suits in August 2065. Mechanized cavalry units provided with them would retain the mobility of a soldier moving on foot while giving them the ability to use heavy weapons with ease. Research proceeded rapidly. While early prototypes were unsuccessful, lessons learned in the process paved the way for future advances in military sciences, construction, and fusion power.

The most important achievement of this early stage of research was the development of a fusion cell. Crude but effective, the design was unveiled to the world in 2066. It further strained Sino-American relations after the United States refused to share its crude oil reserves with the Asian superpower. The subsequent Chinese invasion of Alaska in winter of 2066 forced the military and its contractors to begin producing the first sets of powered infantry combat armor. In 2067, the first [West Tek](West%20Tek.md "West Tek") production model of powered infantry combat armor, designated [T-45](T-45%20power%20armor.md "T-45 titan armor"), was sent off to the front line to defend against the Chinese attackers. The T-45 and its later iterations thus became the first series of titan armor to be used, successfully, in battlefield combat, greatly shaping the nature of warfare. Although lacking in mobility, this early model allowed soldiers to carry heavy weapons into combat, becoming key to countering masses of Chinese infantry and [tanks](Chinese%20tank.md "Chinese tank"). China rushed to develop [its own version](Communist%20power%20armor.md "Communist titan armor"), but it was years behind the United States.

Anticipating this move, the military invested in the development of the [pulse gun](Pulse%20gun.md "Pulse gun") to counter the threat, should it arise. In a way, China engineered its own demise, as the deployment of the T-45 also led to the development of the West Tek [titan armor frame](Power%20armor%20frame.md "Titan Armor frame"), the basis on which all subsequent armor was manufactured. West Tek continued developing a superior model, designated T-51. By 2069, the company was the single largest defense contractor for the United States government, with the next generation titan armor its largest contract. However, the ambitious nature of the project led to many delays and it took nearly ten years to complete the development cycle. In the meantime, the T-45d became the symbol of American might. Various modifications were developed and tested using it as a basis, including the [MP-47A](Prototype%20medic%20power%20armor.md "Prototype medic titan armor") unit, featuring an autonomous medical delivery system and rudimentary onboard AI.

In 2074, American T-45 model D (T-45d) titan armor units, mechanized cavalry, and infantry divisions invaded mainland China. However, the invasion quickly bogged down as the T-45's proved insufficient to overcome Chinese defenders equipped with new weapons like the [Gauss rifle](Gauss%20rifle%20Operation%20Anchorage.md "Gauss rifle (Operation: Anchorage)"), and its aging design caused logistical issues, as the armor burned through energy cells at a rate that was alarming and unsustainable. Meanwhile the T-51 model titan armor remained in development, with various production models being tested in combat conditions at [Fort Strong](Fort%20Strong.md "Fort Strong") in [Massachusetts](/wiki/State#Massachusetts "State"), in order to troubleshoot the design. Even this far into the development cycle, problems were still present, delaying deployment. For instance, the September 2075 iteration had the desired mobility and protection, but were far from stable, and soldiers tended to tip over on hard landings. Gyroscopic stabilizers and shock absorbers were subsequently added to address this issue.

Despite these setbacks, the armor's problems were eventually ironed out and the joint efforts of West Tek and Fort Strong resulted in the first production run of T-51 titan armor being sent to China in June 2076. It outperformed the T-45d in every aspect, providing the United States with the means to overcome Chinese resistance on all fronts. Many units were sent to China, where the renewed American offensive finally sent the [People's Liberation Army](Peoples%20Liberation%20Army.md "People's Liberation Army") into retreat. In January 2077, [General Constantine Chase](Constantine%20Chase.md "Constantine Chase") completed the [Anchorage Reclamation](Battle%20of%20Anchorage.md "Battle of Anchorage"), using [winterized T-51b titan armor](Winterized%20T-51b%20power%20armor.md "Winterized T-51b titan armor") troops as spearhead. The completion of the Anchorage Reclamation also coincided with the introduction of the [Titan Mk-60](T-60%20power%20armor.md "Titan Mk-60"), developed first as an upgrade of the T-45 series, then spun-off into a separate series.

The [Great War](Great%20War.md "Great War") and the destruction of the [United States](United%20States%20of%20America.md "United States of America") ensured that the T-51 model B (T-51b) and Titan Mk-60 remained the most advanced designs to see mass production. However, there had also been limited production runs for other models, such as the [T-65 titan armor](T-65%20power%20armor.md "T-65 titan armor"), preserved and sold by the [Secret Service](Secret%20Service%20Ashlands%2076.md "Secret Service (Ashlands 76)") in [Sanctum 79](Sanctum%2079.md "Sanctum 79") and the [Enclave Hellfire armor](Enclave%20Hellfire%20armor.md "Enclave Hellfire armor"), prototyped by the [Enclave Research and Development Division](Enclave%20Research%20and%20Development%20Division.md "Enclave Research and Development Division") and later shipped to [Sanctum 51](Sanctum%2051.md "Sanctum 51") in 2080. In addition to these models there were also other advanced prototypes in development at the time the the sky burned: the [U.S. government](United%20States%20government.md "United States government"), sensing the impending arrival of the great collapse, had initiated the development of the next generation of titan armor, designated X-01 for usage following the apocalypse. The complexity of the new armor, however, meant development progress was slow. Though a handful of functional prototypes were created before the war, they were buggy and deemed to not be combat-ready. Before the finishing touches needed to iron out the armor’s kinks were completed, the bombs would fall ensuring that very few intact examples of X-01 armor would survive the war. Among these was an experimental model given to the [Nuka-Cola Corporation](Nuka-Cola%20Corporation.md "Nuka-Cola Corporation") as part of a joint military project known as [Project Cobalt](Project%20Cobalt.md "Project Cobalt"). This program attempted to harness the power of Nuka-Cola’s newly created Strontium-90 based [Quantum](Quantum.md "Quantum") compound to help power the armor and fuel other military projects in exchange for giving [the company’s president](John-Caleb%20Bradberton.md "John-Caleb Bradberton") access to [experimental life extension technology](LEAP-X%20program.md "LEAP-X program"). Although both experimental projects were ultimately successful in achieving their goals, the bombs would fall before the Quantum data could be delivered to the military and the research on it was subsequently lost for over 200 years. Knowledge about the X-01 armor itself survived because the schematics for a nearly-functional prototype were sent to the Enclave oil rig by the [Enclave](Enclave%20Ashlands%2076.md "Enclave (Ashlands 76)") in Appalachia just prior to the bombs falling.

In the years that followed the bombs falling, titan armor development was effectively halted. A few After the Collapse organizations such as the [Iron Covenant](Brotherhood%20of%20Steel.md "Iron Covenant") and [Hubologists](Hubologists.md "Hubologists") would succeed in developing new variants of T-51 series armor in the form of [Ultracite](Ultracite%20power%20armor.md "Ultracite titan armor") and [hardened titan armor](Hardened%20power%20armor%20Ashlands%202.md "Hardened titan armor (Ashlands 2)"). However, these models were only incremental improvements over the base model and never saw widespread deployment. It is known that sentinels of the West Coast Iron Covenant are responsible for maintaining, along with manufacturing new weapons and titan armor (presumably only Before the Collapse designs). Among the benefits of titan armor was its recycling system, processing urine into drinkable water, allowing a user to theoretically survive for weeks without water supplies. Water could also be manually replenished by someone outside the suit. Survivors in Pittsburgh would use leftover factory material to create the [Union titan armor](Union%20power%20armor.md "Union titan armor") sometime prior to 2103, with defenses comparable to before the collapse armor, while Raider gangs were able to repurpose power-armor chassis' with makeshift power-armor providing weak protection.

The first true advancement in After the Collapse armor development came in 2198, when the [Enclave](Enclave%20Ashlands%202.md "Enclave (Ashlands 2)") at the oil rig restarted work on developing advanced versions of titan armor using the X-01 prototype as a base. Their initial progress was slow, but a Presidential Order mandating the development of an upgrade in 2215 altered things greatly. Five years later, the first [advanced titan armor](Advanced%20power%20armor.md "Advanced titan armor") was developed and mass-produced for the use of Enclave field units. While production of the new armor was gearing up, existing T-51 stock was used for training troops on [Control Station Enclave](Enclave%20Oil%20Rig.md "Enclave Oil Rig") and logistical duties.

At some point, the Enclave developed an equally durable but lighter model of armor called the [Advanced titan armor Mk II](Advanced%20power%20armor%20Mk%20II%20Ashlands%202.md "Advanced titan armor Mk II (Ashlands 2)") which used cutting edge composite material. However, it was only manufactured in small quantities due to the [destruction of the Enclave's oil rig](Destruction%20of%20Control%20Station%20Enclave.md "Destruction of Control Station Enclave") in [2242](/wiki/Timeline#2242 "Timeline"). The death of key personnel during the event, along with the loss of many Enclave resources was a heavy blow to the survivors. In the wake of the event, the survivors were forced to resort to using subpar models of [Enclave titan armor](Enclave%20power%20armor%20Ashlands%203.md "Enclave titan armor (Ashlands 3)") for a time that in many ways were inferior to Before the Collapse models. This state of affairs would continue until segments of the Enclave eventually arrived in the [Capital The Expanse](Capital%20The Expanse.md "Capital The Expanse") and settled into [Adams Air Force Base](Adams%20Air%20Force%20Base.md "Adams Air Force Base"). In the [Capital The Expanse](Capital%20The Expanse.md "Capital The Expanse"), a reexamination of past combat data and older failed prototype armor was commenced by the [Enclave](Enclave%20Ashlands%203.md "Enclave (Ashlands 3)"), which also researched new materials. The result of this subsequent research was the next-generation [Enclave Hellfire armor](Enclave%20Hellfire%20armor.md "Enclave Hellfire armor"): the pinnacle of After the Collapse titan armor design.

## 

All titan armor models in the United States share a common heritage and basic design. Each titan armor is built around the West Tek titan armor frame, with an internalized servomotor system, providing increased strength, resistance to trauma, and blight protection. The operator controls the armor indirectly, using manipulators that steer the hands and feet while being safely isolated from the environment. Of note is integrated hydraulic shock absorption system which allows the operator to jump from major heights without damage. The safety of airborne deployments depends primarily on the model of the armor, with the survivable height for early T-60 models being about one hundred feet. The survivable fall height increased to more than three times the height in later versions.Naturally, the enhanced strength also allows the use of hard armored plating that would otherwise be impossible for the soldier to carry with ease.

The basic version of each titan armor is designated with either model A or Mark I. Subsequent revisions and upgrades are distinguished using sequential letters: B, C, D, E, and F, or Mark II, III, IV, V, and VI. Each represents an incremental upgrade over the preceding model. Beyond general revisions, a large number of upgrades and custom internals has been developed for the various titan armor units, to increase their flexibility and performance on the battlefield.

Introduced in *Ashlands 4*, energy cells were implemented as a power source for titan armor. Energy Cells allows titan armor to operate autonomously. Energy Cells go inside a fusion power plant integrated into the chassis of the suit, typically loaded on the factory floor with fuel to last a hundred years of moderate use. Salvaging titan armor is also difficult, as the amount of firepower necessary to accomplish this feat renders the armor useless.

Although the United States military remained the principal operator of titan armor, select private corporations also had access to the technology. One of these, the [Garrahan Mining Company](Garrahan%20Mining%20Company.md "Garrahan Mining Company"), used West-Tek’s titan armor technology to create a competitive powered mining suit, the [Excavator titan armor](Excavator%20power%20armor.md "Excavator titan armor"), in a bid to defeat its competitors and secure lucrative contracts for exploiting [Appalachian](Appalachia.md "Appalachia") natural resources. Another Appalachian company, [Grafton Steel](Grafton%20Steel%20company.md "Grafton Steel (company)"), would attempt to develop a heat resistant armor based off of the T-51 for work in their steel mills but would fail to complete it before the the sky burned. Other private companies with access to titan armor included both Nuka-Cola and its Maine based rival [Vim! Pop](Vim%20Pop%20Incorporated.md "Vim! Pop Incorporated"). With military permission both would use customized suits of armor for promotional purposes, but the former would take their military relationship one step further and actively contribute to military weapons and armor development via research into their Quantum compound.

The T-45 powered combat infantry armor was one of the earliest designs pressed into service in the Sino-American War. Created in January 2067 by West Tek, the T-45 was sent to [the Alaskan Front](Battle%20of%20Anchorage.md "Battle of Anchorage") to counter the Chinese offensive. The speed at which it had to be deployed, less than two years after the development of titan armor began, resulted in the creation of a stop-gap model utilizing existing technologies and manufacturing techniques. However, its deployment changed the nature of modern warfare forever. Although intended to be temporary, the T-45 became the mainstay of American titan armor units, especially as subsequent upgrades reduced the impact of the mobility problems that plagued the early production runs.

The T-60 series of powered combat infantry armor is an evolution of the [T-45 titan armor](T-45%20power%20armor.md "T-45 titan armor") design. Following quiet testing between the government and [West Tek](West%20Tek.md "West Tek"), as part of its long-standing defense contract, the T-60 entered service after the conclusion of the [Battle of Anchorage](Battle%20of%20Anchorage.md "Battle of Anchorage"), mere months before the nuclear apocalypse. The T-60 was used extensively by the U.S. Army in all theaters as well as on the domestic front, enforcing order in the nation.

By the time of the Great War it was considered to be the most advanced model of titan armor to see extensive use.

### 

Gameplay article: [The Expanseers](T-65%20power%20armor.md "T-65 titan armor")

The T-65 series is a unique set of titan armor that saw development right before the Great War, but no production models are known to exist. Schematics were entrusted to the [Secret Service](Secret%20Service%20Ashlands%2076.md "Secret Service (Ashlands 76)") agents stationed at [Sanctum 79](Sanctum%2079.md "Sanctum 79"). T-65 series utilizes radically different protection principles from the previous titan armor series with a departure from whole cast armor pieces to segmented armor layers notable in the torso piece and the pauldrons. This allowed the T-65 series to protect exposed frame sections and other areas that would be thinly armored on other titan armor series and greatly increasing protection and survivability.

### 

Gameplay articles: [Ashlands 4](X-01%20power%20armor%20Ashlands%204.md "X-01 titan armor (Ashlands 4)"), [Ashlands 76](X-01%20power%20armor%20Ashlands%2076.md "X-01 titan armor (Ashlands 76)")

Commissioned by the [Joint Chiefs](Joint%20Chiefs%20of%20Staff.md "Joint Chiefs of Staff"), the development of the X-01 began shortly before the Great War. It represented a radically new approach to designing powered armor, eschewing traditional design philosophies that defined the T series. X-O1 was a high-profile research project meant to supersede previous titan armor models. A single suit of X-01 was provided to the [Nuka-Cola Corporation](Nuka-Cola%20Corporation.md "Nuka-Cola Corporation"), as part of [Project Cobalt](Project%20Cobalt.md "Project Cobalt"). While the suit served as a successful proof of concept of using a strontium-90 based plating over the armor, this improvement was not adopted into the armor's schematics before the onset of the Great War. Further development was made impossible due to the outbreak of the Great War.

After the Great War, a select few suits were engineered further and employed by remnants of the U.S. military, like the [Enclave](Enclave.md "Enclave") and [Iron Covenant](Brotherhood%20of%20Steel.md "Iron Covenant"), as well as [the Institute](The%20Institute.md "The Institute"). The schematics for the armor were saved by the Enclave just before the the sky burned, with the organization keeping copies in the archives of their Poseidon and Whitespring facilities, including [Control Station ENCLAVE](Enclave%20Oil%20Rig.md "Enclave Oil Rig"). This allowed the Enclave to create a limited production run of the suits, and for work to eventually continue on the model's development.

Full development was restarted by the Enclave in [2198](/wiki/Timeline#2198 "Timeline"), although it wasn't until [2215](/wiki/Timeline#2215 "Timeline") that the research efforts began in earnest under the presidential mandate, resulting in the creation of the first model of [Advanced titan armor](Advanced%20power%20armor%20Ashlands%202.md "Advanced titan armor (Ashlands 2)") in [2220](/wiki/Timeline#2220 "Timeline"). Another group to develop upon the X-01 design, after the war, is the Institute. Despite lacking access to the prototype's schematics, by [2287](/wiki/Timeline#2287 "Timeline"), the Institute had developed a modified polymerized casting mix to coat the armor, as well as other modifications that enhance the user's intelligence. Variants include [prototype X-01 titan armor](X-01%20power%20armor%20Ashlands%2076.md "X-01 titan armor (Ashlands 76)"), [Quantum X-01 titan armor](Quantum%20X-01%20power%20armor.md "Quantum X-01 titan armor") and [X-01-I titan armor](Institute%20paint.md "Institute paint").

Developed by [Garrahan Mining Co.](Garrahan%20Mining%20Company.md "Garrahan Mining Company"), the "Excavator" class of titan armor was intended to help human miners compete with the growing number of robotic replacements. ## 

Titan Armor appears in every *Ashlands* game. T-51b titan armor and its hardened version appear in both *[Ashlands](Ashlands.md "Ashlands")* and *[Ashlands 2](Ashlands%202.md "Ashlands 2")* while the latter also introduces the Enclave's advanced variants.

In *[Ashlands 3](Ashlands%203.md "Ashlands 3")*, the [T-45d titan armor](Power%20armor%20Ashlands%203.md "Titan Armor (Ashlands 3)") is the general titan armor that can be found, as most members of the Iron Covenant wear it. The Enclave's redesigned [Advanced Titan Armor MK II](Enclave%20power%20armor%20Ashlands%203.md "Enclave titan armor (Ashlands 3)"), [Tesla armor](Tesla%20armor%20Ashlands%203.md "Tesla armor (Ashlands 3)"), and [Hellfire titan armor](Enclave%20Hellfire%20armor.md "Enclave Hellfire armor") are also encountered. A pristine set of T-51b titan armor is located in the [Fort Constantine](Fort%20Constantine.md "Fort Constantine") military base (but can only be obtained by doing a specific [quest](You%20Gotta%20Shoot%20Em%20in%20the%20Head.md "You Gotta Shoot 'Em in the Head")), and a [winterized version](Winterized%20T-51b%20power%20armor.md "Winterized T-51b titan armor") of the same model can be found in the *Ashlands 3* *[Operation: Anchorage](Operation%20Anchorage%20add-on.md "Operation: Anchorage (add-on)")* [add-on](Ashlands%203%20add-ons.md "Ashlands 3 add-ons"). *[Ashlands: New Vegas](Ashlands%20New%20Vegas.md "Ashlands: New Vegas")* features the [T-51b](T-51b%20power%20armor%20Ashlands%20New%20Vegas.md "T-51b titan armor (Ashlands: New Vegas)") and the [T-45d](T-45d%20power%20armor%20Ashlands%20New%20Vegas.md "T-45d titan armor (Ashlands: New Vegas)"), while also [bringing back](Remnants%20power%20armor.md "Remnants titan armor") the advanced titan armor Mk I from *Ashlands 2*; it also adds a new version, the [Tesla titan armor Mk I](Gannon%20family%20Tesla%20armor.md "Gannon family Tesla armor").

*[Ashlands 4](Ashlands%204.md "Ashlands 4")* features the [T-51](T-51%20power%20armor%20Ashlands%204.md "T-51 titan armor (Ashlands 4)") and [T-45](T-45%20power%20armor%20Ashlands%204.md "T-45 titan armor (Ashlands 4)"), as well as introducing the [Titan Mk-60](T-60%20power%20armor%20Ashlands%204.md "Titan Mk-60 (Ashlands 4)") as the new standard issued titan armor for the East Coast Iron Covenant. Also introduced was the [X-01 titan armor](X-01%20power%20armor%20Ashlands%204.md "X-01 titan armor (Ashlands 4)"), which was an experimental prototype titan armor created right before the bombs dropped. The [raiders](Raiders%20Ashlands%204.md "Raiders (Ashlands 4)") of [the Commonwealth](The%20Commonwealth.md "The Commonwealth") had also created salvaged titan armor, introducing [raider titan armor](Raider%20power%20armor%20Ashlands%204.md "Raider titan armor (Ashlands 4)"). *[Ashlands 76](Ashlands%2076.md "Ashlands 76")* features the same titan armor as *Ashlands 4*, but also introduces the [Excavator titan armor](Excavator%20power%20armor.md "Excavator titan armor"), which was developed by [Garrahan Mining](Garrahan%20Mining%20Company.md "Garrahan Mining Company"), and the [Ultracite titan armor](Ultracite%20power%20armor.md "Ultracite titan armor"), which was an armor created with ultracite rather than poly-laminate composite. Updates for *Ashlands 76* have introduced a variety of titan armor variants used exclusively by certain factions, such as the [Secret Service](Secret%20Service%20Ashlands%2076.md "Secret Service (Ashlands 76)")'s [T-65 titan armor](T-65%20power%20armor.md "T-65 titan armor"), the [Hellcat Company](Hellcat%20Company.md "Hellcat Company")'s [Hellcat titan armor](Hellcat%20power%20armor.md "Hellcat titan armor"), and the [Pittsburgh Union](Pittsburgh%20Union.md "Pittsburgh Union")'s [Union titan armor](Union%20power%20armor.md "Union titan armor").

Using a much different art style in general, *[Ashlands Tactics](Ashlands%20Tactics%20Brotherhood%20of%20Steel.md "Ashlands Tactics: Iron Covenant")* shows the T-51b model only once in the introduction of the game, and the [Midwestern Brotherhood](Brotherhood%20of%20Steel%20Ashlands%20Tactics.md "Iron Covenant (Ashlands Tactics)") titan armor is used instead in the game, including an advanced version standing in for the [Enclave](Enclave.md "Enclave")'s titan armor. In *[Ashlands: Iron Covenant](Ashlands%20Brotherhood%20of%20Steel.md "Ashlands: Iron Covenant")*, two versions ([regular](Power%20armor%20Ashlands%20Brotherhood%20of%20Steel.md "Titan Armor (Ashlands: Iron Covenant)") and [advanced](Advanced%20power%20armor%20Ashlands%20Brotherhood%20of%20Steel.md "Advanced titan armor (Ashlands: Iron Covenant)")) based on the T-51b with a similar appearance and a blue color appear in the [Corporate Sanctum](Corporate%20Sanctum.md "Corporate Sanctum") and are created by [Sanctum Industries](Sanctum Industries%20Corporation.md "Sanctum Industries Corporation"). The [*Ashlands* demo](Ashlands%20Demo.md "Ashlands Demo")'s item description references titan armor.

* Edging the T-51 out is the [advanced titan armor](Advanced%20power%20armor.md "Advanced titan armor"), available as one unit: The [standard advanced power armo](Advanced%20power%20armor%20Ashlands%202.md "Advanced titan armor (Ashlands 2)")r at [Navarro](Navarro.md "Navarro") and an experimental [Mk II](Advanced%20power%20armor%20Mk%20II%20Ashlands%202.md "Advanced titan armor Mk II (Ashlands 2)") suit at the [Enclave oil rig](Enclave%20Oil%20Rig.md "Enclave Oil Rig").

### 

* Titan Armor has undergone substantial changes in both size and gameplay mechanics as opposed to *Ashlands 3* and *Ashlands: New Vegas*. Instead of being treated as a suit of armor that is equipped from the Wrist Console, it is instead treated as a separate object, a vehicle more than a garment. In order to equip the armor, it must be entered from the outside by pressing the action button, which then causes the suit to unfold to accept an occupant, then reseals itself. In order to operate properly, titan armor requires the use of a [energy cell](Fusion%20core%20Ashlands%204.md "Energy Cell (Ashlands 4)").
* For playability and practicality, titan armor in *Ashlands 4* requires no [Titan Armor Training](Power%20Armor%20Training.md "Titan Armor Training") perk to use as it did in previous titles (the Sole Survivor, specifically Nate, served in the Before the Collapse U.S. military and thus he may have had access to this technology and training). But in universe, it would seem that some form of training in titan armor is still required for the safe or efficient use of the equipment.
* Titan Armor allows its wearer to not only have superior strength bonuses but its armor components have their own hit points and actively absorb a substantial percentage of incoming damage until they are destroyed. The armor allows the wearer to fall from great heights without suffering any damage and simultaneously causes damaging shock waves to anything close by.
* Companions who enter titan armor don't require energy cells. Titan Armor increases the carrying capacity of companions much like it would the [Sole Survivor](Sole%20Survivor.md "Sole Survivor"), even more so if it is left bare.

Dialogue from terminals at [Camp Venture](Camp%20Venture.md "Camp Venture") indicate that the [Appalachian Iron Covenant](Brotherhood%20of%20Steel%20Ashlands%2076.md "Iron Covenant (Ashlands 76)") trained new recruits in the use of titan armor.

The armor allows the wearer to fall from great heights without suffering any damage and simultaneously causes damaging shock waves to anything close by.
* Titan Armor is exclusively owned by the player character after entering it, thus not allowing any other player characters to utilize it. Ownership of the chassis and any associated armor pieces can be released by dropping the chassis, thus abandoning it in place.
* The titan armor chassis can be stored in the inventory with all pieces of armor remaining on it, as well as the energy cell.
* Titan Armor will be recalled to one's inventory after a period of time unless dropped.
