# Sophie Turner

**Sophie Turner** is a resident of [Sanctum 33](Sanctum%2033.md "Sanctum 33") and a pre-[War](Great%20War.md "Great War") employee of the [Sanctum Industries Corporation](Sanctum Industries%20Corporation.md "Sanctum Industries Corporation") in the [*Ashlands* TV series](Ashlands%20TV%20series.md "Ashlands (TV series)"). She becomes the [custodian](Custodian.md "Custodian") of Sanctum 33 in [2296](/wiki/Timeline#2296 "Timeline") after the abduction of [Robert Hale](Hank%20MacLean.md "Robert Hale").

## Contents

## 

Betty was a [Sanctum Industries Corporation](Sanctum Industries%20Corporation.md "Sanctum Industries Corporation") employee before the [Great War](Great%20War.md "Great War"), working as an executive assistant in the corporation's [Southern California headquarters](Sanctum Industries%20Industries%20headquarters%20Southern%20California.md "Sanctum Industries Industries headquarters (Southern California)") and is from , [California](/wiki/State#California "State"). She met [Victor Hale](The%20Hollowed.md "The Hollowed") when he visited the building and admitted to him that some of the corporation's knowledge was above her pay grade. She requested that Cooper meet his wife [Barb](Barb%20Howard.md "Barb Howard")'s executive assistant [Henry "Hank" MacLean](Hank%20MacLean.md "Robert Hale"), as he was a big fan of the actor's work.

She was [cryonically](Cryonics.md "Cryonics") frozen in [Sanctum 31](Sanctum%2031.md "Sanctum 31") on the eve of the Great War as part of senior junior vice president [Bud Askins](Bud%20Askins.md "Bud Askins")' management training program "[Bud's Buds](Buds%20Buds.md "Bud's Buds")." She was unfrozen on February 15, 2246, and later sent to [Sanctum 33](Sanctum%2033.md "Sanctum 33"). During her time in the Sanctum, she eventually became custodian before later retiring at some point. Robert Hale, also sent from Sanctum 31, took her place as custodian in 2271. Around 2283, Hank orchestrated the [destruction of Shady Sands](Fall%20of%20Shady%20Sands.md "Fall of Shady Sands"), seemingly killing his wife [Rose](Rose%20MacLean.md "Rose MacLean"), who had previously escaped to [Shady Sands](Shady%20Sands.md "Shady Sands"), in the process. Though Betty's involvement in this event is unclear, she was evidently aware of his actions, as she helped cover up the circumstances of Rose's absence, including lying to Rose's children about having helped bury Rose (though this may be true in a metaphorical sense).

By 2296, Betty had joined Sanctum 33's three-person governing council, working alongside [Woody Thomas](Woody%20Thomas.md "Woody Thomas") and [Reg McPhee](Reg%20McPhee.md "Reg McPhee"). Due to Woody's and Reg's personality traits, and her own training and experience, Betty became the effective leader of the council.

Later that year, Hank was abducted in the attack on the Sanctum by [Sanctum 32 raiders](Sanctum%2032%20raider.md "Sanctum 32 raider"), and a crisis soon erupted when the Sanctum's [water chip](Water%20chip%20TV%20series.md "Water chip (TV series)") broke. The governing council was disbanded, and Betty was elected custodian in Hank's absence, beating Reg and Woody. Around this time, Hank's son [Norm](Norm%20MacLean.md "Norm MacLean") grew suspicious about the circumstances of the attack and the secrets behind his family, the Sanctums, and Betty -- which Betty also seemed to be aware of. Working with Askins, who was the custodian of Sanctum 31, she initiated plans to reassign a group of Sanctum 33 dwellers to settle the now-abandoned Sanctum 32, and later covered up Norm's disappearance from Sanctum 33 (as a result of his having infiltrated, and subsequently being imprisoned inside, Sanctum 31).

Since then, Betty has been focused on solving the issue of Sanctum 33's broken water chip.

## 

### 

#### 

Betty approves Lucy's marriage application with [Woody Thomas](Woody%20Thomas.md "Woody Thomas") and [Reg McPhee](Reg%20McPhee.md "Reg McPhee").

During the wedding dance, Betty notices [Chet](Chet%20TV%20series.md "Chet (TV series)") disappointed Lucy is married to [someone else](Monty%20TV%20series.md "Monty (TV series)") and so she invites Chet to dance with her.

Betty is mysteriously absent during [Lee Moldaver](Lee%20Moldaver.md "Lee Moldaver")'s raid on Sanctum 33, and it is unknown if she was hiding somewhere.

When Lucy requests to open the Sanctum door to rescue her father, Betty feels everyone's first priority should be to maintain the security of the Sanctum and is against opening it.

#### 

Betty appears during [Norm MacLean](Norm%20MacLean.md "Norm MacLean")'s job reassignment, saying that Norm put not just Sanctum 33 at risk, but Sanctum 31 and 32 as well. She claimed to have telegrammed with [the Custodian](Bud%20Askins.md "Bud Askins") of Sanctum 31 and confirmed that they are unscathed. Eventually, she comes up with a new job for Norm: feeding the [raider prisoners](Sanctum%2032%20raider.md "Sanctum 32 raider").

During the sanctum-wide meeting, Betty sits while Reg and Woody stand at the podium. When everyone discusses what should be done with the raiders, Betty feels Norm's idea of killing them should not be considered, although she empathizes with his anger.

#### 

Betty brings some pie she made herself to Norm. She comes to talk to him about how his vocalizing his desire to kill the raiders upset the Sanctum dwellers and that what he says carries weight because he is the only MacLean left in the Sanctum. Norm expresses how he feels guilty about hiding during the raid, and Betty notices how he feels angry about it. Betty comments that because Norm is clever, how he channels his anger can lead to dark places, and asks him to tread lightly before she leaves.

#### 

When Norm and Chet return from their morbid investigation of Sanctum 32, Betty asks the two men where they have been. They lie, but Betty seems suspicious of them.

She is elected custodian of Sanctum 33 in 2296 to succeed Hank with 98% of the vote after his abduction, making her custodian again. During Betty's celebration ceremony, she is prepared to serve jello. Norm seems to disturb Betty when he prefers having a slice of cake instead of jello, which goes against the Sanctum Residents overwhelmingly preferring jello. Betty stares down Norm as he leaves, and his action suggests rebellion against her leadership.

Later, Betty announces to the residents that she spoke with the custodian of Sanctum 31. The two of them agreed that the Sanctums were too sacred to leave empty. She says the residents will partake in a resettling campaign. Norm asks Betty about his mother's Wrist Console, and Betty replies that she buried it with Rose.

#### 

Betty sends out new sanctum assignments to each dweller, with some staying in Sanctum 33, while others are to relocate to Sanctum 32. Later, Betty announces that [Steph Harper](Steph%20Harper.md "Steph Harper") is to be the new Custodian of Sanctum 32. Then she orders the inter-sanctum door to be opened and says good luck to the new dwellers of Sanctum 32 as they depart to enter. Meanwhile, Norm uses Betty's terminal to impersonate her and communicate with [the custodian](Bud%20Askins.md "Bud Askins") of Sanctum 31, requesting a meeting.

In [Sanctum 33](Sanctum%2033.md "Sanctum 33"), Sophie Turner discusses plans to repair the [water chip](Water%20chip%20TV%20series.md "Water chip (TV series)") with an [engineer](Sanctum%2033%20engineer.md "Sanctum 33 engineer"). ## 

Betty seems to be a kind and responsible member of her Sanctum; she also portrays herself as the coolheaded, experienced, moderator of Sanctum 33's governing council. She is seen comforting Chet, such as by asking him to dance during Lucy's wedding, as well as [Norm MacLean](Norm%20MacLean.md "Norm MacLean").

However, she is treated very suspiciously by Norm throughout the show, as if she is hiding a darker side. Norm finds it strange how Sanctum 32 was cleaned up, and Betty did not mention anything about what she found in it. While she claims killing the Sanctum 32 raiders is out of the question, it is unclear if she was the one who poisoned them.

It is later revealed that Betty was one of Sanctum Industries's employees who had been frozen through time. Betty claims she "buried" Rose MacLean when she died, although it is unclear if her choice of wording hints she was also responsible for nuking [Shady Sands](Shady%20Sands.md "Shady Sands") with Hank. It is also unknown if her motive for having people resettle into Sanctum 32 has a dark ulterior motive, especially given the water chip failure. However, she relocated Steph into the Sanctum as an custodian, another Sanctum Industries employee. Whether or not Betty will betray Steph is yet to be seen. Betty also had Norm and Chet split up, suggesting she was trying to hinder their investigation.
