# Reg McPhee

Doctor **Reg McPhee** is a resident of [Sanctum 33](Sanctum%2033.md "Sanctum 33") in the [*Ashlands* TV series](Ashlands%20TV%20series.md "Ashlands (TV series)").

## Contents

## 

Reg is a native resident of Sanctum 33. Along with three others in the Sanctum, he is to some extent a product of inbreeding, though he claims to have personally "come out on the other side" in terms of self-doubts related to it. He holds a PhD in event planning from the Sanctum's education system and, by 2296, had become a member of the Sanctum's three-person governing council alongside [Sophie Turner](Betty%20Pearson.md "Sophie Turner") and [Woody Thomas](Woody%20Thomas.md "Woody Thomas"), under custodian [Robert Hale](Hank%20MacLean.md "Robert Hale").

However, following the attack on the Sanctum by [Sanctum 32 raiders](Sanctum%2032%20raider.md "Sanctum 32 raider") that year, Hank was abducted, and a crisis erupted when the Sanctum's [water chip](Water%20chip%20TV%20series.md "Water chip (TV series)") broke. The governing council was disbanded, and Betty was elected custodian in Hank's absence, beating Reg and Woody. Though he remained in Sanctum 33 while others including Woody were sent to resettle Sanctum 32, he has since been left adrift, seeing himself as the only one left behind while everyone is "moving up in the world." He was subsequently convinced by Betty to start a club, which became a support group for products of inbreeding.

## 

### 

#### 

Reg, alongside his Sanctum 33 council associates Betty and Woody, listens to [Lucy](Lucy%20MacLean.md "Lena Hart") give her speech request to be allowed to participate in the Triennial Trade with [Sanctum 32](Sanctum%2032.md "Sanctum 32"). The council unanimously agrees to approve Lucy's marriage application.

Sometime later, Reg is seen greeting the [Sanctum 32 dwellers](Sanctum%2032%20raider.md "Sanctum 32 raider") and their new Custodian, [Lee Moldaver](Lee%20Moldaver.md "Lee Moldaver"), who introduces Lucy's new husband, [Monty](Monty%20TV%20series.md "Monty (TV series)"), to her.

After the wedding ceremony is held, he and Woody are seen setting up the podium for Custodian Hank, who gives a poignant speech about his daughter's marriage. The two then stand and watch as the [Sanctum 33 dwellers](Sanctum%2033%20dweller.md "Sanctum 33 dweller") dance with their Sanctum 32 counterparts.

Soon after the wedding, the Sanctum 32 dwellers are revealed to be [raiders from the outer zone](Sanctum%2032%20raider.md "Sanctum 32 raider"). Reg is not seen during the attack, but he is injured during the attack and is forced to wear a neckbrace in the aftermath.

With Hank having been kidnapped by the raiders, Reg, Woody, and Betty hold an emergency assembly with the surviving Sanctum 33 dwellers in their authority as the governing council. During the assembly, Lucy proposes sending a search team to the outer zone to save her father. However, none of the council members agree; Reg asks Lucy if she intends to open the outer Sanctum door in seeming dismay, and Betty refuses on the grounds of preserving the Sanctum's security, to which both Reg and Woody agree. Lucy leaves, disconcerted, while Reg and the others continue the assembly. Lucy's brother [Norm](Norm%20MacLean.md "Norm MacLean") tells her that Reg and the other council members may have refused out of a desire to preserve their newfound authority.

Lucy soon forms a plan to look for her father on her own, with help from [Chet](Chet%20TV%20series.md "Chet (TV series)") and Norm. When they open the exterior Sanctum door, and Lucy is about to exit, Reg and [Davey](Davey%20TV%20series.md "Davey (TV series)") catch up with them. Reg tries to reason with Lucy to stop and not pass through the Sanctum door, but she does so regardless.

#### 

In the wake of Lucy's departure from Sanctum 33, Reg and the other two councilmembers hold disciplinary sessions to punish Chet and Norm, firing Chet from his role as gatekeeper. During Norm's session, Reg and Woody alternate, discussing Norm's involvement and the dangers of opening the Sanctum door. Reg also mentioned how the raiders could have only managed to enter Sanctum 32 because a "bonehead" allowed it. However, Betty seems to get the final say in Norm's punishment, and in response to a comment he makes, she reassigns him to deliver food to the surviving sixteen raiders, who have been imprisoned in the Sanctum.

Afterward, an assembly is held in the Sanctum to brainstorm what to do with the surviving raiders. After Woody announces their ethical dilemma, Reg chimes in, claiming that they have been given a prime opportunity to prove to the outer zone world the Sanctum's goal of repopulating and building a better world on the outer zone.

Woody then notes that a collective decision has to be made about the Sanctum 32 raiders, with Reg commenting that the decision should fit the Sanctum's shared values. Betty suggests that they promote possible ideas; thanks to a "conversation" that Woody had with one of the raiders, Reg suggests that the Sanctum 33 dwellers rehabilitate the Sanctum 32 raiders to integrate them into their society. On the other hand, Woody notes that it's understood that the process may take years, but claims it is something they can accomplish if they put their minds to it. A few ideas are then proposed by some Sanctum 33 dwellers, including Davey and [Marianne](Marianne%20TV%20series.md "Marianne (TV series)").

Hearing the suggestions, Norm scoffs at the ideas, and when Reg asks him if he has something to say, he opines that it's not their obligation to help the raiders. This opinion is met with backlash, and Reg tries to reason with Norm, stating that they can't simply "let them go" because they have intimate knowledge of their Sanctum security. He then asks Norm what he suggests they do, and Norm answers that they should kill the raiders as retribution, although he takes back his comment following further disagreement.

At that moment, the [Sanctum 33 engineer](Sanctum%2033%20engineer.md "Sanctum 33 engineer") appears and tries to privately talk with Reg and Woody. However, the two insist that they talk in public. The engineer insists that Custodian Hank would have done so privately, but Reg argues that it will be fine. As such, the engineer announces that Sanctum 33's [water chip](Water%20chip%20TV%20series.md "Water chip (TV series)") has been destroyed, leaving only two months of water reserves left for the Sanctum 33 residents to survive on. Before the engineer can finish, Reg cuts him off, and the meeting is adjourned.

#### 

With Hank now gone, an election is set to be held for the Sanctum's new custodian. Woody, Reg, and Betty are all running in the election, and Woody and Reg are seen hanging up posters for their electoral campaign. Later, they meet outside a cell where a semi-sedated Sanctum 32 raider is being held for interrogation about his background and the kidnapping of Hank.

As they continue talking, the semi-sedated Sanctum 32 raider regains consciousness and acts wildly and uncooperatively, thrashing about and not providing any answers. Woody decides to give up and try again the next day, moving to grab a [tranq gun](Tranq%20gun%20TV%20series.md "Tranq gun (TV series)"). Meanwhile, Reg watches the Sanctum 32 raider struggle to undo his restraints.

#### 

On the day of the election, Reg gets in line to vote and encounters Davey, who apologizes, first alluding to but then directly admitting that he will vote for Betty instead of Reg given the current water crisis and Betty's stated past experience as custodian. Reg expresses understands and doesn't hold it against Davey. Admitting to himself that he is unlikely to win, he also votes for Betty.

Betty indeed wins the election in a landslide, with 98% of the vote. During the ceremony for Betty's victory, Woody and Reg are in the back, also clapping for Betty. Woody comments "better luck next time" to Reg, and he softly agrees.

Soon after, Betty announces that she and the [Sanctum 31 custodian](Bud%20Askins.md "Bud Askins") have formulated a plan to resettle Sanctum 32, which will involve transferring many of Sanctum 33's dwellers into the new Sanctum. Reg tours Sanctum 32 along with many other Sanctum 33 dwellers, unaware that its newly-cleaned facade hides the violence and death that had occurred in the Sanctum.

#### 

The reassignment decisions for the Sanctum 33 dwellers are released by Betty. Reg is sitting on a toilet when he receives the decision, and is relieved to learn he will continue staying in Sanctum 33. However, Woody is set to be transferred. Before Woody leaves for Sanctum 32, Reg hugs him goodbye.

In [Sanctum 33](Sanctum%2033.md "Sanctum 33"), [Sophie Turner](Betty%20Pearson.md "Sophie Turner") discusses plans to repair the [water chip](Water%20chip%20TV%20series.md "Water chip (TV series)") with an [engineer](Sanctum%2033%20engineer.md "Sanctum 33 engineer"). Reg, licking a lollipop, comes in and asks if he can help, but he instead breaks a piece of technology. Betty hints that he should not interfere with important matters, suggesting, for example, that he create a support group. Reg takes her suggestion too seriously and organizes a support group for victims of inbreeding, which he himself is.

Reg soon hosts the group's first meeting, attended by only three others: Marianne and a [red-haired man](Red-haired%20man.md "Red-haired man") and [woman](Red-haired%20woman.md "Red-haired woman"). As Reg leads the group in a chant about not needing to feel ashamed about their lineage, the red-haired duo smile at each other suggestively, which arouses Marianne's suspicion; Reg himself seems totally unaware. The red-haired duo then ask Reg if they plan to change the Sanctum's rules to allow inbreeding; Reg says no, clarifying that the "rules are good" and that he is only trying to help people accept themselves for being inbred. This causes the red-haired duo to leave.

Marianne then asks Reg if her dust allergy could be due to her father's inbreeding; Reg professes not to know, as he never studied genetics. This causes Marianne to grab her plate of snacks and prepare to leave, but Reg hurriedly claims that it *could* be her father's fault to stop her from leaving. This causes Marianne to, after thanking Reg for the snacks that he prepared for the group, start talking in depth about her convoluted inbred family lineage. Reg sits and listens, seemingly satisfied.

## 

Reg portrays himself as idealistic and moralistic, perhaps to a fault. This is illustrated when Reg proposes that Sanctum 33 can reform the raiders into well-mannered people and assimilate them into Sanctum 33's society, even though the raiders have recently attacked Sanctum 33's people and have been disrespectful ever since. However, Reg firmly believes that the raiders want to adopt Sanctum 33's civilized values and that the sanctum-dwellers are responsible for reforming them.

Reg is also very arrogant in his leadership capabilities, even though it backfires on him. When a maintenance man advises him to keep an important issue private, Reg insists on discussing the matter publicly in front of an assembly. When the maintenance man complies and reveals their water chip is broken. Thus, the community will face water scarcity. Reg, in a panic, tries to cover up his mistake by interrupting the maintenance man. Although Reg vies with his peer, Woody, for relevance in the governing council, and even competes with his other peer, [Sophie Turner](Betty%20Pearson.md "Sophie Turner"), for the position of Custodian. However, he knows that Betty's reputation as an experienced and more competent leader will overshadow him; ultimately, even he votes for her.

Reg desperately clings to the status and prestige he has cultivated in Sanctum 33 and is relieved that he wasn't one of the residents reassigned to Sanctum 32.
