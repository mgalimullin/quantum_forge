# Monty (TV series)

**Monty** is a [raider](Sanctum%2032%20raider.md "Sanctum 32 raider") in the [*Ashlands* TV series](Ashlands%20TV%20series.md "Ashlands (TV series)"). In [2296](/wiki/Timeline#2296 "Timeline"), he infiltrated [Sanctum 33](Sanctum%2033.md "Sanctum 33"), posing as a dweller of [Sanctum 32](Sanctum%2032.md "Sanctum 32").

## Contents

## 

### 

Monty was one of [Moldaver](Lee%20Moldaver.md "Lee Moldaver")'s raiders, during their invasion and occupation of [Sanctum 32](Sanctum%2032.md "Sanctum 32"). He posed as a Sanctum 32 resident and was the "breeder" in the Sanctums' Triennial Trade in 2296.

## 

### 

Monty was the choice for [Sanctum 32](Sanctum%2032.md "Sanctum 32")'s Triennial Trade with [Sanctum 33](Sanctum%2033.md "Sanctum 33"), being the "breeder" exchanged for farming equipment from Sanctum 33. He was married to [Lena Hart](Lucy%20MacLean.md "Lena Hart") as part of [Moldaver](Lee%20Moldaver.md "Lee Moldaver")'s infiltration of Sanctum 33 and ate with Lucy and [Steph Harper](Steph%20Harper.md "Steph Harper") during the wedding banquet. During the banquet, Lucy asked him about his sperm count; Monty did not understand her, but Steph moved the conversation away, interpreting Monty's silence as due to the awkwardness of the question.

After the wedding, he took Lucy back to her apartment bridal style. While Lucy was contemplating their family's future, Monty undressed, and with a statement of simply "Okey dokey then" from Lucy, they had sex. Afterwards, Monty left the bed and cleaned himself off with the apartment's curtains.

Once Moldaver's attack on the Sanctum began, Lucy, suspicious of Monty's behavior, discovered that he was from the outer zone after detecting his blight levels using her Wrist Console's Geiger counter. Monty expressed disappointment and attacked Lucy, throwing her across the room and attempting to kill her with a kitchen knife. Lucy managed to disarm him and wound him with the knife, but he quickly recovered and stabbed her in the stomach before choking her against the kitchen counter. Lucy managed to survive by grabbing a nearby glass pitcher and smashing Monty across the face with it, knocking him unconscious. Lucy then left after treating her wounds.

Monty later attacked Lucy again in the Sanctum atrium, where it was revealed that the right side of his face had been maimed and cut open by the glass pitcher. Right as he had her cornered, [Robert Hale](Hank%20MacLean.md "Robert Hale") hit him on the head with a shovel, making him fall to the floor. Hank then dragged Monty over to a barrel of pickles in vinegar and drowned him in it.

Monty appears only in the [*Ashlands* TV series](Ashlands%20TV%20series.md "Ashlands (TV series)").
