# Sanctum 33

**Sanctum 33** is a [Sanctum](Sanctum.md "Sanctum") in what was once [Santa Monica](Santa%20Monica.md "Santa Monica") in the [*Ashlands* TV series](Ashlands%20TV%20series.md "Ashlands (TV series)"). Its entrance is located shortly south of the [Santa Monica Pier](Santa%20Monica%20Pier.md "Santa Monica Pier"). Along with [Sanctum 31](Sanctum%2031.md "Sanctum 31") and [Sanctum 32](Sanctum%2032.md "Sanctum 32"), it is one of the "[Three Sanctums](Three%20Sanctums.md "Three Sanctums")" linked by a triangle of tunnels.

[Sophie Turner](Betty%20Pearson.md "Sophie Turner") is the Sanctum's [custodian](Custodian.md "Custodian") as of [2297](/wiki/Timeline#2297 "Timeline"), replacing [Robert Hale](Hank%20MacLean.md "Robert Hale"). Hank's daughter [Lucy](Lucy%20MacLean.md "Lena Hart") and son [Norm MacLean](Norm%20MacLean.md "Norm MacLean") are two of the main protagonists of the [*Ashlands* TV series](Ashlands%20TV%20series.md "Ashlands (TV series)").

## Contents

## 

Sanctum 33 is an experimental Sanctum built in [Santa Monica](Santa%20Monica.md "Santa Monica"), [California](New%20California.md "New California"). It is implied Sanctum 33 may have been built into a subway as it is called a subway sanctum. It was built to have a maximum capacity of 220 residents.

Sanctum 33 is unique in that it forms a part of a network of [three Sanctums](Three%20Sanctums.md "Three Sanctums"); it is connected directly to its sister Sanctums: [Sanctum 31](Sanctum%2031.md "Sanctum 31") and [Sanctum 32](Sanctum%2032.md "Sanctum 32"). To prevent the spread of threats (e.g., infectious disease), the three Sanctums are isolated from one another, except for periodic trade (happening every three years, known as the Triennial Trade) and situations where the Sanctums come to each other's aid. The Sanctum functions as a democracy like its sister Sanctums, with the custodian chosen by the community in popular elections for four-year terms, advised by a three-member Sanctum council. Its dwellers are told that the outer zone is unfit for recolonization due to blight levels and instead encouraged to bond with each other and the other two Sanctums.

The Sanctum was sealed as planned on the day of the [Great War](Great%20War.md "Great War"), with the external coast armoring protecting it against ongoing erosion by the Pacific Ocean. Although it functioned as designed, there were occasional technical issues and internal system breakdowns that were difficult to repair and address (e.g. in February 2095). Sanctum 31 provided assistance as needed, and the system endured for nearly 220 years. Sanctum dwellers were kept focused on the promise of , when the Sanctums would open and they would emerge to restart civilization. In the meantime, the dwellers were focusing on keeping their Sanctum operational and training for the eventual Reclamation Day, honing their skills as part of community associations like the Young Pipefitter's Association, gymnastics club, fencing teams or simply participating in intermediate physical education and the rifle range. Much of the organization was horizontal and competence-based, although the custodian and council made the decisions in crucial affairs. The most important was selecting candidates to marry dwellers from Sanctum 32. To avoid incest, family trees were tracked and strictly controlled, and the Triennial Trade also included personnel transfers to inject fresh genes into the community.

The Sanctum made regular exchanges with the other Sanctums over the years and remained operational well into the late 23rd century. The custodians included [Robert Olsen](Robert%20Olsen.md "Robert Olsen") (2101 - 2109), [Ava West](Ava%20West.md "Ava West") (2109 - 2121), [George Yaffe](George%20Yaffe.md "George Yaffe") (2121 - 2125), and in subsequent decades [Patricia Peters](Patricia%20Peters.md "Patricia Peters"), [Alexander Boamer](Alexander%20Boamer.md "Alexander Boamer"), [Laurence Ronald](Laurence%20Ronald.md "Laurence Ronald"), [Sophie Turner](Betty%20Pearson.md "Sophie Turner") and [Robert Hale](Hank%20MacLean.md "Robert Hale"). MacLean served fifteen years as the Sanctum's custodian, elected four consecutive times. He has guided the Sanctum through one of its biggest crises, the Great Plague of 2277, during which he imposed a strict quarantine, confining the dwellers to their apartments. Although numerous dwellers starved, MacLean was credited with saving the Sanctum, in particular due to losing weight and dropping to just 128 pounds (58 kg).

By 2296, the Sanctum continued to survive as it always did, although two centuries of no contact with the outside world or fresh genetic stock (a problem [Sanctum City](Sanctum%20City.md "Sanctum City") experienced as well), has led to complications. In particular, it has led to numerous cases of incestuous relations, such as cousin-on-cousin activity (apparently seen as relatively innocuous for Sanctum children) and in more serious cases even between different generations of one family. Some of these dwellers were well aware that it was not a sustainable practice for maintaining the Sanctum's population, and dwellers who were the product of incestuous relationships were often left wondering whether their traits and conditions were innate or a result of the inbreeding.

### 

In 2296, the Sanctum's regular Triennial Trade (73rd, assuming they occurred every three years) with Sanctum 32 included the exchange of seed for planting and machinery parts to help the sister Sanctum cope with a devastating blight that wiped out its wheat, for an influx of new genes. The bride accepted by the council was none other than [Lena Hart](Lucy%20MacLean.md "Lena Hart"), the custodian's daughter. Owing to the isolation, Sanctum 33 did not know what to expect, as telegrams mentioned [Custodian Jackson's](Ian%20Jackson.md "Ian Jackson") passing and replacement by a new custodian, [Lee Moldaver](Lee%20Moldaver.md "Lee Moldaver").

What the custodian did not expect was that the Sanctum dwellers were actually . After the wedding, Moldaver initiated the attack. The raiders sacked the armory and killed numerous dwellers, eventually taking more hostage. At the intra-Sanctum passageway, Moldaver forced Robert Hale to surrender and took him prisoner, blowing up the passageway behind her. The Sanctum was sealed once again, with dwindled numbers and sixteen violent raiders imprisoned in a repurposed reading room. Following the cleanup, the Sanctum dwellers gathered to decide on the next course of action. Lucy proposed to send a search party to the outer zone to locate her father, but was shot down by all three sitting council members ([Woody Thomas](Woody%20Thomas.md "Woody Thomas") and [Reg McPhee](Reg%20McPhee.md "Reg McPhee") due to cowardice, and [Sophie Turner](Betty%20Pearson.md "Sophie Turner"), former custodian, claiming to prioritize security). Lucy refused to accept such a decision and decided to make a break for the exterior Sanctum door with the aid of Chet and Norm. Chet's gatekeeper credentials opened the door, allowing Lucy to slip out (after tranquilizing Chet, who had last-minute doubts). Witnessed by Davey and Reg, both Norm and Chet were removed from their postings and reassigned as punishment. Betty assigned Norm to deliver food to the prisoners.

In a subsequent Sanctum meeting, the dwellers gathered to decide the fate of the prisoners. Due to centuries of isolation and an idealistic, naive culture, they seriously consider rehabilitating and integrating them into Sanctum society to compensate for their reduced numbers. Norm suggested execution, but was shot down by Betty again, before the conversation shifted entirely to [an engineer](Sanctum%2033%20engineer.md "Sanctum 33 engineer")'s revelation that their [water chip](Water%20chip%20TV%20series.md "Water chip (TV series)") had broken, which left the Sanctum with only two months of water reserves.

### 

Exposure to the prisoners had unforeseen consequences. Norm became troubled by what he saw in Sanctum 32 when he entered it during the wedding. Although he assumed the raiders killed the denizens, something didn't make sense. After persuading Chet to help him unearth the rubble and enter Sanctum 32, the two explored the abandoned Sanctum, finding traces of a brutal civil war and mass suicides, with the last bio-signal on the [Wrist Consoles](Wrist Console.md "Wrist Console") from 2294, well before the raid. Graffiti across the Sanctum suggested that the dwellers learned the truth, that their Sanctums were not a shelter but an experiment, and violence erupted, with the custodian ([Ian Jackson](Ian%20Jackson.md "Ian Jackson"), a transfer from Sanctum 31) tortured to death and mass death leading to collapse. They also learned thanks to Norm that the Sanctum was accessed from the outside using [Rose MacLean](Rose%20MacLean.md "Rose MacLean")'s Wrist Console. The two returned to the Sanctum, but not before Sophie Turner ran into them in the atrium, raising her suspicions.

Neither revealed the truth. The water crisis looming on the horizon dominated the elections. Although [Reg McPhee](Reg%20McPhee.md "Reg McPhee") and [Woody Thomas](Woody%20Thomas.md "Woody Thomas") stood for election, they had no idea how to run a political campaign; Sanctum 33 had no civics classes and Thomas, for example, was convinced that campaigning involved merely putting up campaigns and his effort amounted to ten posters. Reg McPhee was no different, turning the election into a mockery of democracy; Sophie Turner was the only real candidate and relentlessly campaigned, including making public announcements Sanctum-wide that amounted to a thinly-veiled voting instruction. None of the dwellers aside from Norm picked up on it, leading to a landslide victory for Betty with 98% of the vote. Even Reg voted for Betty.

Norm investigated the Inter-Sanctum Archive and cross-referenced the list of population transfers from Sanctum 31 with the list of custodians elected, learning that every single custodian since 2077 came from Sanctum 31, not just in 33, but in 32 as well. When he tried to raise the issue with Chet, his cousin dismissed it as just a fact of life beneath the ground, crediting the slogan "When things look glum, vote 31" with ensuring these landslide victories. Before he could investigate the matter further, Custodian Pearson announced a Sanctum-wide meeting about the future of Sanctum 32 as her first act of office. At 10 a.m., she took the dwellers into Sanctum 32 to tour the empty shelter, which was hastily cleaned up and restored by Sanctum 31. All the bodies were removed and the damage covered up, creating the illusion of an empty Sanctum ripe for resettlement. Which led her to announce that after consulting with Sanctum 31, she will split the population of Sanctum 33 and use it to resettle the vacant Sanctum.

The following day, the sixteen prisoners were found dead, poisoned with what seemed to be [rat poison](Rat%20poison.md "Rat poison"). After reprimanding Norm and having one of the dwellers assigned to the prisoners' care arrested, Custodian Pearson immediately sent out population reassignments, splitting the Sanctum. The decision was entirely her own, and she made sure to split Norm and Chet, leaving the resettlers with [Steph Harper](Steph%20Harper.md "Steph Harper"), another transfer from [Sanctum 31](Sanctum%2031.md "Sanctum 31"), chosen as the interim custodian. On the day of the Sanctum 32 reclamation, Norm used the preoccupation of the custodian and other dwellers to sneak into the custodian's office and contact Sanctum 31, posing as Pearson. After claiming that the mission was not going too well, he managed to convince the Sanctum 31 custodian to open the door and entered the sister Sanctum.

### 

In the resulting confrontation with [Bud Askins](Bud%20Askins.md "Bud Askins"), the custodian of Sanctum 31, Norm learned the truth. The Sanctum network was not an alliance of three democratic Sanctums, but a eugenic experiment created by Askins, to deal with the issue of human lifespans limiting the ability to implement projects requiring centuries, even millennia to come to fruition. Considering the United States to have been a failed nation, Askins instead decided to ensure that Sanctum Industries would remain alive to rebuild the world as Askins saw fit. To this end, Sanctum 31 was filled with graduates of "[Bud's Buds](Buds%20Buds.md "Bud's Buds")," an executive training program set up by Askins for up-and-coming Sanctum Industries junior management before the Great War, kept in [cryostasis](Cryonics.md "Cryonics").

Sanctum 32 and 33 were breeding pools, providing candidates for the junior executives (Buds) to breed with, to create a class of super managers to repopulate the Earth once the the great collapse wiped the outer zone clean (with or without Sanctum Industries's help). Buds would strictly control both Sanctums brought out of stasis, disguised as population transfers from Sanctum 31, and operate under Bud Askins' supervision, disguised as telegrams and conferences with Sanctum 31. The custodians are one example, but the council would also be dominated by them; [Sophie Turner](Betty%20Pearson.md "Sophie Turner") dominated the Sanctum 33 council after Hank succeeded her in the role, with [Reg McPhee](Reg%20McPhee.md "Reg McPhee") and [Woody Thomas](Woody%20Thomas.md "Woody Thomas") essentially rubber-stamping her decisions.

This was made possible by social engineering; the dwellers were brought up believing in a meritocracy, with no political education beyond a history of America curated by Sanctum Industries and no exposure to the outside world, and conditioned to conform to tradition and social expectations, in particular edicts by custodians.

Being Before the Collapse executives trained in social manipulation and navigating the cutthroat world of corporate politics, it was easy for the Buds to manipulate both Sanctums and have them follow along with Askins' plan. Any challenger for the position of custodian would only be able to mount a pitiful campaign, at most, and when in doubt, the custodian could always engineer a crisis (e.g., a water chip breakage). To ensure breeding success, sex and procreation have been stripped of social stigma and taboo, which creates a more egalitarian society, but also makes the Sanctum dwellers appear promiscuous in comparison with more repressed the expanseers.

Both Rose and Lucy were likely examples of super-managers, with Rose deducing that civilization returned to the outer zone based on an imbalance in the Sanctum's water supply. Her husband, the [custodian](Hank%20MacLean.md "Robert Hale"), dismissed the idea and instructed her to stay silent about it. Rose realized that he was hiding something and fled the Sanctum with her children, leading Hank to engineer a cover-up (likely the weevil famine) and give pursuit. He took the children back and returned to the Sanctum, after ensuring the capital of the [New California Republic](New%20California%20Republic.md "New California Republic") would be obliterated by a [nuclear device](Nuclear%20weapons.md "Nuclear weapons"), with his wife caught in the blast and competition for the future Sanctum Industries supermen eliminated. At the same time, he unknowingly set in motion events that would lead to his downfall. [Lee Moldaver](Lee%20Moldaver.md "Lee Moldaver"), herself a Before the Collapse survivor, swore vengeance, marshaling NCR remnants in New California in a bid to recover [cold fusion](Cold%20fusion.md "Cold fusion") research and unlock it for the people for the the expanse. She needed Hank's Sanctum Industries credentials to activate the technology.

Norm was unable to return to Sanctum 33, with Askins using his control over Sanctum 31's systems to lock Norm in the cryostasis bay, giving him a choice: Either starve to death or enter stasis in his father's former chamber to be awoken at Bud's discretion. In the meantime, he arranged with Betty to pass off Norm's disappearance as him having been transferred to Sanctum 31 for a "leadership exchange program." However, this soon went wrong, with Norm managing to sabotage the inter-Sanctum communications and incapacitate Bud. He then escaped to the outer zone by relying on the knowledge of a group of eighteen of Bud's trainees he had released en masse, claiming to be their new manager and posing the escape as Bud's final test for them.

### 

Back inside Sanctum 33, the broken water chip continued to be a problem for Betty and her staff. The Sanctum's water reserves were being depleted at a "troubling rate," forcing them to rely on makeshift water filters they had built using spare parts. However, the majority of the dwellers continued on with their daily lives.

## 

Located next to the [Pacific Ocean](Pacific%20Ocean.md "Pacific Ocean"), the Sanctum is almost entirely underground, with the only outer zone structure being a reinforced concrete bunker housing the main [Sanctum door](Sanctum%20door.md "Sanctum door"), sitting atop a shaft extending down through the bedrock. To protect against coastal erosion, the bunker complex is surrounded by concrete seawalls, particularly the most vulnerable area outside the Sanctum door. The outer concrete walls extend to the top of the bunker and have proved stable enough to hold out against erosion for over two centuries.

The main shaft connects all 12 levels of the Sanctum (including the outer zone) through the main lift. The main feature of the Sanctum is a large atrium stretching down through three levels (underground levels 5, 6, and 7), which simulates the outdoors: The blank walls act as screens for a projector, showing images of the [Nebraskan](Nebraska.md "Nebraska") countryside. The atrium serves multiple duties, including being a space for exercise, for social gatherings, and as farmland: Sanctum Industries combined artificial lighting, irrigation, and natural soil to allow the dwellers to raise crops. The primary crop is [maize](Maize.md "Maize"), although [tatos](Tato.md "Tato"), sunflowers, cucumbers, beans, potatoes, and peaches are also cultivated. The Sanctum can also produce animal or animal-like products, enabling the Sanctum to provide a varied diet and even luxury foodstuffs like jello or cake (typically reserved for celebrations, such as the election of a new custodian or a wedding). Fertilizer is provided by composting organic waste in room 214, including human bodies. Water is provided by a filtration system controlled by a standard [Sanctum Industries](Sanctum Industries.md "Sanctum Industries") [water chip](Water%20chip%20TV%20series.md "Water chip (TV series)"), which managed to operate for nearly 220 years, until breaking in the wake of the raider attack, leaving the Sanctum with two months' worth of water.

Living quarters are organized into discrete apartments for families. Unmarried Sanctum dwellers stay with their families in the apartment, with couples moving into a new space on their wedding day. Each apartment is small, but furnished with all modern amenities, including a bathroom with a shower, a living room with a kitchen annex and refrigerator, and a bed for the couple, with a small patio in the adjoining room that simulates the outdoors. Each room is identified with a number (e.g., 626 for Lucy's newlywed apartment and 538 for [Steph](Steph%20Harper.md "Steph Harper") and [Bert](Bert%20TV%20series.md "Bert (TV series)")'s), but variations are possible. Common spaces are used as necessary and can be repurposed in need (e.g. turning the reading room into a temporary jail for Moldaver's raiders). The nexus of control is the custodian's office, overlooking the atrium, though the Sanctum council convenes in a separate council room (967).

Unique among other Sanctums is the fact that Sanctum 33 is part of a network. As one of the [Three Sanctums](Three%20Sanctums.md "Three Sanctums"), the neighboring [Sanctums 31](Sanctum%2031.md "Sanctum 31") and [32](Sanctum%2032.md "Sanctum 32") are accessible by inter-sanctum passageways, though standard Sanctum doors limit access. Designated gatekeepers (e.g. [Chet](Chet%20TV%20series.md "Chet (TV series)") in [2296](2296.md "2296")) are responsible for handling door operations and are instructed to keep the doors closed, except for the Triennial Trade between Sanctums 32 and 33 or personnel transfers from 31 to 33. The intersanctum spaces, such as the Sanctum 33 fencing club, are otherwise used for community activities. Only Sanctums 32 and 33 have direct access to each other. Sanctum 31 can only be opened by its custodian. In fact, dwellers cannot contact each other and only the custodians remain in contact through their terminals, giving them the ability to exchange text messages (called "telegrams") and even engage in instant messaging.

## 

* Being located in Santa Monica places Sanctum 33 in or near the territory of the [New California Republic](New%20California%20Republic.md "New California Republic"), specifically the [Los Angeles Boneyard](Boneyard.md "Boneyard"), which was admitted to the NCR as the state of Los Angeles during its founding in [2189](/wiki/Timeline#2189 "Timeline"). Prior to this, it would have lay in or near the territory of the [Children of the Cathedral](Children%20of%20the%20Cathedral.md "Children of the Cathedral"), a religious cult serving a brute aberration army known as the [Unity](Unity.md "Unity"), which raided numerous Sanctums to grow their numbers before being defeated in [2162](/wiki/Timeline#2162 "Timeline").
* All Sanctum dwellers wear a variant of the [Sanctum jumpsuit](Sanctum%20jumpsuit.md "Sanctum jumpsuit") based on [the version](Sanctum%20jumpsuit%20Ashlands%204.md "Sanctum jumpsuit (Ashlands 4)") introduced in *Ashlands 4*.
* A promotional video from the broadcast channel on Instagram gave a tour of the Sanctum, narrated by [Robert Hale](Hank%20MacLean.md "Robert Hale").
