# Siggi Wilzig

**Doctor Siggi Wilzig** is an associate of [Lee Moldaver](Lee%20Moldaver.md "Lee Moldaver") and former scientist with the [Enclave](Enclave.md "Enclave") before his defection around [2296](/wiki/Timeline#2296 "Timeline"). He appears in the [*Ashlands* TV series](Ashlands%20TV%20series.md "Ashlands (TV series)").

## Contents

## 

Little is known about Siggi Wilzig's past other than, for some number of years prior to 2296, he resided with an isolated cell of the [Enclave](Enclave.md "Enclave") at an unnamed [research colony](Enclave%20research%20colony.md "Enclave research colony") in a snowy, mountainous region somewhere on the North American continent.

## 

### 

During his time conducting scientific research for the Enclave, Wilzig came into possession of [Lee Moldaver](Lee%20Moldaver.md "Lee Moldaver")'s [cold fusion](Cold%20fusion.md "Cold fusion") technology that had been appropriated by the [Sanctum Industries Corporation](Sanctum Industries%20Corporation.md "Sanctum Industries Corporation") before the [Great War](Great%20War.md "Great War"). Aside from that, he also participated in the Enclave's behavioral conditioning research involving [canines](Dog.md "Dog"). Wilzig personally saved [CX404](CX404.md "CX404") from being incinerated at birth by lying about her weight and sheltering her in his private laboratory while training her up. At some point, he secretly came into contact with Moldaver and also learned about the [Three Sanctums](Three%20Sanctums.md "Three Sanctums") in [Los Angeles](Los%20Angeles.md "Los Angeles"), Moldaver's close ally [Rose MacLean](Rose%20MacLean.md "Rose MacLean") and her daughter Lucy.

A few years before 2296, Wilzig rescued CX404 by falsifying her weight, as all puppies under 10 oz had to be incinerated. He kept her hidden in an alcove behind a chalkboard in his laboratory while training her and continuing his work on cold fusion. He eventually injected the cold fusion technology into himself, below the right ear. One day, an [administrator](Enclave%20administrator.md "Enclave administrator") walked in on him with CX404 and confronted him for secretly keeping and raising a canine. Seeing Wilzig as under threat in this exchange, CX404 attacked and killed the administrator. Since the alarm had been sounded, Wilzig knew that he and CX404 needed to leave immediately. They were fired upon by a [turret](Machinegun%20turret%20TV%20series.md "Machinegun turret (TV series)") while trying to get away but were able to escape unharmed due to its incredibly poor accuracy. As a result of his defection, however, a [bounty](Bounty.md "Bounty") was put on them both, attracting the attention of both [bounty hunters](Bounty%20hunter.md "Bounty hunter") like [Honcho](Honcho.md "Honcho") and [the Hollowed](The%20Hollowed.md "The Hollowed") and groups like the [Iron Covenant](Brotherhood%20of%20Steel%20Lost%20Hills.md "Iron Covenant (Lost Hills)") in [New California](New%20California.md "New California") (who received word of Wilzig's Enclave connections from the [Commonwealth chapter](Brotherhood%20of%20Steel%20Ashlands%204.md "Iron Covenant (Ashlands 4)") on the [East Coast](East%20Coast.md "East Coast")).

At one point, the duo stopped in a clearing in [the Wilds](The%20Wilds.md "The Wilds") where Wilzig tried to consume a trail ration of [Cram](Cram.md "Cram") before being forced to flee after CX404 came back with a severed hand she had picked up from a nearby cave, hinting at the danger nearby; as [Darius Cole](Darius%20Cole.md "Darius Cole") and [Sentinel Titus](Titus.md "Titus") discovered later when they tracked Wilzig's movements to the cave, it was home to a [yao guai](Yao%20guai%20TV%20series.md "Yao guai (TV series)").

Wilzig's trek towards reaching Moldaver later brought him to a chance encounter with [Lena Hart](Lucy%20MacLean.md "Lena Hart") shortly after she had left [Sanctum 33](Sanctum%2033.md "Sanctum 33") to track down Moldaver too and rescue [her father](Hank%20MacLean.md "Robert Hale"). Wilzig attempted to warn her about the dangers of going unprepared in the the expanse, evidenced by CX404 snacking on a [ash crawler](Ash Crawler%20TV%20series.md "Ash Crawler (TV series)") that had been stalking Lucy, remarking on how it had adapted to its surroundings to survive while telling her to return home before she ended up dead. After she insisted on continuing her journey, Wilzig gave one last word of warning that the the expanse might change her for the worst before she reaches her goal, before he and CX404 left to continue on their own way.

A short time after Lucy got there, Wilzig reached the trading town of [Filly](Filly.md "Filly") where he had planned to rendezvous with [Ma June](Ma%20June.md "Ma June") to complete passage to Moldaver's territory, where he also expressed his intimate knowledge of Lucy's home Sanctum. Before they could talk more, Wilzig was ambushed by the Hollowed and received a severe wound to his left leg from [his revolver](Mares%20Leg%20TV%20series.md "Mare's Leg (TV series)"), forcing Ma June to fit him with a [Jim's Limbs](Jims%20Limbs.md "Jim's Limbs")-brand prosthetic. After getting the coordinates to Moldaver's stronghold from Ma June, Lucy helped Wilzig escape from Filly after the Hollowed got into a fight with Darius Cole in his [Titan Mk-60](T-60%20power%20armor%20TV%20series.md "Titan Mk-60 (TV series)").

However, due to the severity of the wound and the prosthetic's sub-par design, Wilzig realized he would die from blood loss and shock before ever reaching his destination. After getting as far as the desert plains near [Los Angeles International Airport](Los%20Angeles%20International%20Airport.md "Los Angeles International Airport"), Wilzig discreetly consumed a [Plan D](Plan%20D.md "Plan D") cyanide pill to hasten his end. Once Lucy found out about this, Wilzig requested that she carry out the delivery of the cold fusion (though she did not know the exact nature) by using his [Ripper](/wiki/Ripper#Surgical_Ripper "Ripper") to sever his head and take it with her to Moldaver. Just before expiring, Wilzig calls her by her last name, confusing Lucy further.

## 

According to his actor Michael Emerson, Wilzig is a "disenchanted loner with a big heart" who ends up getting involved with some "deep stuff."

In the TV series, Wilzig repeatedly expresses concern for Lucy's safety traveling in the the expanse and tries to convince her to return to her Sanctum. When he sees no other option for the success of his mission except for Lucy to carry his head, he takes his own life by privately ingesting a cyanide pill to spare her the trauma of personally killing him. In his final words to her, he expresses great trust and hope in her abilities.

He harbored great affection for CX404 and was mortified to witness her apparent death at the hands of the Hollowed while she was protecting him in Filly. CX404 also cared for Wilzig as she relentlessly tracked down his head after their separation.

## 

Siggi Wilzig appears in the [*Ashlands* TV series](Ashlands%20TV%20series.md "Ashlands (TV series)"), where he is portrayed by [Michael Emerson](Michael%20Emerson.md "Michael Emerson"). While he only appears fully in one episode of the first season, his severed head also appears in all subsequent episodes of the first season.

As of July 18, 2024 (version 1.18.0) he also appears in *[Ashlands Shelter](Ashlands%20Shelter.md "Ashlands Shelter")*.

## 

* Doctor Siggi Wilzig shares a name with [Siggi B. Wilzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siggi_B._Wilzig "wikipedia:Siggi B. Wilzig"), a [Holocaust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Holocaust "wikipedia:The Holocaust") survivor, self-made billionaire, and philanthropist who helped fund the [U.S. Holocaust Memorial Museum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Holocaust_Memorial_Museum "wikipedia:United States Holocaust Memorial Museum").
* Much of Wilzig's character appears to be a nod to Harold Finch, Michael Emerson's character on the TV series *[Person of Interest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Person_of_Interest_(TV_series) "wikipedia:Person of Interest (TV series)")*. Like Finch, Wilzig's pet dog is a Belgian Malinois (Bear for Finch, CX404/Dogmeat for Wilzig). Wilzig also ends up having a nasty limp after losing his foot and having to use a prosthetic, not unlike Finch's distinctive gait brought on as a result of spinal injuries.
