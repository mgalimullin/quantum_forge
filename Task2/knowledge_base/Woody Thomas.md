# Woody Thomas

**Woody Thomas** is a resident of [Sanctum 33](Sanctum%2033.md "Sanctum 33") who later moves into [Sanctum 32](Sanctum%2032.md "Sanctum 32") in the [*Ashlands* TV series](Ashlands%20TV%20series.md "Ashlands (TV series)").

## Contents

## 

Woody is a native resident of Sanctum 33. By 2296, he had become a member of the the Sanctum's three-person governing council alongside [Sophie Turner](Betty%20Pearson.md "Sophie Turner") and [Reg McPhee](Reg%20McPhee.md "Reg McPhee"), under Custodian [Robert Hale](Hank%20MacLean.md "Robert Hale").

However, following the attack on the Sanctum by [Sanctum 32 raiders](Sanctum%2032%20raider.md "Sanctum 32 raider") that year, Hank was abducted, and a crisis erupted when the Sanctum's [water chip](Water%20chip%20TV%20series.md "Water chip (TV series)") broke. The governing council was disbanded, and Betty was elected custodian in Hank's absence, beating Reg and Woody. Woody was subsequently reassigned as part of the group sent to resettle [Sanctum 32](Sanctum%2032.md "Sanctum 32"), and was coerced into joining the group despite his initial objections.

## 

### 

#### 

Woody, alongside his Sanctum 33 council associates Betty and Reg, listens to [Lucy](Lucy%20MacLean.md "Lena Hart") give her speech request to be allowed to participate in the Triennial Trade with [Sanctum 32](Sanctum%2032.md "Sanctum 32"). The council unanimously agrees to approve Lucy's marriage application.

Sometime later, Woody is seen greeting the [Sanctum 32 dwellers](Sanctum%2032%20raider.md "Sanctum 32 raider") and their new custodian, [Lee Moldaver](Lee%20Moldaver.md "Lee Moldaver"), who introduces Lucy's new husband, [Monty](Monty%20TV%20series.md "Monty (TV series)"), to her. After the wedding ceremony is held, he and Reg are seen setting up the podium for Custodian Hank, who gives a poignant speech about his daughter's marriage. The two then stand and watch as the [Sanctum 33 dwellers](Sanctum%2033%20dweller.md "Sanctum 33 dweller") dance with their Sanctum 32 counterparts.

When the Sanctum 32 dwellers are revealed to have been [raiders from the outer zone](Sanctum%2032%20raider.md "Sanctum 32 raider"), Woody hides behind a doorframe. Upon seeing two people hauling in more food for the party, Woody tells them to take it away, but he is too late; he soon witnesses a [shirtless raider](Shirtless%20raider.md "Shirtless raider") kill one dweller and then shoot the two who were bringing food; Woody himself manages to avoid being shot.

Rushing into a nearby classroom, Woody runs past some raiders who are beating up a Sanctum 33 dweller, and then hides behind another doorframe when another dweller in the distant corridor is shot and killed by one of [Moldaver's elite guards](Moldavers%20elite%20guard.md "Moldaver's elite guard").

With Hank having been kidnapped by the raiders, Reg, Woody, and Betty hold an emergency assembly with the surviving Sanctum 33 dwellers in their authority as the governing council. During the assembly, Lucy proposes sending a search team to the outer zone to save her father. However, none of the council members agree with her; Reg questions her plan, Woody tries to mediate, and finally, Betty stresses the importance of the Sanctum's security, which both Reg and Woody agree with. Lucy leaves, disconcerted, while Woody and the others continue the assembly. Lucy's brother [Norm](Norm%20MacLean.md "Norm MacLean") tells her that the three council members may have refused out of a desire to preserve their newfound authority. Soon after, Lucy leaves the Sanctum on her own to search for her father, aided by Norm and [Chet](Chet%20TV%20series.md "Chet (TV series)").

#### 

In the wake of Lucy's departure from Sanctum 33, Woody and the other two councilmembers hold disciplinary sessions to punish Chet and Norm, firing Chet from his role as gatekeeper. Woody introduces Norm to his session, during which he and Reg alternately lead the conversation, discussing Norm's involvement and the dangers of opening the Sanctum door. Woody points out that Norm's performance reviews for his various assignments have all listed him as "lacking enthusiasm," and his confirmation notably displeases Woody. However, Betty seems to get the final say in Norm's punishment, and in response to a comment he makes, she reassigns him to deliver food to sixteen surviving raiders who have been imprisoned in the Sanctum.

Afterward, an assembly is held in the Sanctum to brainstorm what to do with the surviving raiders. After Woody announces their ethical dilemma, Reg chimes in, claiming that they have been given a prime opportunity to prove to the outer zone world the Sanctum's goal of repopulating and building a better world on the outer zone, though Woody calls this a "positive spin" on the situation. He asks for a collective decision, though Betty tells them to promote an idea for discussion. Thanks to a "conversation" that Woody had with one of the raiders, Reg suggests that the Sanctum 33 dwellers rehabilitate the raiders to integrate them into their society. A few ideas are then proposed by some Sanctum 33 dwellers, including [Davey](Davey%20TV%20series.md "Davey (TV series)") and [Marianne](Marianne%20TV%20series.md "Marianne (TV series)"). Norm also raises a suggestion to kill the raiders as retribution, though this is shot down.

At that moment, the [Sanctum 33 engineer](Sanctum%2033%20engineer.md "Sanctum 33 engineer") appears and tries to privately talk with Reg and Woody. However, the two insist that they talk in public, despite the engineer's objections. Reluctantly, the engineer reveals that the Sanctum's [water chip](Water%20chip%20TV%20series.md "Water chip (TV series)") was destroyed, leaving only two months of water for the Sanctum 33 residents to survive. Before the engineer could finish, Reg cut off the engineer, and the meeting was adjourned.

#### 

With Hank gone, an election is set to be held for the Sanctum's new custodian. Woody, Reg, and Betty are all running in the election, and Woody and Reg are seen hanging up posters for their electoral campaign. Later, they meet outside a cell where a semi-sedated Sanctum 32 raider is being held for interrogation about his background and the kidnapping of Hank.

As they continued talking, the semi-sedated Sanctum 32 raider regained consciousness and tried to gnaw off his restraints, though Woody advises him against it. The Sanctum 32 raider curses at Woody and remains uncooperative, so Woody decides to retranquilize the raider and retry the interrogation tomorrow.

#### 

Betty ends up winning the custodian election with 98% of the vote. During the ceremony for Betty's victory, Woody and Reg are in the back, also clapping for Betty. Woody comments "better luck next time" to Reg, and he softly agrees.

Later, Woody stands in front of one of his campaign posters with a forlorn look. Davey walks by and compliments Woody for running a "great" campaign, though Woody notes that he only hung ten posters up. Davey sympathizes, admitting he once ran and lost an election against Hank, but understands that "When things look glum, vote for someone from [Sanctum 31](Sanctum%2031.md "Sanctum 31")." Before leaving, Davey admits to Woody that he voted for Betty.

Soon after, Betty announces that she and the [Sanctum 31 custodian](Bud%20Askins.md "Bud Askins") have formulated a plan to resettle Sanctum 32, which will involve transferring many of Sanctum 33's dwellers into the new Sanctum. Woody tours Sanctum 32 along with many other Sanctum 33 dwellers, unaware that its newly-cleaned facade hides the violence and death that had occurred in the Sanctum.

#### 

Woody and Betty are present when Norm tries to deliver food for the Sanctum 32 raiders only to discover they have all been poisoned to death, with [Veronica](Veronica%20TV%20series.md "Veronica (TV series)") taking the blame. Immediately after Betty leaves scene, she sends out the Sanctum 32 reassignment notices. Woody learns that he is being sent to Sanctum 32, much to his dismay.

On the day when the new Sanctum 32 dwellers are to be sent to Sanctum 32, Woody tries to back out but is stopped by [security guards](Sanctum%2033%20guard.md "Sanctum 33 guard"). Unable to bypass them, Woody shrinks back and decides to get back in line for his transfer. Before he leaves for Sanctum 32, Reg hugs him goodbye, and he's last seen as the Sanctum 32 door closes with him and his fellow new Sanctum 32 dwellers behind it.

## 

Woody is naive and portrays himself as optimistic and agreeable. While he attempts to assert himself as a governing voice, he is overshadowed by his governing peers, Betty and Reg, which causes his fellow Sanctum dwellers to not take him seriously. In truth, he is a coward, and his cowardice occasionally overcomes his positive and agreeable demeanor, such as when he objected to being reassigned to Sanctum 32. However, when he tries to back out of being reassigned to Sanctum 32, the sanctum security guards intimidate him into compliance, which causes him to capitulate and meekly obey the orders of his Custodian.
