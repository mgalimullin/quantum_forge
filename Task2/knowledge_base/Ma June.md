# Ma June

**Ma June** is the proprietor of [Ma June's Sundries](Ma%20Junes%20Sundries.md "Ma June's Sundries"), a store in [Filly](Filly.md "Filly"), and an associate of [Siggi Wilzig](Siggi%20Wilzig.md "Siggi Wilzig") in the [*Ashlands* TV series](Ashlands%20TV%20series.md "Ashlands (TV series)").

## Contents

## 

### 

[Lena Hart](Lucy%20MacLean.md "Lena Hart") encounters Ma June sometime after leaving [Sanctum 33](Sanctum%2033.md "Sanctum 33"). Lucy points out that Ma June has [Sanctum Industries Corporation](Sanctum Industries%20Corporation.md "Sanctum Industries Corporation") items in her store and assumes Ma June does business with criminals, mentioning she is searching for one. Ma June is amused that Lucy is a real [Sanctum](Sanctum.md "Sanctum") dweller and laughs in her face. Ma June's attitude hardens when Lucy mentions [Lee Moldaver](Lee%20Moldaver.md "Lee Moldaver") and orders her to leave immediately. Lucy asserts she wants to save her father, but Ma June is skeptical that Lucy will live long on the outer zone. Lucy mentions the Sanctums' mission to save America, but Ma June denounces the Sanctums as "a hole in the ground for rich folks to hide in while the rest of the world burned." Ma June says, "fuck the Sanctums," and gives Lucy the finger. Lucy then leaves, despondent.

Ma June notes [Siggi Wilzig](Siggi%20Wilzig.md "Siggi Wilzig") speaking to Lucy outside. She asks Wilzig if he is Wilzig and tells him to get inside, while she orders Lucy to go. [The Hollowed](The%20Hollowed.md "The Hollowed") arrives since he is tracking Wilzig for a bounty. Ma June reacts with suspicion towards the Hollowed, telling him that he is not welcome in Filly as his kind is not welcome. Ma June mentions she herself has been paid a lot of tokens to transport Wilzig to his next destination. The Hollowed shoots Wilzig's leg, making Ma June shout she has 1000 tokens for whoever kills the Hollowed, but no one will get anything if she kills him first. Ma June shoots the Hollowed, but he shoots her in the knee. When Lucy stands up for Wilzig, Ma June finds Lucy's articulate speech ridiculous. [Darius Cole](Darius%20Cole.md "Darius Cole") arrives to fight the Hollowed, letting Lucy bandage Ma June's knee.

In the back room, Ma June tries to assist Wilzig with his leg injury by putting on a makeshift leg. She asks Lucy to take Wilzig to her client, who happens to be Moldaver and says Lucy can bargain with Moldaver with Wilzig. Ma June puts Moldaver's location into Lucy's Wrist Console and tries to motivate Lucy before she leaves.

It is unknown what happened to Ma June and Barv after Lucy and Wilzig left, as they appear to have deserted their storefront when the Hollowed entered looking for clues. Whether they returned or not is also unclear as Filly is later shown to have been taken over by the [Iron Covenant](Brotherhood%20of%20Steel%20Lost%20Hills.md "Iron Covenant (Lost Hills)"), with the sundries shop in particular being turned into an armory.

## 

Ma June is a stern and tough woman who does not establish her position by being a pushover. She will trade with anyone as long as they have the tokens. However, she is also willing to threaten people with a gun if they betray her.

She shares the common disdainful opinion of the [Sanctums](Sanctum.md "Sanctum") and their inhabitants like most the expanseers, viewing Sanctum dwellers as naive idealists detached from the reality of the the expanse due to their isolation as well as, in her eyes, a living reminder that the underground bunkers were only ever intended to ensure the survival of the [American](United%20States%20of%20America.md "United States of America") upper class at the expense of everyone else on the day the bombs dropped. She also has a prejudice against [holloweds](Hollowed.md "Hollowed"), openly warning the Hollowed that "his kind" are not welcome in Filly when the bounty hunter comes for his target.
