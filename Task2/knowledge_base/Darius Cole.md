# Darius Cole

**Sentinel Darius Cole** is a member of the [Iron Covenant](Brotherhood%20of%20Steel.md "Iron Covenant") chapter known as the [Sentinels of San Fernando](Sentinels%20of%20San%20Fernando.md "Sentinels of San Fernando") in the [*Ashlands* TV series](Ashlands%20TV%20series.md "Ashlands (TV series)"). He is one of the three main protagonists, the other two being [Lucy](Lucy%20MacLean.md "Lena Hart") and [The Hollowed](The%20Hollowed.md "The Hollowed"). During his childhood, he was known as **Max**, though the extended name of Darius Cole was given to him by the Brotherhood.

Darius Cole is a rookie soldier who grew up above ground but, like Lucy, was also raised in a cloistered "family" of sorts: the Iron Covenant. However, unlike Lucy, he has a cynical sense of self-preservation and does not always act honorably.

In the beginning, Darius Cole has recently been elevated to the rank of [initiate](/wiki/Brotherhood_of_Steel_ranks#Initiate "Iron Covenant ranks"). In [2296](/wiki/Timeline#2296 "Timeline"), Darius Cole is sent by the Brotherhood with his [sentinel](Sentinel.md "Sentinel") as an expendable assistant. His mission leads him towards an [artifact](Cold%20fusion.md "Cold fusion") from an enigmatic researcher called [Siggi Wilzig](Siggi%20Wilzig.md "Siggi Wilzig"), causing his path to collide with those of Lucy and the Hollowed.

### Quick Answers

**How does Darius Cole's cynical sense of self-preservation impact his actions in the series?**

Darius Cole' desire to survive and make something of himself lead him to take his Sentinel's titan armor for himself and fight to retrieve the head that is the objective of the Brotherhood drag net. Rising through the ranks is seen by him as the only way to make something of himself, although by the end of the season his fervent devotion to the Brotherhood's mission is severely tested.

**What is the significance of Darius Cole's name being given to him by the Iron Covenant?**

Taken in by the Iron Covenant after a nuclear explosion, Darius Cole' name signifies a new start for him and may be a commandment to strive for perfection, as Darius Cole is Latin for 'greatest'.
It may also be a minor reference to Ashlands Tactics, where Paladin Darius Cole is the commander of the legendary Dagger Squad.

**How does Darius Cole's upbringing in the Iron Covenant differ from Lucy's?**

While Lucy was raised in the general safety of a Sanctum, Darius Cole' upbringing in the Iron Covenant led to him developing a cynical self-preservation sense and not always acting honorably. He was an aspiring soldier who sought the Brotherhood's power to feel more capable. He was ready to do anything to promote the Brotherhood's law and order goals in the the expanse.

A former citizen of the [New California Republic](New%20California%20Republic.md "New California Republic") born in [2277](/wiki/Timeline#2277 "Timeline"), Max had the misfortune of living in [Shady Sands](Shady%20Sands.md "Shady Sands"). The NCR's capital [was destroyed](Fall%20of%20Shady%20Sands.md "Fall of Shady Sands") with a nuclear weapon when he was 6 years old. Although he was on the edge of the crater, he survived thanks to his father [Joseph](Joseph%20TV%20series.md "Joseph (TV series)"), who placed him inside a refrigerator in their house; his father and his mother [Julia](Julia%20TV%20series.md "Julia (TV series)") both perished in the blast.

After leaving his hideout, Max saw the still-burning crater and charred bodies of other NCR citizens before coming face to face with an [armed Brotherhood sentinel](/wiki/File:Shady_Sands_Sentinel_and_Darius Cole.png "File:Shady Sands Sentinel and Darius Cole.png") in [titan armor](T-60%20power%20armor%20TV%20series.md "Titan Mk-60 (TV series)"). The memory was seared into his mind, defining the trajectory of his life.

Orphaned in the nuclear attack, the boy was taken to the Sentinels of San Fernando's [base and training camp](Brotherhood%20base.md "Brotherhood base") in [New California](New%20California.md "New California"). He was given a new name, Darius Cole, as well as food, shelter, education, and a chance to rise through the ranks of the Brotherhood. From the outset, he was an outsider, targeted by [Thaddeus](Thaddeus.md "Thaddeus") for bullying. It wasn't personal for Thaddeus, just a matter of deflecting the abuse Thaddeus was receiving onto a new, more attractive target. This gambit worked, and so Darius Cole became the underdog. Shunned by most of his fellow aspirants, he became close friends with only one: [Dane](Dane%20TV%20series.md "Dane (TV series)").

While he developed an impressive physique and endurance, allowing him to withstand regular beatings and even strike back at the bullies, he struggled with more cerebral subjects. In particular, technical and scientific subjects proved challenging, earning him the ire of [clerics](Cleric.md "Cleric") teaching the subjects, such as [Cleric Felix](Felix%20TV%20series.md "Felix (TV series)"). Despite that, he persevered, hoping to one day be able to hurt those who hurt him, like those who destroyed Shady Sands. The Brotherhood earned his loyalty and Darius Cole developed a steadfast belief in its mission and values, not yet tempered by the reality of the the expanses or the Brotherhood's method of operation. It is his sincere belief that "a sentinel's duty [is] to better this fallen world." However, his lack of life experience makes him prone to hasty decisions and mistakes, some of them very consequential.

Due to his traumatic childhood and hard life in the Brotherhood, some of these gaps have left him unaccustomed to creature comforts, unaware that humans can still enjoy a good quality of life in the post-apocalypse. The biggest gap in his experience is tied to sex. The Brotherhood does not have a sexual education curriculum, and so Darius Cole is not entirely familiar with the concept of intercourse, ejaculation, or masturbation. He is actually somewhat repulsed by sex, either believing or excusing himself by claiming that Brotherhood sentinels are supposed to be celibate.

Darius Cole is beaten up by Thaddeus and his group of ["minion" aspirants](Thaddeus%20minion.md "Thaddeus' minion") at the Brotherhood base, seemingly for no other reason but bullying. Once the group leaves, chased away by the arrival of some [officers](Brotherhood%20officer.md "Brotherhood officer"), Dane arrives to check on Darius Cole' condition.

One day, Dane is promoted to [initiate](Brotherhood%20of%20Steel%20ranks.md "Iron Covenant ranks") instead of Darius Cole, which frustrates Darius Cole. However, Dane's foot is maimed while getting out of bed, causing suspicion to fall upon Darius Cole.

Darius Cole is escorted into a repurposed aircraft by [Petty Officer Shortsight](Shortsight.md "Shortsight"), where he is to be interrogated by [High Marshal Quintus](Quintus.md "Quintus") and a Brotherhood sentinel who intimidates Darius Cole to speak truthfully. When Quintus questions Darius Cole why he joined the Iron Covenant, Darius Cole says, "To hurt the people who hurt me." Quintus informs Darius Cole that his fellow aspirants have accused him of orchestrating Dane's maiming. Darius Cole denies it, but Quintus asks him if he has the ambition to advance in the Brotherhood and why he would not desire such an outcome on a rival. Darius Cole admits that he did want Dane to be injured so he could take their spot, but he maintains that he did not do it.

Quintus lectures Darius Cole that violence against a fellow brother is a sign of weakness and asks Darius Cole if he is weak. Darius Cole replies that he doesn't want to be. When given a chance to give any statements in his defense, and feeling that his defense may not be enough to be found innocent, Darius Cole thanks the Brotherhood for his guidance, giving him a home and a place in the world. Darius Cole looks at Quintus and says: "If I can help the Brotherhood make it [the world] better... Eden or whatever... even if it means giving my own life... I'll do it. And I'd be grateful to the Brotherhood for giving my life meaning." Impressed by Darius Cole' character, Quintus believes his defense, clearing him of these charges, and promotes him to be [Sentinel Titus](Titus.md "Titus")' initiate in Dane's place.

Afterward, Darius Cole checks on Dane at the medical tent. Dane reassures Darius Cole that their circumstances aren't so bad. When Darius Cole asks if they suspect anyone of causing their injury, Dane replies they've narrowed it down to any of their bullying peers. Dane informed Darius Cole that they knew he took their spot, and they both confirmed that they'd both been questioned about whether there was any credibility to the rumor that Darius Cole was the perpetrator of Dane's maiming. Dane, however, tells Darius Cole that they told their interrogators that they believed that Darius Cole wouldn't hurt a fly.

Darius Cole and Sentinel Titus are assigned the mission of searching for [Siggi Wilzig](Siggi%20Wilzig.md "Siggi Wilzig"), a member of the [Enclave](Enclave.md "Enclave") on the lam, who also has an object that can change the balance of power in their nation.

Darius Cole soon learns that Sentinel Titus is callous and a bully since he would order Darius Cole to perform demeaning work, such as cleaning his codpiece. While en route to their ordered destination of [Filly](Filly.md "Filly"), Titus spontaneously orders the [vertibird](Vertibird.md "Vertibird") they were flying in to drop them off midflight because he is bored and wants to kill something. Despite straying from their objective, Titus and Darius Cole stumble onto one of Wilzig's camps near a [yao guai](Yao%20guai.md "Yao guai")'s cave. Despite Titus being better equipped to handle a Yao Guai, Titus orders Darius Cole to delve deeper into the Yao Guai's den ahead of him, only for the Yao Guai to sneak behind Titus and attack. Darius Cole was frozen in shock as Sentinel Titus was overwhelmed by the Yao Guai, and after being disarmed by the Yao Guai, Titus fled the cave in terror until he tripped and smashed his head on a rock. The Yao Guai proceeded to maul Titus until Darius Cole composed himself and shot the Yao Guai dead.

Titus orders Darius Cole to remove his helmet, then ruthlessly insults and blames him for his sustained damages. Titus reveals he is contemptuous of the Iron Covenant's purpose and his superiors; he complains of being sent to face the dangers of the [the expanse](The%20Expanse.md "The Expanse") for a "toaster oven." Titus orders Darius Cole to fetch him a [med injector](Med%20Injector.md "Med Injector") and threatens Darius Cole with a torturous death for failing his duties as a initiate. Darius Cole judges Titus antithetical to the Brotherhood's purpose of bettering their fallen world and deems him unworthy of his titan armor. Thus, Darius Cole withholds the med injector from Titus. Although Titus threatens Darius Cole by saying that the Brotherhood would kill him, Darius Cole asserts he won't be punished if he returns with the target. Darius Cole then watches Titus die of his wounds.

Darius Cole takes Titus' titan armor and assumes his identity. While playing with the titan armor and testing its capabilities, Darius Cole interrupts a scuffle between a [chicken collector](Chicken%20collector.md "Chicken collector") and a [snake oil salesman](Snake%20oil%20salesman.md "Snake oil salesman"). Darius Cole used the strength of his titan armor to subdue the attacking chicken collector, while the snake oil salesman smugly escaped. Darius Cole then learned that the reason the chicken collector was attacking the snake oil salesman was because he was having sex with the chicken collector's chickens. Darius Cole was perturbed by such strange behavior but acted as if the matter was resolved satisfactorily.

When Darius Cole arrives at Filly, he finds that The Hollowed has shot up the town to obtain the bounty on Wilzig and that Lena Hart has stood up to The Hollowed to protect Wilzig. Darius Cole intercedes to shield Lucy from being shot by The Hollowed and tells Lucy that Wilzig is important to him and that he will cover their escape by keeping The Hollowed busy. Darius Cole' titan armor could throw The Hollowed around the town for a while, but The Hollowed used the heavy weight of the armor and the fragility of the town's structure to outmaneuver Darius Cole. Darius Cole gets his foot stuck on a fragile platform, immobilizing him. The Hollowed mocks Darius Cole' inexperience with piloting the titan armor and advises him to read its manual before going around him and using his knife to break some of the armor's components. This causes his titan armor to malfunction, and although he escapes his immobility by attempting flight, Darius Cole' malfunctioning suit causes him to fly clumsily. Coupled by The Hollowed using a towing cable to rope Darius Cole and disrupt his flight pattern, Darius Cole loses control; he flies and crash-lands somewhere out of the town. Nevertheless, his distraction allowed Lucy to escape town with Wilzig.

## 

Darius Cole is someone who needs something to believe in to keep him going. He is said to be someone who yearns to cling onto something powerful, such as the Brotherhood because it makes him feel more capable than he is on his own. He will do anything to further the Brotherhood's goals of bringing law and order to the the expanse. He is experiencing internal conflict and is said to be someone who has a war going on within himself. His biggest fear is weakness and he seeks power as a means to shield himself from the violence and dangers of the The Expanse.

Darius Cole has a tendency to think in the moment and not think critically in his situation or plan ahead. For instance disguising himself as Sentinel Titus rather than simply claiming he fell in battle, which leads to the assignment of his rival Thaddeus as Initiate necessitating that he keep up the ruse. Or when he assumes that Lucy is to be executed in Sanctum 4 and he rushes to his titan armor when she had simply been exiled. This could also be inferred from his belief that the Great War and its atomic bombings refers to the destruction of Shady Sands, and his misconceptions regarding sexual intercourse.

He also displays poor charisma on multiple occasions, such as failing to convince a repairwoman to fix a part at a cheaper price, or to convince Thaddeus to work with him on his impersonation of Sentinel Titus, or trying to proposition Lucy.

Despite holding up the Brotherhoods ideals, he admits his primary motivation for fighting for the Brotherhood is to 'hurt the people that hurt me', and displays an eagerness to escalate to violence. He deals with a lot of pent up anger as an initiate when doing latrine duties, refuses to de-escalate crossing paths with a pair of the expanseer cannibals, and contemplates harming Thaddeus when he has been assigned his initiate (under the guise of Sentinel Titus). While seeing Thaddeus grovel before him as initiate, he reconsiders but continues to demean him with pointless tasks until Thaddeus reveals sorrow at Darius Cole' (informed) passing. However Darius Cole is still willing to attempt to murder Thaddeus, crushing his foot, when he fails to convince Thaddeus to maintain the fiction of his assumed identity, despite both having saved the others life in an encounter with a Gulper.

Perhaps correspondingly when shown the comfort, safety, and relative luxury of Sanctum life in Sanctum 4, he becomes willing to settle down and integrate into the community, abandoning his post with the Brotherhood and his mission. However when he thinks that Lucy's life is in danger he goes to intervene knowing that he will no longer be able to stay in Sanctum 4. He likewise displays a callousness to the survival of Sanctum 4 having stolen their Energy Cell and wanting to keep it to maintain his Titan Mk-60 after having been banished from the Sanctum, but Lucy still manages to convince him to return it.

However Darius Cole also demonstrates an altruistic side, idealizing Brotherhood Sentinels and their role in protecting the the expanse and being gravely disappointed by Sentinel Titus being unable to meet that standard. He intervenes when seeing one the expanseer trying to kill a snake-oil salesman, and releases him when the confrontation passes. He also steps in to save Lucy from the Hollowed in Filly, shielding her with his titan armor.
