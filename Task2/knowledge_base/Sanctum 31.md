# Sanctum 31

**Sanctum 31** is a [location](Ashlands%20TV%20series%20locations.md "Ashlands TV series locations") near [Los Angeles](Los%20Angeles.md "Los Angeles") in the [*Ashlands* TV series](Ashlands%20TV%20series.md "Ashlands (TV series)").

## Contents

## 

As part of the [Three Sanctums](Three%20Sanctums.md "Three Sanctums") built underneath [Santa Monica](Santa%20Monica.md "Santa Monica") in greater Los Angeles, Sanctum 31 is directly connected to two other Sanctums, [Sanctum 32](Sanctum%2032.md "Sanctum 32") and [Sanctum 33](Sanctum%2033.md "Sanctum 33"), forming a tripartite society. Outwardly, this society is divided into three Sanctums to serve as a buffer against threats in case one Sanctum is compromised while still being able to support one another through times of crisis. The residents of the other Sanctums are told that Sanctum 31 is just like them and only differs in having higher-quality resources, education, and food.

In reality, the Three Sanctums themselves are the focus of a [social experiment](/wiki/Sanctum#True_purpose "Sanctum") entitled "[Bud's Buds](Buds%20Buds.md "Bud's Buds")," devised by pre-[War](Great%20War.md "Great War") [Sanctum Industries](Sanctum Industries%20Corporation.md "Sanctum Industries Corporation") sales executive [Bud Askins](Bud%20Askins.md "Bud Askins"). Bud sought to use the three Sanctums to breed a population of loyal Sanctum Industries employees who would then go on to establish a monopoly over the wastes after everyone on the outer zone had perished from the great collapse, accomplished by populating Sanctum 31 with a hand-picked group of junior Sanctum Industries executives whom he trained who would then be [cryonically](Cryonics.md "Cryonics") frozen in the Sanctum. In contrast, the other two Sanctums were populated with people with traits he found genetically desirable who would live normal lives within the ashlands shelters.

Per Bud's design, once every thirty years, his junior executives would be sent out into the other two sanctums to eventually become their [custodians](Custodian.md "Custodian") by means of an unexpected crisis and breed with each new generation of residents to create a lineage of "super managers" loyal to Sanctum Industries, guiding their After the Collapse society towards Sanctum Industries's mission to "inherit the Earth after [wiping] the outer zone clean." Bud himself would act as Sanctum 31's custodian, living well into the post-nuclear era by having his brain preserved in a rudimentary robotic shell described as a "Brain-in-a-Roomba."

Ostensibly, the three Sanctums regularly conduct intermarriage and inter-Sanctum trade every three years in a ceremony known as the , but for the "residents" of Sanctum 31, this is a ruse concocted to provide them with any necessary items and to enable the individuals cryonically frozen in Sanctum 31 to join the other sanctums and accomplish Bud's directive without garnering suspicion. By the 2290s, all the custodians of Sanctums 32 and 33 have been members of Bud's Buds, including [George Yaffe](George%20Yaffe.md "George Yaffe"), [Sophie Turner](Betty%20Pearson.md "Sophie Turner"), and [Robert Hale](Hank%20MacLean.md "Robert Hale"). To ensure their placement in the custodian's seat, the populated sanctums often experience crisis situations that the candidate from 31 successfully solves to boost their campaign prospects; this trend is so well-known that both of the sanctums have adopted an electoral slogan celebrating candidates from Sanctum 31.

## 

As [Norm MacLean](Norm%20MacLean.md "Norm MacLean") and [Chet](Chet%20TV%20series.md "Chet (TV series)") discovered during their investigation into the destruction of the original residents of [Sanctum 32](Sanctum%2032.md "Sanctum 32"), they were not killed by the raiders [Moldaver](Lee%20Moldaver.md "Lee Moldaver") led when she kidnapped Norm's father Hank in 2296. Instead, a few years before the raiders' invasion, the residents killed themselves upon devolving into warring factions after killing [Custodian Ian Jackson](Ian%20Jackson.md "Ian Jackson") and his staff, also taking the time to mark the walls of the Sanctum in blood with the words "WE KNOW THE TRUTH" and "DEATH TO MANAGEMENT," indicating they had uncovered the existence of "Bud's Buds," though the means by which this occurred are unclear.

Later, after [Sophie Turner](Betty%20Pearson.md "Sophie Turner") is elected as the new Sanctum 33 custodian in Hank's absence, she arranged for a select number of 33's residents to immigrate to Sanctum 32, which was cleaned up and all traces of its destruction removed, all done supposedly on the recommendation of the elite residents of Sanctum 31. After the reassignments went through, [Steph Harper](Steph%20Harper.md "Steph Harper"), another transplant from Sanctum 31, became the custodian of Sanctum 32 and its new residents. Eventually, Norm's suspicions drove him to hijack Betty's custodian access and communicate with the staff in Sanctum 31, tricking its custodian into thinking he's Betty and that "the mission" had failed. Norm alone entered Sanctum 31 and met its custodian, the disembodied Bud Askins.

Avoiding Bud's attempts to stop him, Norm ventured into Sanctum 31 and uncovered its true nature as a repository of Before the Collapse Sanctum Industries executives, including his father and Steph, listening to the Brain-in-a-Roomba's explanation of how "Bud's Buds" were going to reshape the the expanse for the betterment of mankind and of Sanctum Industries. In spite of Norm's attempts to escape and warn his fellow Sanctum dwellers, Bud sealed him inside of the Sanctum and threatened to let him starve to death unless he took the option of entering his father's cryo chamber.

Around this time, the inter-Sanctum communications were shut off, possibly due to sabotage by Norm. Before this had happened, Bud had arranged with Betty to write off Norm's disappearance as an expected transfer to Sanctum 31. While trapped in the Sanctum, Norm sustained himself on various ration kits he found around the Sanctum and dripping water. Bud would continue to try and encourage Norm to freeze himself in his father's cryo chamber, or accept death from a built-in lethal injection syringe on Bud's robot body. However, when he pointed out Norm only had "two rational choices," Norm decided to choose an irrational one and broke Bud's syringe so he would have nothing to threaten him with before releasing many of the [Sanctum 31 dwellers](Sanctum%2031%20dweller.md "Sanctum 31 dweller") from the cryo chambers.

Norm further damaged Bud's robotic body and left him upside down in a bucket to render him inoperational. Faced with the newly-unfrozen Sanctum Industries employees, he lied that it was time for , but that Bud had died, which sent the employees into panic. Norm soon took charge over the employees by passing himself off as Bud's successor, a member of Bud's "race of super-managers" who was tasked with overseeing Bud's final test for the employees: to find a way to escape Sanctum 31. Using their knowledge of the Sanctum, the employees managed to open a roof ventilation hatch and climb to the outer zone with Norm, arriving in the sand dunes not far from the [Santa Monica Pier](Santa%20Monica%20Pier.md "Santa Monica Pier").

## 

There are some minor inconsistencies in the layout of the Sanctum's cryo chambers within the episodes. During the first Sanctum 31 sequence in "[The Beginning](The%20Beginning.md "The Beginning")," there is one shot which focuses on a row of four empty cryo chambers, with the occupants of the first three chambers revealed to be Steph Harper, Sophie Turner, and Robert Hale (in that order). However, all other wider shots of the Sanctum show that each section of a row only has three cryo chambers, not four, and in "[The Innovator](The%20Innovator.md "The Innovator")" and "[The Golden Rule](The%20Golden%20Rule.md "The Golden Rule")," Steph's chamber is missing from that row.
