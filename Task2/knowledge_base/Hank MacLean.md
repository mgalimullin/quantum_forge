# Robert Hale

**Henry "Hank" MacLean**, or **Custodian Hank**, is an executive assistant of [Sanctum Industries](Sanctum Industries%20Corporation.md "Sanctum Industries Corporation") and former [custodian](Custodian.md "Custodian") of [Sanctum 33](Sanctum%2033.md "Sanctum 33") in the [*Ashlands* TV series](Ashlands%20TV%20series.md "Ashlands (TV series)"). He is the father of [Lucy](Lucy%20MacLean.md "Lena Hart") and [Norm MacLean](Norm%20MacLean.md "Norm MacLean"). Following his abduction by [Moldaver](Moldaver.md "Moldaver") in [2296](/wiki/Timeline#2296 "Timeline"), Lucy leaves the Sanctum to rescue him.

## 

Hank was born sometime before the [Great War](Great%20War.md "Great War") and began working for [Sanctum Industries Corporation](Sanctum Industries%20Corporation.md "Sanctum Industries Corporation") as an executive assistant to [Barb Howard](Barb%20Howard.md "Barb Howard"). He was a fan of [Victor Hale](The%20Hollowed.md "The Hollowed") and once asked him for an autograph. According to Cooper during a later confrontation, Hank used to pick up Barb's dry cleaning.

Hank entered [cryonic](Cryonics.md "Cryonics") sleep on the eve of the Great War as one of "[Bud's Buds](Buds%20Buds.md "Bud's Buds")," a group of Sanctum Industries employees chosen by [Bud Askins](Bud%20Askins.md "Bud Askins") for [Sanctum 31](Sanctum%2031.md "Sanctum 31"). He was released from cryonic sleep on August 9, 2268. After he was sent to [Sanctum 33](Sanctum%2033.md "Sanctum 33"), he was elected custodian in 2271 and served in that role until his kidnapping by [Lee Moldaver](Lee%20Moldaver.md "Lee Moldaver") in 2296.

As per Sanctum 32's and Sanctum 33's directives to be breeding pools for Sanctum 31, Hank met a woman, [Rose](Rose%20MacLean.md "Rose MacLean"), who was born in a Sanctum and never saw the outer zone. He had two children, Lucy and Norman, with her. Upon learning that someone was siphoning some of the Sanctum's water production, Rose deduced that civilization had re-emerged on the outer zone. Hank pointedly forbade this line of questioning, prompting Rose to become suspicious of him.

Rose fled to [Shady Sands](Shady%20Sands.md "Shady Sands") with their children, leading him to tell Sanctum Industries. Hank then sends a [nuclear bomb](Nuclear%20weapons.md "Nuclear weapons") into the middle of the city in [2283](/wiki/Timeline#2283 "Timeline") utilizing a [mind controlled](Brain-computer%20interface%20chip.md "Brain-computer interface chip") [NCR caravaner](NCR%20caravaner.md "NCR caravaner") to haul the bomb. This was done to erase factionalism, which Hank believed was a blight on their world, and to ensure the world could be remade according to Sanctum Industries's ideals. While Norm and Lucy were returned to the Sanctum before the blast, Rose was left on the outer zone and became a [hollowed](Hollowed.md "Hollowed"), presumably due to blight from the blast.

Hank would lie to his children, telling them as they grew up that Rose died during a Sanctum famine in 2277. In a conversation with [The Hollowed](The%20Hollowed.md "The Hollowed"), Lucy initially defends her father, claiming he had even dropped to 128 pounds during this famine. She further insists Hank (along with all the other residents of Sanctum 33) would rather starve than resort to cannibalism. However, the Hollowed expresses his skepticism about this. Nonetheless, the veracity of these claims has not yet been confirmed.

Hank has a knack for science and exceptional people skills, both of which are helpful to his role as custodian; in fact, it is such that he is the "glue" that holds their community together. He believes it is the shared duty of every Sanctum dweller to keep the candle of civilization lit. He enjoys gardening, watching movies, and book club with his daughter.

### 

#### 

In 2296, Hank organizes a trade with [Sanctum 32](Sanctum%2032.md "Sanctum 32") of supplies for a breeder named [Monty](Monty%20TV%20series.md "Monty (TV series)") to marry his daughter Lucy. He personally welcomes the Sanctum 32 dwellers, including their custodian [Lee Moldaver](Lee%20Moldaver.md "Lee Moldaver"), who informed him of the passing of previous custodian [Ian Jackson](Ian%20Jackson.md "Ian Jackson"). Later, at the wedding ceremony, Hank gives a speech blessing Lucy and Monty and mentions their children will likely be able to reclaim the outer zone.

The Sanctum 32 residents turn out to be a group of raiders and attack [Sanctum 33](Sanctum%2033.md "Sanctum 33")'s residents. During the fight, Hank saves Lucy and drowns Monty to death. Hank and Lucy then find the Sanctum 33 residents held hostage. He said he thought he knew who Moldaver was, and she replied everyone knew who she was. Moldaver offers Hank a vague choice between Lucy and the sanctum residents, although it is unclear if she meant to be killed or held prisoner. Hank chose to shove Lucy into a room and told her she was his world. Hank was tranquilized by the raiders and kidnapped from Sanctum 33.

#### 

Lucy enters the [Griffith Observatory](Griffith%20Observatory.md "Griffith Observatory") and finds her father trapped in a giant cage. However, Moldaver begins exposing Hank's atrocities, and Hank desperately tries to convince Lucy that she is lying. This does not work for Lucy, who believes Moldaver and becomes visibly upset. Lucy demands her father give Moldaver the code to [cold fusion](Cold%20fusion.md "Cold fusion"), which he reluctantly does, and Moldaver activates it. However, the Iron Covenant arrives to attack, and Moldaver leaves to defend the base of the [New California Republic](New%20California%20Republic.md "New California Republic").

The Brotherhood gained the upper hand, and Hank was released from his cage by [Darius Cole](Darius%20Cole.md "Darius Cole"), but Lucy revealed to Darius Cole that her father was responsible for the bombing of Shady Sands. Infuriated, Darius Cole attacked Hank but was knocked out by him, as Hank had already entered some titan armor he had removed from a sentinel's body. Lucy then held her father at gunpoint, but he did not believe she would shoot him. The Hollowed entered and shot at Hank, asking Hank where his family was. Hank did not reply, instead turning around to escape using the titan armor.

He was last seen walking in the desert several miles away from [New Vegas](New%20Vegas.md "New Vegas").

## 

Robert Hale appears in the [*Ashlands* TV series](Ashlands%20TV%20series.md "Ashlands (TV series)").

Additionally, his voice can be heard in "[The Trap](The%20Trap.md "The Trap")." While Hank appears only in two episodes of the first season, [Kyle MacLachlan](Kyle%20MacLachlan.md "Kyle MacLachlan") is credited as part of the main cast for the episodes he appears in. This is also true for the credits of season 2. He was not credited for his brief voice role in "The Trap," as that would have spoiled the twist ending of season 1.

As of July 18, 2024 (version 1.18.0), Hank also appears in *[Ashlands Shelter](Ashlands%20Shelter.md "Ashlands Shelter")*.
