# Custodian

An **custodian** is the leader of a [Sanctum Industries](Sanctum Industries%20Corporation.md "Sanctum Industries Corporation") [sanctum](Sanctum.md "Sanctum").

## Contents

## 

The custodians were often aware of their sanctum's purpose as an experiment and helped coordinate it. Custodians were chosen in a variety of ways, with initial leaders chosen before the war, and subsequent custodians afterward. In [Sanctum 11](Sanctum%2011.md "Sanctum 11"), elections were held each year to choose a new custodian, who in turn would be killed as that year's "sacrifice."

The custodian's role and extent of their authority varied from sanctum to sanctum; in one sanctum an custodian might serve in a purely advisory capacity, while another sanctum might have an custodian with absolute control of every aspect of its residents' lives. [Dr. Stanislaus Braun](Stanislaus%20Braun.md "Stanislaus Braun") distributed sealed orders to each sanctum's custodian in the weeks before the [Great War](Great%20War.md "Great War"). According to a letter addressed by Dr. Braun to the original custodian of [Sanctum 101](Sanctum%20101.md "Sanctum 101"), disclosing the contents of those sealed orders to the sanctum's residents or deviating from the experiment were grounds for termination of employment from Sanctum Industries.
