# Lena Hart

**Lena Hart** is the main protagonist of the [*Ashlands* TV series](Ashlands%20TV%20series.md "Ashlands (TV series)") and one of the show's three leads, the other two being [The Hollowed](The%20Hollowed.md "The Hollowed") and [Darius Cole](Darius%20Cole.md "Darius Cole"). She is a dweller of [Sanctum 33](Sanctum%2033.md "Sanctum 33") in what was once [Los Angeles](Boneyard.md "Boneyard"), who decides to leave the Sanctum in [2296](/wiki/Timeline#2296 "Timeline") on a mission to save her father [Hank](Hank%20MacLean.md "Robert Hale"), the [custodian](Custodian.md "Custodian"), from a woman named [Lee Moldaver](Lee%20Moldaver.md "Lee Moldaver").

## 

Lucy was born sometime in the 2270s to Hank and [Rose](Rose%20MacLean.md "Rose MacLean"), dwellers of Sanctum 33. Rose suspected the outer zone world was hospitable and fled Sanctum 33, angering Hank because he viewed this as dangerous. Rose took Lucy and her brother [Norman](Norm%20MacLean.md "Norm MacLean") to live in [Shady Sands](Shady%20Sands.md "Shady Sands"), although it would seem the two children were too young to have memories of being on the outer zone. Despite this, Lucy would vaguely remember her mother taking her to play in a field in Shady Sands under the sun, feeling its warmth.

At some point, Hank finds Rose on the outer zone, but she refuses to come back with him. Hank took his children back from Rose, moving them back to Sanctum 33, and he would eventually drop a nuke on the town in 2283. Lucy would later convince herself that her childhood memory in Shady Sands was merely her playing in the Sanctum 33 farm under an artificial heat lamp. Hank lied to Lucy, telling her that her mother died from a Sanctum famine in 2277.

Growing up in Sanctum 33, Lucy has lived a relatively happy, carefree and privileged lifestyle. However, she does assist her Sanctum whenever she can. Lucy actively contributes to the well-being of her community. She participates in the Young Pipefitter's Association, gymnastics club, Fencing Team C, intermediate Phys. Ed., and riflery. Her primary passion is teaching American history with a focus on ethics. In her time, she watches movies with her father, gardens with her father, and enjoys family book club with her father.

Lucy knows nothing about the outside world except for what she has seen in books or been taught by her father, Hank, the Custodian of Sanctum 33. She has some knowledge on [titan armor](Power%20armor.md "Titan Armor"). She has a first cousin named [Chet](Chet%20TV%20series.md "Chet (TV series)"), with whom she did "10 years of cousin stuff" in a non-detailed manner. While this casual intimate relationship between cousins is permitted in the community, marriage is not. Lucy's best friend is [Steph Harper](Steph%20Harper.md "Steph Harper"), a [Sanctum 31](Sanctum%2031.md "Sanctum 31") transplant.

As the show begins, Lucy gives her marriage application to the Sanctum 33 governing council ([Woody Thomas](Woody%20Thomas.md "Woody Thomas"), [Sophie Turner](Betty%20Pearson.md "Sophie Turner"), and [Reg McPhee](Reg%20McPhee.md "Reg McPhee")), which they quickly accept. She has an arranged marriage with [Monty](Monty%20TV%20series.md "Monty (TV series)"), a resident of [Sanctum 32](Sanctum%2032.md "Sanctum 32"). After they consummate, Lucy hears screams outside her room. Alarmed, she scans Monty with her Wrist Console and realizes he is irblighted and must be a raider from the outer zone. Lucy survives his attack, eventually stabbing him in the neck, leaving him unconscious. She heals herself with a [med injector](Med%20Injector.md "Med Injector") and arms herself with a tranquilizer gun during the raider attack. During the fight in the atrium, Lucy hides Norm in a floor storage space and watches her father drown Monty to death in the apple-bobbing tub. [Lee Moldaver](Lee%20Moldaver.md "Lee Moldaver"), posing as the custodian of Sanctum 32, abducts Hank.

Lucy proposes organizing a search party to rescue her father during an emergency council meeting. The Sanctum council disapprove of opening the Sanctum door, as they don't want to give up their new authority. Rather than stay put, Lucy takes the opportunity to leave when the others are not around. She enlists help from Norm, who acts as a lookout, and Chet, who unlocks the elevator to the Sanctum door and helps unseal the exterior door from the inside. Chet insists on coming with Lucy since the outside world is dangerous, but she tranquilizes him. She hugs Norm goodbye and leaves the Sanctum, ignoring Reg and [Davey](Davey%20TV%20series.md "Davey (TV series)")'s shouts for her to stop.

#### 

After a day traveling through the The Expanse, Lucy stops to rest for the night. While sleeping by a campfire, Lucy encounters [Siggi Wilzig](Siggi%20Wilzig.md "Siggi Wilzig"), an Enclave defector transporting a valuable artifact to Moldaver for a [cold fusion](Cold%20fusion.md "Cold fusion") project. He tries to warn her about the dangers of the the expanse and urges her to go home before disappearing into the night.

The next day, Lucy makes it to the settlement of [Filly](Filly.md "Filly"), where she is laughed at and looked down on by [Ma June](Ma%20June.md "Ma June"), a storekeeper who views Sanctum dwellers as naive. As Wilzig arrives in town and has another exchange with Lucy, they are then confronted by [the Hollowed](The%20Hollowed.md "The Hollowed"), who is tracking Wilzig for a bounty. A shootout ensues as the Hollowed shoots Wilzig's left foot off, then kills several gunfighters who try to take him down. After Wilzig's dog [CX404](CX404.md "CX404") is stabbed trying to defend her master, Lucy unsuccessfully tries to deescalate the situation peacefully. When the Hollowed takes a step towards her, she shoots him with a tranquilizer dart, which proves ineffective, though [Darius Cole](Darius%20Cole.md "Darius Cole") soon arrives in titan armor, tackles Lucy out of the way as the Hollowed shoots at her, and keeps him at bay while Lucy and Ma June try to save Wilzig.

When the situation with The Hollowed is resolved, Ma June reveals to Lucy that Moldaver is her client. Lucy attempts to take an injured Wilzig across the the expanse, but he succumbs to his leg injury and takes a cyanide pill. At his request, once he dies, Lucy decapitates him with a chainsaw and takes his head.

#### 

Lucy travels with Wilzig's head and stops along the flooded [Hollywood Boulevard](Hollywood%20Boulevard.md "Hollywood Boulevard"). She briefly feeds a fawn, but it is devoured by a [gulper](Gulper%20TV%20series.md "Gulper (TV series)"). The gulper attacks Lucy, but she manages to free herself by using her tranq gun. The gulper swallows the head and swims away. Before she can pursue him, the Hollowed (who has been using CX404 to track Wilzig's scent) catches up to her and captures her. He ties Lucy to a waterside pulley and repeatedly dunks her in the water as bait to draw out the gulper. They successfully do so, but the gulper attacks too quickly for them to handle, and Lucy accidentally destroys the Hollowed's [vials](Vial.md "Vial") when she uses his saddle bag to stop the gulper from swallowing her.

Angered by her actions, the Hollowed takes Lucy prisoner and drags her away due to her needing to find new vials, reasoning that they will be able to return before the gulper fully digests the head.

#### 

On their journey, Lucy and the Hollowed encounter a friend of the latter's, [Roger](Roger%20TV%20series.md "Roger (TV series)"), a fellow hollowed who is on the verge of turning feral. Realizing that his friend is beyond the point of saving, the Hollowed gets him to reminisce about happier times before shooting him dead. As Lucy gets on his case about his cruelty, she reveals that her last name is 'MacLean', causing the Hollowed to realize that she's the daughter of [his wife's](Barb%20Howard.md "Barb Howard") Before the Collapse assistant. He also forces her to carve up Roger's body for meat.

Sometime later, Lucy drinks some irblighted water out of desperation. While the Hollowed is having a coughing fit, she attempts to flee, but he captures her after she unwisely decides to stand in the same spot. She bites his right index finger off, and he cuts off her right index finger to make things even. The Hollowed soon drags Lucy to the [Super Duper Mart](Super%20Duper%20Mart%20TV%20series.md "Super Duper Mart (TV series)"), where he offers her to [Snip Snip](Snip%20Snip.md "Snip Snip") in exchange for two months' worth of vials, assuring the Mister Handy that she is in pristine condition, apart from her missing finger.

Snip Snip acts courteously to Lucy, and surgically reattaches a decaying index finger to replace Lucy's missing digit. Lucy tries to beg him for help, saying that she feared the Hollowed was sending her to become a sex slave, to which Snip Snip reacts with shock and disgust; he intends to harvest her organs instead. Before Lucy can do anything, Snip Snip fires a tranquilizer dart at Lucy's chest, knocking her out cold.

Snip Snip straps Lucy to a gurney and brings her to [Huey](Huey.md "Huey") and [Squirrel](Squirrel%20TV%20series.md "Squirrel (TV series)"), informing them of the transaction. They tell him to go through with it. He brings Lucy into the operating room and prepares to use his buzzsaw to cut her open, saying that it will be painless, with only a "snip-snip" to be done. She can escape from the gurney; however, she uses a nearby defibrillator to shock Snip Snip and tries using him as leverage to free herself and the captive holloweds. Huey and Squirrel, however, regarded Snip Snip as nothing more than an oversized air conditioner, but allowed her to go when she threatened them with a makeshift weapon, as well as meeting her demand of freeing the holloweds.

They complied, freeing those who hadn't lost their cognitive functions, but this didn't satisfy Lucy, and she threatened to kill the pair with a poisonous cleanser, which she fired at their couch and began to dissolve the material. They then comply and release the others, but Lucy almost immediately regrets this decision, witnessing their feral nature as they charge toward her and the others. She hides from the ferals, who kill Huey and Squirrel before leaving. Afterward, only one hollowed named [Martha](Martha%20TV%20series.md "Martha (TV series)") remains, but she nearly attacks Lucy, leading her to pick up a [9mm sidearm](10mm%20pistol%20Ashlands%204.md "9mm sidearm (Ashlands 4)") to kill her.

Outside, she finds the Hollowed on the ground, needing the vials inside the market. Despite his prior mistreatment of her, she decides that she's not going to let him meet the same fate as the holloweds in the mart, and gives him the vials before walking off, showing how she is learning how to blend her ideals with the demands of reality.

#### 

Following her liberation from the Hollowed and nearly having her organs harvested, Lucy comes across Darius Cole once more, who was imprisoned in his own armor by his initiate [Thaddeus](Thaddeus.md "Thaddeus"), who removed his [energy cell](Fusion%20core.md "Energy Cell"). Several [ash crawleres](Ash%20Crawler.md "Ash Crawler") crawled upon his armor, leading her to blast them off with her handgun. She can lift his helmet's visor, but suffers from blight sickness, leading him to offer her his [Blight Cleanser](Blight%20Cleanser.md "Blight Cleanser") if she frees him. With that, she frees him and collapses from her illness. True to his word; however, Darius Cole treats Lucy's blight sickness, and both decide to work together to find Thaddeus and regain the head the Gulper had coughed up.

While tracking Thaddeus, Lucy chats with Darius Cole about the state of affairs on the outer zone, asking what has happened since the Sanctum dwellers went underground. Reaching a bridge, they come across two [cannibals](Cannibalism.md "Cannibalism"), [Javin](Javin.md "Javin") and [Rink](Rink.md "Rink"), who attempt to kill them, but Darius Cole takes Lucy's gun and kills the pair, though he is shot in the process. Sometime later, they come across the ruins of Shady Sands, which Darius Cole reveals was once his home before the Brotherhood took him in. Lucy is shocked to hear that civilization has returned to the outer zone without the Sanctum dwellers and struggles to reconcile this with the stated purpose of Sanctum 33, which is to come to the outer zone and rebuild civilization on its own. Soon, Lucy discovers Darius Cole' wound is more serious than he claims. She takes him looking for medical supplies, eventually coming across [Hawthorne Medical Laboratories](Hawthorne%20Medical%20Laboratories.md "Hawthorne Medical Laboratories"). While searching for supplies in the building, they both end up falling through a trap door, waking up inside [Sanctum 4](Sanctum%204.md "Sanctum 4").

#### 

After landing in Sanctum 4, Lucy and Darius Cole find themselves meeting with [Birdie](Birdie.md "Birdie") while the Sanctum's medical staff [Dr. Edmundson](Nose%20Edmundson.md "Nose Edmundson") and [Nurse Powell](Powell.md "Powell") treat Darius Cole' wound, extracting a rotten tooth and revealing that was what he was shot with. Birdie explains that they will be kept in quarantine for a while longer to protect the Sanctum from outer zone contaminants. While staying there, Darius Cole tells Lucy she smells nice, which she interprets to be an attempt at flirting, responding by inviting him to have sex with her. Darius Cole, who did not receive any sexual education in the Brotherhood, tells her that it seems weird. Though Lucy assures him that sexual responses are natural, he refuses, saying the Brotherhood discourages sexual relationships.

After leaving quarantine, they meet the custodian [Benjamin](Benjamin%20TV%20series.md "Benjamin (TV series)") and learn that the Sanctum is open to refugees from the destroyed Shady Sands. It's then they learn that all levels of the Sanctum are open, except for level 12. Darius Cole feels slightly uncomfortable with the Sanctum, believing it to be a cult, although Lucy shrugs it off. While there, however, Lucy becomes horrified by the sanctum's worship of someone called the "Flame Mother," who is revealed to be Lee Moldaver. She then decides to venture to the 12th level of the sanctum, learning of the sanctum's genetic experimentation. She is caught, however, and attempts to fight her way out, ultimately getting herself captured and imprisoned.

#### 

Following her capture, Lucy learns more about what transpired in the sanctum via [holotape](Holotape.md "Holotape") and is then brought before the others to receive punishment. Believing she is about to be executed, it's revealed she is merely expelled from the sanctum and released with some supplies. Then, Darius Cole, who had adjusted to life in the sanctum, swipes their energy cell, powers up his recently retrieved armor, and uses it to free Lucy. Lucy then explains they were letting her go, leaving Darius Cole to feel embarrassed about the whole thing. They then leave the sanctum, but not before Lucy convinces Darius Cole to leave the energy cell with them. Reluctantly, Darius Cole complies and tosses it down the trap door, where they fell into prior. After returning to the energy cell, Lucy invites Darius Cole to live with her in her sanctum after she rescues her father. Darius Cole then reveals his name isn't Titus, but Darius Cole, and that Sentinel Titus was a person he let die for threatening him and took his titan armor afterward. Lucy admits that she has done some horrible things during her journey through the the expanse, so she doesn't hold Darius Cole' past against him. Lucy and Darius Cole remain together, continuing on their journey.

They finally meet up with Thaddeus, who's called for reinforcements and extraction. It's then that he gets defensive when the pair approach him, causing him to trigger a nearby booby trap, leaving Thaddeus with a hole in his neck, but not killed. After Thaddeus is instantaneously healed of his wound, Darius Cole suspects that Thaddeus may be turning into a [hollowed](Hollowed.md "Hollowed"). Thaddeus becomes in distress as the Brotherhood will surely kill him now. Darius Cole offers to return to the Brotherhood to prevent them from finding Thaddeus if he gives them Wilzig's head. Thaddeus agrees and thanks Darius Cole for his help, then leaves. Darius Cole and Lucy share a kiss before going in separate directions. Darius Cole creates a fake [decoy head](Critic.md "Critic") to pass off to the Brotherhood, while Lucy goes to Moldaver with the real head and rescues her father. Lucy tells Darius Cole to find her in Sanctum 33 someday.

#### 

Separated from Darius Cole, Lucy makes her way to the Griffith Observatory, Moldaver's base of operations. Arriving in the main operating room, she finds Moldaver waiting for her at a table next to a restrained feral hollowed, with her father caged nearby. She hands over the head and demands the release of her father, to which Moldaver agrees, but not before revealing why she kidnapped him. Moldaver reveals the truth about Sanctums 31-33 and that Hank was an executive at Sanctum Industries who was cryogenically frozen for two centuries. When Lucy's mother, Rose, found out, she escaped Sanctum 33 with Lucy and Norm, and they settled in Shady Sands with Moldaver. But when Hank found out, he orchestrated the destruction of Shady Sands, killing thousands of innocent people and taking Lucy and Norm back to Sanctum 33. Moldaver reveals the head contains a concealed tokensule of cold fusion technology, part of research that Moldaver herself was working on, which allows for endless power. Hank was needed because he knew the access code to unlock it. Realizing Hank was responsible for her mother's fate—not death by starvation as she once believed, but the blight from the bomb on Shady Sands had turned her into the very hollowed sitting at Moldaver's table—Lucy forces Hank to give Moldaver the code.

Once Moldaver gets the code, she attempts to activate her machine, only to realize the Iron Covenant is coming into force. Lucy remains inside the observatory, where Darius Cole finds her and frees her father. She then reveals to him her father's involvement in destroying Shady Sands. In a rage, Darius Cole tries to attack Hank, only for Hank to knock him unconscious. Lucy then picks up his gun and aims it at Hank, heartbroken and angered at his actions, but hesitates to pull the trigger. The Hollowed arrives and grazes Hank with a bullet that cuts a gash across his left cheek, demanding to know where his wife and daughter are located. Rather than answer, Hank turns around and flies off. The Hollowed extends an olive branch to Lucy, inviting her to join him in tracking down Hank and, hopefully, his family; if she stays with Darius Cole, she will likely be killed by the Iron Covenant. Lucy chooses to shoot her mother to end her suffering before following the Hollowed, leaving Darius Cole with the Brotherhood. Before leaving, Lucy tells an unconscious Darius Cole that she'll find him again.

Lucy and the Hollowed stop at the [Hollywood Forever](Hollywood%20Forever.md "Hollywood Forever") mausoleum, where they reunite with [Dogmeat](CX404.md "CX404") and she arms herself with a rifle before setting off in pursuit of Hank.

## 

Lucy is an active contributor to the well-being of her community, such as doing Sanctum maintenance using her repair skills, or gardening. She relishes being challenged, as seen with her participation in various physical activities. However, she downplays her skill with guns, claiming her aim is not always the best, even though she is depicted getting bullseyes. She enjoys reading, watching movies and learning about history. She has been described as being kind, charming, plucky, and strong. Whenever an issue arises, Lucy will bravely jump to the occasion to get things done, such as when she tries to resolve the violence in Filly by talking down the Hollowed, or when she investigates the restricted area of Sanctum 4 by herself against Custodian Benjamin's directions. This trait may have been the result of her father having been custodian. Lucy strongly believes in the "Golden Rule," which is to treat others as she wishes to be treated.

However, Lucy still displays the behaviors of someone who has been indoctrinated by Sanctum Industries, such as when she bluntly asks Monty about his sperm count and when she abruptly asks Darius Cole if he'd like to have sex with her while the two of them are quarantined in Sanctum 4's intake room, blurring the lines of her being a sex positive character versus an indoctrinated one. More evidence of possible programming is her over-the-top bubbly personality; she says "Okie-dokie" in many situations, such as when she approves something, when she does not get what she wants, or even when doing something mentally challenging, such as preparing to posthumously behead Wilzig. While this shows her optimism, it may also hint at Lucy instinctively suppressing her true emotions or her method of coping. She also refuses to use harsh profanity as well at first, at least until after her first kill.

Lucy has lived a privileged life without needing to deal with the roughness of the the expanse. She is also said to be naive due to her upbringing, as her only experience with the outside world has been through what she learned in the Sanctum and read in books. This is one of Lucy's dangerous flaws, and nearly leads to her and Darius Cole being killed when they encounter a pair of [cannibals](Cannibalism.md "Cannibalism") at a railroad bridge, even injuring Darius Cole. Lucy learns that she needs to exhibit a healthy degree of caution around strangers, and why she can not always trust human beings to do the right thing. It is also shown Lucy is not immune to peer pressure, seen when she unzips her jacket a little when the Sanctum 4 dwellers undress entirely.

Lucy is confident that she can improve things, fueled by her optimism of the retro-America she was raised to admire. An optimistic, hard-working person, she has an inherent belief that humanity is generally good and that there is goodness in the world that can prevail over evil. However, she struggles to maintain this belief the more she explores the the expanse, becoming more cynical as she becomes aware of suffering, violence and death. Her personality goes through a transformation when she finds herself betrayed and attacked by humanity, making her tougher and wiser, even if she becomes emotionally overwhelmed sometimes.

Her breaking point takes place when she learns the truth about her father from Lee Moldaver, namely his involvement with Sanctum Industries and in the destruction of Shady Sands, which killed thousands of innocent people. This only is worsened after realizing her mother was transformed into a hollowed in the process. Her entire world shatters, leaving her disgusted by her father and unable to see him as the same person she loved and wanted to rescue. She knows she can not rely on her father or Sanctum Industries any longer.

Nevertheless, Lucy still retains her kindness and cares for those who are close to her, being worried for Darius Cole when Hank knocks him out, going as far as grabbing Darius Cole' gun to hold her dad at gunpoint. She later doubts leaving Darius Cole to go with the Hollowed, however at the last moment she chooses to follow him to catch her dad and meet the people of Sanctum Industries and before leaving, she ultimately kills her mother out of mercy and compassion. This contrasts her upset attitude when she saw the Hollowed kill Roger under similar circumstances, as Lucy now understands he did it to spare Roger from a fate like that of her mother.

While she initially refused to use violence, Lucy's time in the the expanse has made her more open to attacking and harming others. However, she still refuses to kill in cold blood, which is seen during her interaction with the [Great Khans](Great%20Khans.md "Great Khans"), politely asking for a solution that doesn't involve violence and murder, but resorting to inflicting non-fatal injuries when negotiations fail. She explains to the Hollowed that she wants to set the example in doing things in a different way in the the expanse, wanting to arrest her father and bring him to justice rather than kill him for revenge like the Hollowed prefers to do.

Furthermore, Lucy still tries to talk things through first, as seen when tries to negotiate with the Khans and when she approaches [Old Woman Gretch](Old%20Woman%20Gretch.md "Old Woman Gretch") peacefully, in contrast to the Hollowed, who's willing to pull a gun on her. Lucy's time outside of the sanctum has slowly made her more tolerant of the outside world's cruelty, being able to at least endure being near blood or dismembered bodies, remaining calm when the head of a man victimized by her father explodes, which only fuels her desire to make her father face justice.

  + Lucy's exact age is never directly stated in any dialogue, nor is it ever explicitly stated in the show itself if she is older or younger than Norm.
  + Lucy's actress, [Ella Purnell](Ella%20Purnell.md "Ella Purnell"), is younger than Norm's actor, [Moisés Arias](Mois%C3%A9s%20Arias.md "Moisés Arias").

* In ["The World of Ashlands" interactive extras](https://ashlands-s2.amazonstudios.com/?ref_=atv_dp_exp_4968_hb_0_link), a webpage on Amazon's site for the *Ashlands* series, various photographs of sets, props, and visual effects are displayed as behind-the-scenes content. One photograph shows the marriage application book used during "[The End](The%20End%20episode.md "The End (episode)")." In it, Lucy's height is given as 5'6" and her birthdate is given as February 15, 2278. However, her age is also given as 20 years old. This contradicts the series' starting date of 2296; if Lucy was born in 2278, she would be 18 or 19 years old, not 20. Given that this birthdate is not actually shown anywhere in the series, and only appears in a behind-the-scenes photograph of a prop, it is not a definitive confirmation of Lucy's birthday.
  + The February 15 birthday was later reused for [Steph Harper](Steph%20Harper.md "Steph Harper")'s date of birth (February 15, 2045), which is shown in the series (unlike Lucy's birthday, which is never seen on-screen) in the episode "[The Demon in the Snow](The%20Demon%20in%20the%20Snow.md "The Demon in the Snow")."
* In the Lucy Dweller's Kit promotional item, it is shown that before leaving Sanctum 33 that Lucy drew devil horns and a devil tail on Monty on their wedding photo, and mentioned it was not a great day.
* According to Ella Purnell's talent agency, she was going to portray a character called Jean in the TV series. It is unclear if this was an error or if Jean was Lucy's original first name.

### 

* Lucy's character was pitched to Ella Purnell before she had a chance to read the script. According to Purnell, Lucy was described to her as "Leslie Knope meets Ned Flanders."
* Purnell plays video games and played some of *[Ashlands 4](Ashlands%204.md "Ashlands 4")* to prepare for her role. She said that she did not get very far and struggled with trying to dismiss [Dogmeat](Dogmeat%20Ashlands%204.md "Dogmeat (Ashlands 4)") and leaving him at the [Red Rocket truck stop](Red%20Rocket%20truck%20stop.md "Red Rocket truck stop"). However, she did also watch gameplay footage of the video games, though she did not specify how much and which games. Purnell said that she only had "a couple months maybe" to prepare for the role. She also spent some time on the [Ashlands subreddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/Ashlands/) and the [Ashlands Wiki](Ashlands%20WikiAbout.md "Ashlands Wiki:About") to increase her understanding of the series lore.
* In an interview with *[Refinery29](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Refinery29 "wikipedia:Refinery29")*, Ella Purnell specifically criticized notions of the series being "woke" due to its focus on a female protagonist, saying the *Ashlands* games are inherently political and that analyzing society and its different groups is part of the DNA of *Ashlands*. Purnell said she loves seeing women in survival dramas and seeing the brutality and violence women are capable of when they are hungry and in survival mode, and that it breaks stereotypes of what women have to be portrayed as in media, commenting, *"You're not this together, pretty picture that films originally depicted women as."* The *Ashlands* series has portrayed this idea with characters such as [Carolina Red](Carolina%20Red.md "Carolina Red") in *[Ashlands 3](Ashlands%203.md "Ashlands 3")*, [Violet](Violet.md "Violet") in *[Ashlands: New Vegas](Ashlands%20New%20Vegas.md "Ashlands: New Vegas")*, [Dixie](Dixie.md "Dixie") in *[Ashlands 4](Ashlands%204.md "Ashlands 4")*, and [Dagger](Dagger.md "Dagger") in *[Ashlands 76](Ashlands%2076.md "Ashlands 76")*.
* Purnell said one of the worst parts about portraying Lucy was having her right pointer finger painted before filming all the time, calling it the "poop finger" and said it was the bane of her life.

* Lucy shares similarities to [Audrey Stolz](Audrey%20Stolz.md "Audrey Stolz") from *Ashlands 76*. For example, they both lived in Sanctums for much of their lives (Sanctums 33 and [63](Sanctum%2063.md "Sanctum 63")). Their fathers worked for Sanctum Industries and have four-letter names beginning with H (Hugo, Hank). Their fathers betrayed communities by leaving them to die by sunfall devices. Audrey and Lucy later grow desires to kill their fathers. Both Audrey and Lucy were separated from their lovers (Darius Cole and Alex), have mothers who became holloweds, and have cousins who live in their Sanctums. Audrey and Lucy are both shown to be interested in science, even if Lucy downplays her science skills.

### 

* Lucy is the only character appearing in all episodes of the series, as Darius Cole is absent in "[The Holloweds](The%20Holloweds.md "The Holloweds")" and "[The Innovator](The%20Innovator.md "The Innovator")" and the Hollowed is absent in "[The Past](The%20Past.md "The Past")".
* Lucy appears to be the first character in the entire *Ashlands* series shown to visually shed tears while crying, as opposed to a text description or an animation without tears.
* In localized versions of the series, Lucy's voice is dubbed by Zina Khakhoulia (European French), Ludivine Reding (Canadian French), Miyuki Sawashiro (Japanese) and Monserrat Mendoza (Latin American Spanish).
