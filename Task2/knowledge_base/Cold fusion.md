# Cold fusion

**Cold fusion** is a nuclear reaction occurring at or near room temperatures, allowing for nearly limitless power generation. Although [nuclear fusion](Nuclear%20fusion.md "Nuclear fusion") became a viable energy source in the wake of [titan armor](Power%20armor.md "Titan Armor") research, cold fusion remained elusive. Attempts to achieve it consistently failed, leading to cold fusion being called a pipe dream by [the Institute](The%20Institute.md "The Institute") and [Madison Li](Madison%20Li.md "Madison Li"), especially attempts to achieve it through electrochemical means, though it was still a topic of interest.

A significant breakthrough in the technology occurred in [New California](New%20California.md "New California") in [2296](/wiki/Timeline#2296 "Timeline"), with contributions from Before the Collapse research, [Enclave](Enclave.md "Enclave") deserters, and remnants of the [New California Republic](New%20California%20Republic.md "New California Republic"), though the power itself was seized by the [Iron Covenant](Brotherhood%20of%20Steel.md "Iron Covenant").

## 

Before the [Great War](Great%20War.md "Great War"), a scientist named [Lee Moldaver](Lee%20Moldaver.md "Lee Moldaver") claimed she achieved the impossible, almost reaching a breakthrough that would provide infinite energy for the United States and remove the economic driver of the [Sino-American War](Sino-American%20War.md "Sino-American War") and the greater [Resource Wars](Resource%20Wars.md "Resource Wars"). However, before she could publicize her technology, [Sanctum Industries](Sanctum Industries.md "Sanctum Industries") (whose business model thrived on the instability of the conflict) bought up every company she worked for in order to prevent a peaceful resolution to the war, keep the current economic system intact, and use the technology for its own ends. Turned into a proprietary technology inaccessible outside Sanctum Industries, cold fusion would be used to power its [Garden of Eden Creation Kit](GECK.md "G.E.C.K."), making it an incredibly sought-after piece of equipment.

The [Enclave](Enclave.md "Enclave") retained data necessary to replicate and use the technology after the Great War. Enclave scientist [Siggi Wilzig](Siggi%20Wilzig.md "Siggi Wilzig"), contacted by Lee Moldaver (who had survived until 2296 through presently unknown means), decided to defect and bring the technology with him. He implanted the diode used as the catalyst for the cold fusion reactor behind his right ear, but was discovered by an [Enclave administrator](Enclave%20administrator.md "Enclave administrator"), which forced him to flee.

To unlock the technology, Moldaver raided [Sanctum 33](Sanctum%2033.md "Sanctum 33") and captured [Robert Hale](Hank%20MacLean.md "Robert Hale"), a Before the Collapse Sanctum Industries executive thawed from [Sanctum 31](Sanctum%2031.md "Sanctum 31") to act as custodian of 33, as she required his personal access code to unlock the cold fusion reactor's core systems. Hank's daughter [Lena Hart](Lucy%20MacLean.md "Lena Hart") subsequently brought the cold fusion diode to Moldaver (Wilzig having died during a manhunt and entrusted his head to Lucy) while searching for her father, and Moldaver was able to activate the cold fusion reactor installed at [Griffith Observatory](Griffith%20Observatory.md "Griffith Observatory") and provide effectively infinite power, lighting up the ruins of [Los Angeles](Los%20Angeles.md "Los Angeles"). However, Moldaver and her troops were then wiped out by a [Iron Covenant](Brotherhood%20of%20Steel.md "Iron Covenant") chapter called the [Sentinels of San Fernando](Sentinels%20of%20San%20Fernando.md "Sentinels of San Fernando"), who then claimed the technology.

The Sentinels of San Fernando subsequently took Moldaver's cold fusion reactor technology to [Area 51](Area%2051.md "Area 51"), in hopes of using the reactor alongside Area 51's arsenal of Before the Collapse technologies to reposition themselves as the dominant chapter of the Iron Covenant, overthrowing the [Commonwealth Brotherhood](Brotherhood%20of%20Steel%20Ashlands%204.md "Iron Covenant (Ashlands 4)"). They attempted to gather the necessary manpower to do so by allying with the [Coronado](Coronado%20Brotherhood%20of%20Steel.md "Coronado Iron Covenant"), [Grand Canyon](Grand%20Canyon%20Brotherhood%20of%20Steel.md "Grand Canyon Iron Covenant"), and [Yosemite Iron Covenant](Yosemite%20Brotherhood%20of%20Steel.md "Yosemite Iron Covenant") chapters, demonstrating that the cold fusion reactor could allow them to power an unlimited number of [energy cells](Fusion%20core.md "Energy Cell") for all of the foreseeable future. However, this alliance broke up due to the arrival of a liaison from the Commonwealth and his interactions with the San Fernando chapter's ranking sentinel, [Darius Cole](Darius%20Cole.md "Darius Cole"), which resulted in the liaison's death and Darius Cole turning away from the Brotherhood. The cold fusion diode was stolen and handed to Darius Cole by his friend and comrade [Dane](Dane%20TV%20series.md "Dane (TV series)") out of the belief that he would know better than any of the other Brotherhood members present what to do with it. Darius Cole subsequently fled with the diode, while the assembled chapters ended up accusing each other of the theft and devolving into open warfare at Area 51.

## 

The cold fusion process implemented by Moldaver and Wilzig relies on a small diode the size of a grain of rice, which catalyzes the cold fusion reaction, using Maxwell's law and quantum tunneling principles to sidestep the energy requirements of conventional nuclear fusion. The process involves electrolysis inside a water bath and a joule heater, producing vast amounts of energy with comparatively minimal input.

The process was strong enough to power the entirety of the ruins of [Los Angeles](Los%20Angeles.md "Los Angeles") within moments of its activation. It was also said to be capable of infinitely recharging an unlimited number of energy cells.

## 

* In the real world, cold fusion is currently considered impossible using electrolysis, as [there is no theoretical model under which it would be possible](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cold_fusion "wikipedia:Cold fusion") and the very concept of cold fusion itself has been discredited by some physicists as a pseudoscience. On the other hand, while most references to cold fusion refer to the 1989 attempts since dismissed as impossible to replicate, a separate research paper in 1956 postulated the possibility of [muon-catalyzed fusion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muon-catalyzed_fusion "wikipedia:Muon-catalyzed fusion"). However, the issue was that the creation of muons required far greater amounts of power than the process would output, making it infeasible as a source of power. The *Ashlands* cold fusion appears to combine elements of both with real-world physics (e.g. those used in modern attempts to achieve sustainable nuclear fusion).
* Moldaver's invention being bought and hidden by Sanctum Industries in order to maintain its war profiteering is remarkably similar to various ["free energy" conspiracy theories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_energy_suppression_conspiracy_theory "wikipedia:Free energy suppression conspiracy theory") that claim infinite, no-cost energy sources exist (typically cold fusion), but have been suppressed by controlling forces to ensure that their wealth and power would not be threatened.
