# Gulper (TV series)

**Gulpers** are [creatures](Ashlands%20TV%20series%20creatures.md "Ashlands TV series creatures") found around [Los Angeles](Los%20Angeles.md "Los Angeles") in the [*Ashlands* TV series](Ashlands%20TV%20series.md "Ashlands (TV series)").

## Contents

## 

These creatures were created in [Sanctum 4](Sanctum%204.md "Sanctum 4") by its initial leaders, the research team under custodians and geneticists [Lloyd](Lloyd%20Hawthorne.md "Lloyd Hawthorne") and [Cassandra Hawthorne](Cassandra%20Hawthorne.md "Cassandra Hawthorne"), through the hybridization of humans with animal DNA. Shortly after the [Great War](Great%20War.md "Great War"), the gulpers would escape containment, overrunning Sanctum 4 and killing the Hawthornes. By the twenty-third century, gulpers began appearing in the local wilderness of the [Boneyard](Los%20Angeles.md "Los Angeles").

At least two specimens are seen, one having escaped containment and taken to a natural body of water. The people of the Boneyard colloquially named the creature a "gulper," possibly indicating the existence of a larger wild population. At least one other gulper, or the remains of one, remained in containment in Sanctum 4, which had subsequently fallen under the control of the researchers' former test subjects.

## 

The gulpers resemble axolotls. They were created through experiments in mutating or hybridizing humans, hoping to create a blight-resistant humanoid species. Some or all of the "gulper" specimens may have been twisted from normally developed adult humans.

On close inspection, their many fleshy, prehensile "feelers" resemble human fingers. Many of these appendages line the insides of their mouths and grab into prey. Additionally, their front limbs end in nearly human hands, and their eyes are human-like.

## 

### 

[Lucy](Lucy%20MacLean.md "Lena Hart") encounters a gulper while trying to feed a [baby deer](Deer.md "Deer") near a lake. The deer is suddenly snatched and eaten by the Gulper, which also manages to snatch [Wilzig's](Siggi%20Wilzig.md "Siggi Wilzig") head away from her. [The Hollowed](The%20Hollowed.md "The Hollowed") then shows up, takes Lucy hostage, and uses her as bait to lure the Gulper back. The Gulper nearly eats Lucy, but she is able to fend it off.

Later, [Darius Cole](Darius%20Cole.md "Darius Cole") and [Thaddeus](Thaddeus.md "Thaddeus") encounter the Gulper when they come upon the same lake. After a drawn out fight, they manage to kill the Gulper and recover the head from it.

### 

Lucy encounters a gulper in a specimen tank on the 12th floor of [Sanctum 4](Sanctum%204.md "Sanctum 4"). She also watches a [holotape](Holotape.md "Holotape") of a woman giving birth to a school of baby Gulpers which immediately turn on her and eat her alive.

### 

It is revealed in a holotape depicting the final moments of the original custodian [Lloyd Hawthorne](Lloyd%20Hawthorne.md "Lloyd Hawthorne") and his wife [Cassandra](Cassandra%20Hawthorne.md "Cassandra Hawthorne") that the gulpers were created as a result of experimenting on humans with blight-resistant species. As Lloyd attempts to rationalize the experiments on the holotape while Cassandra tries to barricade the door, a Gulper breaks through the door and proceeds to eat both of them. This Gulper is claimed by [Custodian Benjamin](Benjamin%20TV%20series.md "Benjamin (TV series)") to be his maternal great-uncle [Peter](Peter%20TV%20series.md "Peter (TV series)").

## 

* They share their colloquial name with the "[gulpers](Gulper.md "Gulper")" of the East Coast, which they superficially resemble.
  + East Coast gulpers are altered hellbender salamanders. Much more compact and maneuverable, they are capable of bipedal sprinting and hanging from trees by their tails. They hunt in packs.
  + The solitary Los Angeles gulper strongly resembles an axolotl, another variety of salamander, which may or may not contain axolotl DNA. It has a frame comparable to a crocodile, moving on its belly to support an oversized mandible.

## 

Gulpers appear in the [*Ashlands* TV series](Ashlands%20TV%20series.md "Ashlands (TV series)") episodes "[The Head](The%20Head.md "The Head")", "[The Trap](The%20Trap.md "The Trap")", and "[The Radio](The%20Radio.md "The Radio")".

## 

The TV series' version of the gulper was designed by concept artist [Thomas du Crest](Thomas%20du%20Crest.md "Thomas du Crest") based on preliminary sketches by [Ben Kovar](Ben%20Kovar.md "Ben Kovar").
