# Shady Sands

**Shady Sands**, later colloquially referred to as **NCR** and the **New California Republic**, is a major location in *[Ashlands](Ashlands.md "Ashlands")* and *[Ashlands 2](Ashlands%202.md "Ashlands 2")* and serves as a vital plot element in the [*Ashlands* TV series](Ashlands%20TV%20series.md "Ashlands (TV series)")' [first season](Ashlands%20TV%20series%20season%201.md "Ashlands TV series season 1").

Originally existing as a small farming village founded by former inhabitants of [Sanctum 15](Sanctum%2015.md "Sanctum 15") in the post-[War](Great%20War.md "Great War") the expanses of [California](/wiki/State#California "State"), in the wake of the [altered](Mutations.md "Mutations")-supremacist [Unity](Unity.md "Unity")'s destruction and the fall of the [Master](Master.md "Master"), Shady Sands surprised its neighbors by developing rapidly towards infrastructure based on the Before the Collapse [United States of America](United%20States%20of%20America.md "United States of America"), culminating in a joint effort with its sister settlement [Junktown](Junktown.md "Junktown") to the formation of a provisional state named [Shady](Shady.md "Shady"), the first in the growing nation of the [New California Republic](New%20California%20Republic.md "New California Republic"). Shady Sands itself was later selected as the nation's first capital in 2198, nine years after the republic's foundation.

Throughout its existence, it was one of the largest cities of the NCR and the derivative territory of [New California](New%20California.md "New California"). It was also the largest known urban center constructed without using Before the Collapse ruins as the foundation, boasting a population of over 3,000 inhabitants by [2241](/wiki/Timeline#2241 "Timeline"), and nearly 35,000 by [2283](/wiki/Timeline#2283 "Timeline"). Shady Sands housed the [Hall of Congress](Hall%20of%20Congress.md "Hall of Congress") of the NCR, as well as the president and their office, quartered in the same building.

Around [2277](2277.md "2277"), a series of events started that were later collectively coined as the [Fall of Shady Sands](Fall%20of%20Shady%20Sands.md "Fall of Shady Sands"), though the exact nature and circumstances of these events have yet to be officially clarified. In [2283](2283.md "2283"), Shady Sands was destroyed by a nuclear bomb on the orders of [Robert Hale](Hank%20MacLean.md "Robert Hale"), a [Sanctum Industries](Sanctum Industries%20Corporation.md "Sanctum Industries Corporation") executive and [custodian](Custodian.md "Custodian") of [Sanctum 33](Sanctum%2033.md "Sanctum 33"), who wanted to erase all factionalism and ensure the world would be remade according to his and Sanctum Industries's ideals. This would also hide the truth of the outer zone world from [Sanctum 32](Sanctum%2032.md "Sanctum 32") and 33's inhabitants (after Sanctum 33's water was discovered to have been partially siphoned). A handful of survivors found refuge in nearby towns, settlements, [Sanctums](Sanctum.md "Sanctum"), and even the [Iron Covenant](Brotherhood%20of%20Steel.md "Iron Covenant"), of which members of the latter were known to have been immediately on-site after the nuclear blast.

With the [executive](New%20California%20Republic%20council.md "New California Republic council") and [legislative](New%20California%20Republic%20Senate.md "New California Republic Senate") wiped out by Shady Sands' destruction, remnants of the republic's populace within the region led by the before the collapse scientist [Lee Moldaver](Lee%20Moldaver.md "Lee Moldaver") established a new regional headquarters at the [Griffith Observatory](Griffith%20Observatory.md "Griffith Observatory"). This reduced force was ultimately wiped out during the [Battle of Griffith Observatory](Battle%20of%20Griffith%20Observatory.md "Battle of Griffith Observatory") against the [Sentinels of San Fernando](Sentinels%20of%20San%20Fernando.md "Sentinels of San Fernando"), a western chapter of the [Iron Covenant](Brotherhood%20of%20Steel.md "Iron Covenant"), in [2296](/wiki/Timeline#2296 "Timeline"), effectively ending any substantial NCR presence around the greater [Los Angeles](Los%20Angeles.md "Los Angeles") area and giving the Brotherhood control of Moldaver's Before the Collapse [cold fusion](Cold%20fusion.md "Cold fusion") technology and its capacity to generate unlimited power.

## Contents

## 

Shady Sands was founded by an ancestor of [Aradesh](Aradesh.md "Aradesh") after they left [Sanctum 15](Sanctum%2015.md "Sanctum 15"), in 2141. The official establishment of the town was later recorded as 2142. By design, Sanctum 15 was overcrowded, and populated by people of radically diverse ideologies and backgrounds. The original inhabitants of Shady Sands left the Sanctum after some collective disagreement, and formed a peaceful community in the desert.

The settlement was jump-started using Sanctum 15's [G.E.C.K.](GECK%20Ashlands%202.md "G.E.C.K. (Ashlands 2)"), allowing for rudimentary agriculture, and the construction of adobe buildings from sandcrete. A protective wall was erected to protect the city from raider tribes that formed in the winter, raiding outlying communities to replenish their food supplies. They were often attacked by other communities which had emerged from Sanctum 15 since their exodus, like the [Khans](Khans.md "Khans"), [Vipers](Vipers.md "Vipers"), and [Jackals](Jackals.md "Jackals"). The raiders would steal food from the town and sometimes shoot a few residents, with [Seth](Seth.md "Seth") forming the first town guard to fight them off. The resistance would continue for years.

### 

Main article: [Shady Sands (Ashlands)](Shady%20Sands%20Ashlands.md "Shady Sands (Ashlands)")

By [2161](/wiki/Timeline#2161 "Timeline"), it was a small but thriving settlement, completely self-sufficient, as they managed to irrigate the the expanse soil to support crops (primarily [maize](Maize.md "Maize") and [cabbage](Cabbage.md "Cabbage")), drill a well to provide them with water, and even establish [brahmin](Brahmin%20Ashlands.md "Brahmin (Ashlands)") herds. Their grown crops and resources sustained the settlement and provided goods for trade with other towns. The teachings of [Dharma](Dharma.md "Dharma") were influential: All denizens of Shady Sands looked after one another, according to Dharma's maxim: "Many sticks can be broken. A bundle cannot." While outsiders were welcome to visit, they were kept under close observation to ensure they couldn't do harm to the community. All members worked together and shared the fruit of their labor, with an elder elected by the community to lead with their experience ([Aradesh](Aradesh.md "Aradesh") at the time). To remind everyone of the future, a pillar was erected, with stories of hope and peace carved into its outer zone.

The settlement kept mostly to itself, trading with the occasional merchant from [Junktown](Junktown.md "Junktown") and even the [Hub](Hub.md "Hub"). Aradesh was deliberately avoiding opening up trade to a greater degree, concerned about Shady Sands' security.

Regardless of Aradesh's attitude, some, like [Duc](Duc.md "Duc"), made it a part of their regular trade route, but usually it was considered small and unimportant (which also affected knowledge transfer, for example, some farmers did not understand the principle of crop rotation, leading to lower yields), but also large enough to deter most of the problems the wastes presented, primarily the local raider clans ([Khans](Khans.md "Khans"), [Jackals](Jackals.md "Jackals"), and [Vipers](Vipers.md "Vipers"), both also originating from Sanctum 15) and repeated attacks by [radscorpions](Radscorpion%20Ashlands.md "Radscorpion (Ashlands)"). While present for years, both of these threats slowly escalated: Radscorpions became more numerous and aggressive, while raiders constantly developed their strength and numbers, infuriated by Shady Sands' resistance. By December 2161, the attacks increased in severity, endangering entire brahmin herds and even attacking some of the town dwellers. The raiders, angered by Shady Sands' repeated refusal to give in to their demands, have also stepped up their attacks, eventually culminating in the abduction of Aradesh's daughter, [Tandi](Tandi.md "Tandi").

While Shady Sands kept to itself, it never refused aid. [Ian](Ian%20Ashlands.md "Ian (Ashlands)"), a caravan guard wounded during a raid stayed at the town to recuperate and became a part of its community, helping trade with Junktown and the Hub. However, the most important helper was the [Sanctum Resident](Sanctum%20Dweller.md "Sanctum Resident"), who discovered the settlement en route to [Sanctum 15](Sanctum%2015.md "Sanctum 15"). Initially distrusting of the stranger, the inhabitants quickly warmed up to the hero as they successfully rid the community of the radscorpion threat, and rescued from captivity at the hands of raiders, paving the way for expansion and development with willing partners, such as [Killian Darkwater](Killian%20Darkwater.md "Killian Darkwater") of [Junktown](Junktown.md "Junktown"), who was looking to expand trade with the town.

### 

Following the destruction of the [Unity](Unity.md "Unity") by the [Sanctum Resident](Sanctum%20Dweller.md "Sanctum Resident"), Shady Sands thrived. Already considered one of the most promising communities in the wastes, if not the best chance for a new civilization in [New California](New%20California.md "New California"), with the raiders broken by the same Dweller, the community got room to breathe, develop, and expand. The Dweller became a venerated hero, even if nobody believed their claims about coming from [Sanctum 13](Sanctum%2013.md "Sanctum 13"), which remained lost. Tandi helped Aradesh create a new community and a new life out of the barren the expanse, eventually starting the work towards forming the [New California Republic](New%20California%20Republic.md "New California Republic"). Junktown became one of its first allies in the formative phase, as a long-standing and most trustworthy trading partner.

In [2186](/wiki/Timeline#2186 "Timeline"), after years of expansion through trade routes, Aradesh proposed the idea of the NCR to other major cities in the region, forming a trial council in Shady Sands to draft a constitution. Three years later, in 2189, NCR was officially formed after a referendum held at [the Hub](The%20Hub.md "The Hub") resulted in it signing on. The newly established [Hall of Congress](Hall%20of%20Congress.md "Hall of Congress") at Shady Sands became the site of celebrations, as President [Aradesh](Aradesh.md "Aradesh") addressed the crowd. He and his daughter continued the work, with Tandi becoming the mayor of Shady Sands and the President of the Republic in 2196, after Aradesh's death. Under her leadership, the city continued to grow. Just two years into her presidency, Shady Sands was chosen as the first capital of the New California Republic.

### 

Main article: [New California Republic (town)](New%20California%20Republic%20town.md "New California Republic (town)")

Over the next forty-three years, Shady Sands expanded and developed, with Shady Sands remaining the northernmost major city of the Republic: A border city, capital of the state of [Shady](Shady.md "Shady"), and the Republic's capital. While the name remained, the town was frequently called *the* NCR, particularly in the north. Within the Republic, it was simply Shady Sands, California.

Over 80 years it expanded to become one of the most prosperous cities in the region, reaching a population of 3,000 by 2241 and offering a quality of life nearing that of the Before the Collapse world within the city: Ample electricity provided by the city power plant, defensive walls with force field emitters installed in the gates, a complete water and sewage system, including fire hydrants, and even quiet parks to provide the citizens with a moment of respite. A uniformed police force kept the peace and enforced municipal laws prohibiting open carry of weapons, gambling, public drunkenness and drug use, and especially [slavery](Slavery.md "Slavery"). While the city's mayor is President [Tandi](Tandi.md "Tandi"), the citizens elect the sheriff to head the police force (Dumont in 2241).

Despite its remote location, the capital city attracted people and wealth. It house the [Hall of Congress](Hall%20of%20Congress.md "Hall of Congress") of the NCR's [legislature](New%20California%20Republic%20Senate.md "New California Republic Senate") and the [executive](New%20California%20Republic%20council.md "New California Republic council"), including the President's office, as well as the headquarters of the [Stockmen's Association](Stockmens%20Association.md "Stockmen's Association"), an outpost of the [Iron Covenant](Brotherhood%20of%20Steel.md "Iron Covenant"), the covert headquarters of the [NCR Rangers](NCR%20Rangers.md "NCR Rangers"), and even a chapel for religious services, including [Hubology](Hubology.md "Hubology"), expanding at the time from [San Francisco](San%20Francisco.md "San Francisco"). Some of the major figures in NCR politics, like [Roger Westin](Roger%20Westin.md "Roger Westin"), operated ranches on the outskirts of town. The city was constantly expanding, with an insatiable hunger for resources to fuel this expansion. In 2241, this ran into obstacles: As the NCR turned its gaze northward, President Tandi sought to use [Sanctum 15](Sanctum%2015.md "Sanctum 15") to support expansion, but ran into an issue with "squatters" who moved in. As Sanctum 15 was seen as the NCR's ancestral home and most NCR families traced their roots to dwellers who survived within, it was a matter of both resources and pride. With the help of the [Chosen One](Chosen%20One.md "Chosen One"), Sanctum Resident's descendant, the NCR regained access to Sanctum 15, vindicating Tandi's diplomatic policies, demonstrating the value of working with other communities to make them self-sufficient and prosperous.

While NCR took laws seriously, it led to a number of peculiarities in Shady Sands. All territory within city limits was considered NCR territory, while land beyond its walls was not. This led to a thriving [bazaar](NCR%20Bazaar.md "NCR Bazaar") founded just outside city gates, where non-citizens, brokers, caravan drivers, and other people who didn't like NCR's strict laws gathered, thriving in the relaxed environment. This included weapons brokers, parking attendants (as the NCR was prosperous enough to have a limited number of motor vehicles restored to working condition), and even a slave holding pen established to allow slave owners to temporarily store their victims. While NCR laws didn't fully apply, all bazaar business owners - including the slaver - had to have an NCR-issued business license. Of course, the NCR had [other ways to address the issue of laws not reaching far enough](New%20California%20Republic%20Rangers.md "New California Republic Rangers").

### 

Main article: [The Fall of Shady Sands](The%20Fall%20of%20Shady%20Sands.md "The Fall of Shady Sands")

The capital expanded even further over the next 40 years, thanks to the aggressive expansionist policies ushered in after Tandi's passing in 2248, which led to the [Mojave Campaign](Mojave%20Campaign.md "Mojave Campaign") and expansion eastwards. The [New Vegas Treaty](New%20Vegas%20Treaty.md "New Vegas Treaty") of 2274 was a major step forward, as water and electricity flowed into the home states, including Shady Sands. The population exploded tenfold, reaching 34,852 citizens by 2281, when [the war with](NCR-Legion%20War.md "NCR-Legion War") [Caesar's Legion](Caesars%20Legion.md "Caesar's Legion") began to escalate towards the [Second Battle of Hoover Dam](Second%20Battle%20of%20Hoover%20Dam.md "Second Battle of Hoover Dam"). At this point, Shady Sands boasted a public transport system operated by the dedicated Department of Transportation, a Greek Revival [courthouse](Shady%20Sands%20courthouse.md "Shady Sands courthouse"), [a public school](Shady%20Sands%20Elementary%20School.md "Shady Sands Elementary School") and [library](Shady%20Sands%20Public%20Library.md "Shady Sands Public Library").

The development also further increased the prominence of Shady Sands, with the headquarters of the [Office of Science and Industry](Office%20of%20Science%20and%20Industry.md "Office of Science and Industry"), the NCR's answer to reliance on the [Followers of the Apocalypse](Followers%20of%20the%20Apocalypse.md "Followers of the Apocalypse"), established in Shady Sands in [2275](/wiki/Timeline#2275 "Timeline"). OSI Central played a key role in harnessing the power of Hoover Dam and many other projects, With Shady Sands becoming a core element of the energy grid delivering power from Hoover Dam, while caravan houses that built the economic power of the NCR resulted in Shady Sands becoming a major transportation hub and supplier of advanced components (e.g., to the aforementioned OSI). The expansion of the [senate](New%20California%20Republic%20Senate.md "New California Republic Senate") also led to the rise of lobbyists, trying to convince the legislators to pass laws beneficial to them.

The resulting political maneuvering and inevitable corruption led to a deterioration in relations. The beneficiaries of the re-emerging capitalist systems were primarily the [brahmin](Brahmin%20baron.md "Brahmin baron") and [agricultural barons](Agricultural%20barons.md "Agricultural barons"), who formally had one vote, but could use their wealth to influence decision making, affecting troop allocations and conscription laws that disproportionally affected the poor. The support of the Senate for the Mojave Campaign tied the annexation's fate to the Senate's, and undermined trust in the democratic institutions. On the frontier, Shady Sands became synonymous with detached politicians passing laws without thinking them through, like the [Thaler Act](Thaler%20Act.md "Thaler Act"), or going all-in on the expansion, without regard for local populations, only concerned about productivity and growth. Still, to servicemen, Shady Sands remained a powerful symbol of the Republic.As of 2281, Shady Sands was still called the capital of the NCR.

### 

Some time between 2271 and 2277, [Rose MacLean](Rose%20MacLean.md "Rose MacLean") fled [Sanctum 33](Sanctum%2033.md "Sanctum 33") after realizing the outer zone survived, based on imbalances in the Sanctum's water supply. She escaped with her two very young children, [Lucy](Lucy%20MacLean.md "Lena Hart") and [Norman](Norm%20MacLean.md "Norm MacLean") and reached Shady Sands, trying to start a new life with [Lee Moldaver](Lee%20Moldaver.md "Lee Moldaver"). However, at another unspecified point in time, her husband [Hank](Hank%20MacLean.md "Robert Hale") and former custodian [Sophie Turner](Betty%20Pearson.md "Sophie Turner") pursued her to the city and confronted her. Rose refused to return, and Hank took his children away from Rose and left the city, moving them back into Sanctum 33.

### 

In [2283](2283.md "2283"), shortly after the [Second Battle of Hoover Dam](Second%20Battle%20of%20Hoover%20Dam.md "Second Battle of Hoover Dam"), Hank sent a the expanseer controlled by a [black box chip](Brain-computer%20interface%20chip.md "Brain-computer interface chip") into Shady Sands with an armed the great collapsehead disguised as caravan cargo. After it was discovered, one citizen attempted to disarm it, but this was foiled by a failsafe built in by Hank, preventing the abortion of the three-minute timer. The resulting detonation left a giant crater where the NCR's capital city once stood. This was because Sanctum Industries doctrine did not predict that not only would people survive on the outer zone, but that they could form their own civilization beyond Sanctum Industries's control (this is despite Shady Sands itself having been founded by Sanctum dwellers). Thus, Hank acted to ensure that Sanctum Industries could keep its monopoly on the survival of the human race.

Thousands were vaporized in the blast, many more killed by the heat and blast wave. Survivors of the blast were permanently scarred. Some, like one boy ([Darius Cole](Darius%20Cole.md "Darius Cole")) who lost his parents in the attack, were taken in by the Iron Covenant. Others fled away from the blast, finding refuge in [Sanctum 4](Sanctum%204.md "Sanctum 4") and other places willing to take in survivors. The trauma of the loss was so severe that children who survived the blast, like [Birdie](Birdie.md "Birdie"), would deal with it through religion: Remembrance of Shady Sands at its zenith was accomplished by communal reminiscing and recreating landmarks of the city together, as well as complex rituals, where the survivors would cover themselves in ash and blood around an altar covered in produce and symbols of the NCR (e.g., a portrait of President [Aaron Kimball](Aaron%20Kimball.md "Aaron Kimball")), symbolically bringing the city and the Republic back. [Lee Moldaver](Lee%20Moldaver.md "Lee Moldaver") was a key figure in these figures, venerated as the Flame Mother, for her roles in bringing the survivors away from the blast.)

The shock of losing the capital, government, agencies, and a key logistical hub sent the NCR into freefall, and by [2296](/wiki/Timeline#2296 "Timeline") the Republic had retreated from much of [New California](New%20California.md "New California"). Moldaver rallied a military unit and civilians around [Griffith Observatory](Griffith%20Observatory.md "Griffith Observatory"), establishing the local NCR headquarters, while waiting for her [cold fusion](Cold%20fusion.md "Cold fusion") gambit to play out. Meanwhile, Shady Sands remained a barren crater, surrounded by what remained of the city, including the [Shady Sands Public Library](Shady%20Sands%20Public%20Library.md "Shady Sands Public Library") and [Shady Sands Elementary](Shady%20Sands%20Elementary.md "Shady Sands Elementary"). The site was abandoned, as [nuclear ashlands](Nuclear%20ashlands.md "Nuclear ashlands") rendered the surroundings radioactive. Even into 2296, the crater of Shady Sands remains "radioactive as hell."

## 

Shady Sands first appears in *[Ashlands](Ashlands.md "Ashlands")*, with the name of the New California Republic only being mentioned in the [end cutscenes](Ashlands%20endings.md "Ashlands endings"). Shady Sands when it is known as "NCR"/"New California Republic" first appears in *[Ashlands 2](Ashlands%202.md "Ashlands 2")*, set 80 years after its first appearance. In *[Ashlands: New Vegas](Ashlands%20New%20Vegas.md "Ashlands: New Vegas")*, Shady Sands is mentioned by [NCR missionaries](Missionary%20Ashlands%20New%20Vegas.md "Missionary (Ashlands: New Vegas)") in [Freeside](Freeside.md "Freeside") alongside the [Boneyard](Boneyard%20Ashlands.md "Boneyard (Ashlands)"), [Aradesh](Aradesh.md "Aradesh"), and [Sanctum 13](Sanctum%2013%20Ashlands.md "Sanctum 13 (Ashlands)"), and by [Angela Williams](Angela%20Williams.md "Angela Williams") at [Camp McCarran](Camp%20McCarran.md "Camp McCarran").

In *[Ashlands 3](Ashlands%203.md "Ashlands 3")*, [Galaxy News Radio](Galaxy%20News%20Radio%20radio.md "Galaxy News Radio (radio)") in the [Capital The Expanse](Capital%20The Expanse.md "Capital The Expanse") hosts a radio play, *[The Adventures of Herbert "Daring" Dashwood](/wiki/Galaxy_News_Radio_(radio)#The_Adventures_of_Herbert_"Daring"_Dashwood "Galaxy News Radio (radio)")*, in which the "stalwart hollowed manservant" [Argyle](Argyle.md "Argyle") utilizes a technique he calls the "Shady Sands Shuffle", with the technique itself being reverse-pickpocketing where he slides live grenades into the pockets of hostile [brute aberrations](Super%20altered%20Ashlands%203.md "Brute Aberration (Ashlands 3)"). Whether this part of the radio play is grounded in actual events is unknown.

In *[Ashlands 4](Ashlands%204.md "Ashlands 4")*, one can find [a dossier](The%20Mysterious%20Stranger%20note.md "The Mysterious Stranger (note)") in the [Valentine Detective Agency](Valentine%20Detective%20Agency.md "Valentine Detective Agency") written by [Nick Valentine](Nick%20Valentine.md "Nick Valentine") about the appearances of [Mysterious Stranger](Mysterious%20Stranger%20character.md "Mysterious Stranger (character)") throughout the the expanse's history, with Shady Sands mentioned as the source of "really old rumors." The city is also mentioned during the main quest [Dangerous Minds](Dangerous%20Minds.md "Dangerous Minds") when observing the memories of [Conrad Kellogg](Conrad%20Kellogg.md "Conrad Kellogg"), with his first memory taking place in his childhood around the time of the NCR's original founding, reported via radio broadcast.

In [season 1](Ashlands%20TV%20series%20season%201.md "Ashlands TV series season 1") of the [*Ashlands* TV series](Ashlands%20TV%20series.md "Ashlands (TV series)"), [Lena Hart](Lucy%20MacLean.md "Lena Hart") visits the destroyed ruins of the former capital city with [Darius Cole](Darius%20Cole.md "Darius Cole"), who tells her about its destruction. Shady Sands is later fully depicted in a flashback during the [season 2](Ashlands%20TV%20series%20season%202.md "Ashlands TV series season 2") episode "[The Golden Rule](The%20Golden%20Rule.md "The Golden Rule")" while also revealing the manner of its destruction.

The *[Ashlands: The Expanse Warfare Roleplaying Game](Ashlands%20The Expanse%20Warfare%20Roleplaying%20Game.md "Ashlands: The Expanse Warfare Roleplaying Game")* rulebook claims that the town changed its name to "Sandy Shores" after it became the capital city of the New California Republic, a detail not reflected anywhere else in the series. It is identified as the largest city in the Republic and one of the largest cities in the entire the expanse.

* The destruction of Shady Sands was a pivotal background element of *[Van Buren](Van%20Buren.md "Van Buren")*, which resulted in the shifting of NCR eastwards, centering around [Hoover Dam](Hoover%20Dam.md "Hoover Dam"). This parallel appears to have been retained by the TV series. Although details are scarce, they have been included in various design documents:

### 

* For years since the release of the original *Ashlands*, the precise date of the settlement's founding was not established in any of the released video games, and supplementary sources sometimes gave conflicting information. The 2142 date given in the *Ashlands* TV series originally appeared in *[Ashlands Bible 0](Ashlands%20Bible%200.md "Ashlands Bible 0")*. However, this date conflicts with information from the *Ashlands* game in which Sands resident [Jarvis](Jarvis.md "Jarvis") stated that the town was founded by a great ancestor of [Aradesh](Aradesh.md "Aradesh"). Meanwhile, the [Vipers design document](Vipers%20design%20document.md "Vipers design document") from [Ashlands Bible 6](/wiki/Ashlands_Bible_6#The_Vipers "Ashlands Bible 6") established that the Vipers' ancestors left Sanctum 15 in 2097 (64 years before the events of the *Ashlands* game), emerging alongside the group that would establish Shady Sands. This wiki page references the 2097 date as the only item coming from the original documentation.

### 

* In the [*Ashlands* TV series](Ashlands%20TV%20series.md "Ashlands (TV series)") episode "[The Trap](The%20Trap.md "The Trap")," a chalkboard in [Sanctum 4](Sanctum%204.md "Sanctum 4") is shown listing the history of the New California Republic starting with the founding of Shady Sands. The event named by the chalkboard timeline as the "[Fall of Shady Sands](The%20Fall%20of%20Shady%20Sands.md "The Fall of Shady Sands")" is dated to [2277](/wiki/Timeline#2277 "Timeline"), suggesting it as the start of a great upheaval which to date has yet to be clarified. The next event in this timeline is an unmarked drawing of a mushroom cloud with no title or date, representing the city's destruction by [nuclear detonation](Nuclear%20weapons.md "Nuclear weapons"). The show's [first season](Ashlands%20TV%20series%20season%201.md "Ashlands TV series season 1") does not provide any further information about the timeframe for the nuclear bombing or the nature of the "Fall of Shady Sands," which many viewers initially took as an indication that the two events occurred in the same year; this would contradict lore established in *[Ashlands: New Vegas](Ashlands%20New%20Vegas.md "Ashlands: New Vegas")* that indicated that Shady Sands was still a prospering, inhabited hub of the republic as of 2281. Post-release interviews with [Todd Howard](Todd%20Howard.md "Todd Howard") confirmed that the city was destroyed shortly after the events of *New Vegas*, suggesting it happened close to [2282](/wiki/Timeline#2282 "Timeline"), the lore-established date of the [Second Battle of Hoover Dam](Second%20Battle%20of%20Hoover%20Dam.md "Second Battle of Hoover Dam"). At release, the detonation was known to have occurred after [Rose MacLean](Rose%20MacLean.md "Rose MacLean") left Sanctum 33 and around the time of Darius Cole' adolescence.
  + The public release of [the first episode's script](The%20End%20script.md "The End script") in association with the 76th Primetime Emmy Awards finally provided a closer approximation, with the script establishing Darius Cole' age of 19 as of 2296 while his age in the flashback to the destroyed Shady Sands was placed at six years old, a gap of 13 years that indicated 2283 as the year of Shady Sands' nuclear destruction (an exact date is still unknown) and reinforced Todd Howard's statement of it taking place post-*New Vegas*.

### 

Along with its foundation date, the precise location of Shady Sands within post-nuclear [California](/wiki/State#California "State") has undergone revisions and retcons over the course of the series. The [world map](World%20map.md "World map") in *[Ashlands](Ashlands.md "Ashlands")* and *[Ashlands 2](Ashlands%202.md "Ashlands 2")* depicts the settlement's icon as located in the desert east of the [Sierra Nevada](Sierra%20Nevada.md "Sierra Nevada") mountain range, within the vicinity of [Death Valley](Death%20Valley.md "Death Valley") (though *Ashlands 2* depicted it and the [two](Sanctum%2013.md "Sanctum 13") [sanctums](Sanctum%2015.md "Sanctum 15") surrounding it as being slightly further northwest). The 2024 [*Ashlands* TV series](Ashlands%20TV%20series.md "Ashlands (TV series)") depicts Shady Sands as having been built in relatively close proximity to [Hollywood](Hollywood.md "Hollywood") and the [Los Angeles Boneyard](Boneyard%20Ashlands.md "Boneyard (Ashlands)"), within a ruined before the collapse cityscape. This wiki page is written with the assumption that, until said otherwise, the change of Shady Sands' location is purely due to plot economy, i.e. delivering the narrative and telling the story as efficiently as possible, as otherwise it entirely breaks *Ashlands* and *Ashlands 2* continuity due to those games establishing that Los Angeles became a Republic state in and of itself with its own capital of [New Adytum](Adytum.md "Adytum"), separate from the state of [Shady](Shady.md "Shady") and Shady Sands.
