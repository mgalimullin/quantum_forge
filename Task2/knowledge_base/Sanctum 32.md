# Sanctum 32

**Sanctum 32** is a [location](Ashlands%20TV%20series%20locations.md "Ashlands TV series locations") and [Sanctum Industries](Sanctum Industries%20Corporation.md "Sanctum Industries Corporation") [Sanctum](Sanctum.md "Sanctum") in [Los Angeles](Los%20Angeles.md "Los Angeles"), [California](/wiki/State#California "State") in the [*Ashlands* TV series](Ashlands%20TV%20series.md "Ashlands (TV series)").

## Contents

## 

### 

One of the interconnected [Three Sanctums](Three%20Sanctums.md "Three Sanctums") near [Santa Monica](Santa%20Monica.md "Santa Monica"), [Los Angeles](Los%20Angeles.md "Los Angeles"), Sanctum 32 was directly connected with [Sanctum 31](Sanctum%2031.md "Sanctum 31") and [33](Sanctum%2033.md "Sanctum 33"). However, direct contact between the populations was limited - officially to prevent the spread of threats, but with the ability to come to each other's aid in times of need. The Sanctum remained sealed after the [Great War](Great%20War.md "Great War"), only exchanging population and necessary supplies with its sister Sanctums. For decades, [the dwellers](Sanctum%2032%20dweller.md "Sanctum 32 dweller") were told that the outer zone was uninhabitable, but encouraged to prepare themselves for the eventual .

### 

The situation was stable for over two centuries, as entire generations were born, lived, and died underground. However, a crisis struck in [2294](2294.md "2294"), as the dwellers of Sanctum 32 learned that they were actually guinea pigs, rats in a maze, existing solely for the purpose of [Bud Askins](Bud%20Askins.md "Bud Askins")' eugenics project at [Sanctum 31](Sanctum%2031.md "Sanctum 31"). To breed a class of super-managers that would inherit the Earth after the nuclear holocaust, Askins devised an experiment with [Barb Howard](Barb%20Howard.md "Barb Howard"), where selected graduates of his [Bud's Buds](Buds%20Buds.md "Bud's Buds") executive training program would be placed in a state of [cryostasis](Cryonics.md "Cryonics") in [Sanctum 31](Sanctum%2031.md "Sanctum 31"), and then cross-bred with selected candidates from Sanctums 32 and 33, all under Bud's supervision, as the robot custodian of 31. In essence, 32 and 33 were just breeding pools.

To keep the populations docile, all custodians of 32 and 33 were thawed junior executives from Sanctum 31, awakened periodically and transferred to the other two Sanctums, before replacing their predecessors through supposedly democratic elections or appointments. The consistent selection of custodians from Sanctum 31 was not a secret. Still, none of the dwellers truly questioned it, ascribing it to the superior education and resources of Sanctum 31, as well as the slogan "When things look glum, vote 31." It's not clear how the dwellers learned of the secret, but Custodian [Ian Jackson](Ian%20Jackson.md "Ian Jackson") was tortured to death and his staff hanged. At the same time, the resulting violence across the Sanctum led to widespread loss of life as they split into factions and started killing each other or committing suicide. Graffiti painted on the walls was a stark reminder to others that the dwellers knew the truth and what was inside 31, promising death to Management (Askins' eugenics project). The anti-Management faction appears to have prevailed and attempted to force their way into Sanctum 31, using whatever tools they had at hand, including blowtorches and other construction equipment. However, the revolt ultimately failed, with the dwellers perishing at the [Sanctum door](Sanctum%20door.md "Sanctum door").

Askins restricted communications between Sanctum 33 and Sanctum 32, to cover up the incident, and did not inform [Robert Hale](Hank%20MacLean.md "Robert Hale"), the custodian of 33, of what happened beyond noting Jackson's passing. This allowed [Lee Moldaver](Lee%20Moldaver.md "Lee Moldaver") to use [Rose MacLean](Rose%20MacLean.md "Rose MacLean")'s Wrist Console to open the door to Sanctum 32, and infiltrate Sanctum 33 while posing as the next custodian of Sanctum 32, explaining the sorry state of her crew as a result of wheat blight that hit their crops.

### 

Following the attack on Sanctum 33, Moldaver retreated with the captured Robert Hale through Sanctum 32, burying the passageway behind her with a bomb. However, [Norm MacLean](Norm%20MacLean.md "Norm MacLean") and [Chet](Chet%20TV%20series.md "Chet (TV series)") managed to gain access to the Sanctum while others were distracted by [Steph Harper](Steph%20Harper.md "Steph Harper") going into labor. They discovered that the Sanctum wasn't wiped out by raiders as believed, but instead collapsed in a civil war. Realizing what was happening, [Sophie Turner](Betty%20Pearson.md "Sophie Turner"), the re-elected custodian of 33, decided to resettle Sanctum 32 after consulting with Bud Askins. One reason was to keep the experiment running, while another was to split apart potential troublemakers, such as Norm and Chet. In the meantime, Askins made arrangements to clean up Sanctum 32, removing the bodies and restoring the largely superficial damage of the civil war.

Like before, the "interim" custodian was chosen according to the experiment's rules: Stephanie Harper, the latest Sanctum Industries management trainee to be thawed from [Sanctum 31](Sanctum%2031.md "Sanctum 31"). Other dwellers sent to Sanctum 32 were Chet, [Steph's then-unnamed son](Chet%20Jr.md "Chet Jr."), [Woody Thomas](Woody%20Thomas.md "Woody Thomas"), [Davey](Davey%20TV%20series.md "Davey (TV series)"), and a dozen more.

As of July 2296, Sanctum 32's new dwellers seem to be acclimating well to the Sanctum apart from some minor concerns, such as with Davey expressing confusion with his new home's layout and Steph having trouble with the terminal systems in the custodian's office.

## 

Sanctum 32 is identical in layout to Sanctum 33, except mirrored horizontally relative to Sanctum 33. Its outer zone entrance is not located near the Santa Monica beach, but inland, near a drive-in theater and concealed beneath a pair of railroad bridges.

The principal crop grown in Sanctum 32's farm atrium was , with the soil tilled using [tractors](Tractor.md "Tractor").

## 

Sanctum 32 appears only in the [*Ashlands* TV series](Ashlands%20TV%20series.md "Ashlands (TV series)").

## 

* Steph's [*Ashlands* season 1 character biography on Amazon Prime](Ashlands%20season%201%20Amazon%20overview.md "Ashlands season 1 Amazon overview") initially mentioned that her husband [Bert](Bert%20TV%20series.md "Bert (TV series)") came from Sanctum 32, but this was later removed.
* While the fate of the original Sanctum 32 residents is being investigated, a television is seen playing the in-universe equivalent of the famous real-world ["rat utopia"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Behavioral_sink "wikipedia:Behavioral sink") experiments (this clip can be seen in the [Sanctum Seller's Survival Guide](Sanctum%20Sellers%20Survival%20Guide.md "Sanctum Seller's Survival Guide") episode "[Bud's Buds!](Buds%20Buds.md "Bud's Buds!")"). In these experiments, communities of rats were given unlimited access to the necessities of life, leaving the rats to want for nothing. Ultimately, rather than achieving overpopulation, the rats stopped socializing, mating, eating, or raising their young. Some became extremely hostile and violent. Without any real hardship, the rats lost the will to live, leading to the extinction of the community in short order.
  + Conversely, the in-universe result of the experiment, as shown on the television, was that the rats became overpopulated and ultimately killed each other in the struggle for resources. This could feasibly be the reason for why Sanctum 32 failed, in conjunction with their discovery of the Sanctum 31 experiment and the possible crop failure claimed by Moldaver.
* The existence of Sanctum 32 was first known in 2022 via leaked set photos, which went viral on social media. However, these photos are unable to be uploaded to this wiki for legal reasons. The first official appearance of Sanctum 32 in any show-related material was at the *Ashlands* display at [CCXP 2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CCXP "wikipedia:CCXP"), where cast and crew members including [Jonathan Nolan](Jonathan%20Nolan.md "Jonathan Nolan") and [Walton Goggins](Walton%20Goggins.md "Walton Goggins") were also available for interview. The display included an interactive Sanctum tour including staff members in Sanctum 32 jumpsuits. The tour included a game in which winners would be allowed to "enter" Sanctum 33, and losers would be sent to Sanctum 32 instead.
