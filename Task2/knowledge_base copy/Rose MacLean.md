# Rose MacLean

**Rose MacLean** was the wife of [Hank MacLean](Hank%20MacLean.md "Hank MacLean") and the mother of [Lucy](Lucy%20MacLean.md "Lucy MacLean") and [Norm MacLean](Norm%20MacLean.md "Norm MacLean"). By [2296](/wiki/Timeline#2296 "Timeline"), she is a [ghoul](Ghoul.md "Ghoul") held by [Lee Moldaver](Lee%20Moldaver.md "Lee Moldaver"). She appears in the [*Fallout* TV series](Fallout%20TV%20series.md "Fallout (TV series)").

## Contents

## 

Born and raised in Vault 33 in the 23rd century, around her early 20s, Rose took on the name MacLean after marrying her husband Hank sometime around 2271 when he became the next overseer of Vault 33. She appeared to be unaware at the time that Hank was actually a pre-[War](Great%20War.md "Great War") employee of the [Vault-Tec Corporation](Vault-Tec%20Corporation.md "Vault-Tec Corporation") who had been released from [cryogenic suspension](Cryonics.md "Cryonics") in [Vault 31](Vault%2031.md "Vault 31") as part of the [Bud's Buds](Buds%20Buds.md "Bud's Buds") program to create "super managers" through selective breeding. After marriage, Rose gave birth to her first child, a daughter named Lucy in 2272, followed by a son named Norm some years after.

### 

Sometime around [2277](/wiki/Timeline#2277 "Timeline"), Rose discovered that someone was siphoning from the Vault's water source, which she brought to Hank as evidence that civilization still existed on the surface. However, Hank pointedly forbade this line of questioning, but this only served to deepen Rose's suspicions of her husband's character. At some point, Rose rebelled against him, taking her children and exiting the Vault to the surface, angering Hank in the process who believed Rose was taking the children into a dangerous world.

A short while after leaving the Vault, Rose and her children found their way to [Shady Sands](Shady%20Sands.md "Shady Sands"), capital city of the [New California Republic](New%20California%20Republic.md "New California Republic"), although Norm and Lucy were too young at the time to have developed formative memories about the surface world. During their time on the surface, Rose formed a close friendship with [Lee Moldaver](Lee%20Moldaver.md "Lee Moldaver"), though it is unknown if she ever knew that she too was from the pre-War era like her husband.

### 

That same year, however, Hank and members of Vault 31 "management" (possibly including [Betty Pearson](Betty%20Pearson.md "Betty Pearson")) confronted Rose in Shady Sands and, according to Moldaver, against Hank's demands, she refused to return home. While Rose was left on the surface, Hank took the children back with him. The overseer then lied to the people of Vault 33, including his children as they grew up, telling them that their mother had died during the "Plague of 2277" when "everyone had to quarantine" leading to the Vault's farms withering amidst famine.

Around [2283](/wiki/Timeline#2283 "Timeline"), Hank, out of a combination of the mandate to ensure Vault-Tec's supremacy in the wasteland and anger at Rose spurning him, launched a [nuclear weapon](Nuclear%20weapons.md "Nuclear weapons") at Shady Sands, annihilating it and the inhabitants. Though Rose was not among those killed, at some point during or after, the radiation turned her into a severely deformed [ghoul](Ghoul.md "Ghoul"). She was eventually found by the still-living Moldaver who, out of a sense of sentimentality, kept her alive and imprisoned in Moldaver's headquarters at [Griffith Observatory](Griffith%20Observatory.md "Griffith Observatory") in [Los Angeles](Los%20Angeles.md "Los Angeles"), where the remnants of the NCR's armed forces rallied under her as the "Flame Mother" as she worked on her plan to acquire the Vault-Tec [cold fusion](Cold%20fusion.md "Cold fusion") device to bring power and life back to the ruined city.

## 

Sometime prior to 2296, Rose's [Pip-Boy](Pip-Boy%203000%20Mark%20V.md "Pip-Boy 3000 Mark V") was used by Moldaver and her hired raiders to enter [Vault 32](Vault%2032.md "Vault 32") and disguise themselves as its residents to access Vault 33, which was logged into the systems.

Rose when she was human is seen in multiple flashback sequences when Lucy is reminiscing on her childhood with her mother.

### 

When Lucy arrived at the Griffith Observatory with [Siggi Wilzig](Siggi%20Wilzig.md "Siggi Wilzig")'s head, she found Moldaver sitting at a dinner table next to the wretched ghoul of Rose. Moldaver eventually revealed the ghoul's former identity, which Lucy confirmed seeing her mother's signature pendant embedded in its neck.

During the [Battle of Griffith Observatory](Battle%20of%20Griffith%20Observatory.md "Battle of Griffith Observatory") as the [Lost Hills chapter](Brotherhood%20of%20Steel%20Lost%20Hills.md "Brotherhood of Steel (Lost Hills)") of the [Brotherhood of Steel](Brotherhood%20of%20Steel.md "Brotherhood of Steel") attacked the NCR forces, Lucy, after learning about the extent of her father's actions, chose to turn her [pistol](10mm%20pistol%20TV%20series.md "10mm pistol (TV series)") on, and kill her mother with a headshot as a mercy-kill. Rose's dead corpse was left where it sat as the dying Moldaver came to sit beside her former partner, holding her hand as she watched the cold fusion device restore power to Shady Sands before succumbing to her wounds as well.

## 

According to Moldaver, Lucy is much like her mother, being a kind, loving, and curious person.

## 

Rose MacLean appears only in the [*Fallout* TV series](Fallout%20TV%20series.md "Fallout (TV series)").

In flashbacks, Rose is portrayed by [Elle Vertes](Elle%20Vertes.md "Elle Vertes"). In her feral ghoul form in "The Beginning," she is portrayed in makeup by [Chelsea Reuter](Chelsea%20Reuter.md "Chelsea Reuter").

## 

During an interview, when asked if Rose and Moldaver were lovers in the past, Moldaver's actress [Sarita Choudhury](Sarita%20Choudhury.md "Sarita Choudhury") replied, "I have a backstory and I can't say much. But I can say I love that [fans] are picking up on all of this and adding — or whatever — to it. There is such a closeness and who knows… if there's a Season 2, you'll find out."
