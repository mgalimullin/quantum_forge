# Lucy MacLean

**Lucy MacLean** is the main protagonist of the [*Fallout* TV series](Fallout%20TV%20series.md "Fallout (TV series)") and one of the show's three leads, the other two being [The Ghoul](The%20Ghoul.md "The Ghoul") and [Maximus](Maximus.md "Maximus"). She is a dweller of [Vault 33](Vault%2033.md "Vault 33") in what was once [Los Angeles](Boneyard.md "Boneyard"), who decides to leave the Vault in [2296](/wiki/Timeline#2296 "Timeline") on a mission to save her father [Hank](Hank%20MacLean.md "Hank MacLean"), the [overseer](Overseer.md "Overseer"), from a woman named [Lee Moldaver](Lee%20Moldaver.md "Lee Moldaver").

## 

Lucy was born sometime in the 2270s to Hank and [Rose](Rose%20MacLean.md "Rose MacLean"), dwellers of Vault 33. Rose suspected the surface world was hospitable and fled Vault 33, angering Hank because he viewed this as dangerous. Rose took Lucy and her brother [Norman](Norm%20MacLean.md "Norm MacLean") to live in [Shady Sands](Shady%20Sands.md "Shady Sands"), although it would seem the two children were too young to have memories of being on the surface. Despite this, Lucy would vaguely remember her mother taking her to play in a field in Shady Sands under the sun, feeling its warmth.

At some point, Hank finds Rose on the surface, but she refuses to come back with him. Hank took his children back from Rose, moving them back to Vault 33, and he would eventually drop a nuke on the town in 2283. Lucy would later convince herself that her childhood memory in Shady Sands was merely her playing in the Vault 33 farm under an artificial heat lamp. Hank lied to Lucy, telling her that her mother died from a Vault famine in 2277.

Growing up in Vault 33, Lucy has lived a relatively happy, carefree and privileged lifestyle. However, she does assist her Vault whenever she can. Lucy actively contributes to the well-being of her community. She participates in the Young Pipefitter's Association, gymnastics club, Fencing Team C, intermediate Phys. Ed., and riflery. Her primary passion is teaching American history with a focus on ethics. In her time, she watches movies with her father, gardens with her father, and enjoys family book club with her father.

Lucy knows nothing about the outside world except for what she has seen in books or been taught by her father, Hank, the Overseer of Vault 33. She has some knowledge on [power armor](Power%20armor.md "Power armor"). She has a first cousin named [Chet](Chet%20TV%20series.md "Chet (TV series)"), with whom she did "10 years of cousin stuff" in a non-detailed manner. While this casual intimate relationship between cousins is permitted in the community, marriage is not. Lucy's best friend is [Steph Harper](Steph%20Harper.md "Steph Harper"), a [Vault 31](Vault%2031.md "Vault 31") transplant.

#### 

As the show begins, Lucy gives her marriage application to the Vault 33 governing council ([Woody Thomas](Woody%20Thomas.md "Woody Thomas"), [Betty Pearson](Betty%20Pearson.md "Betty Pearson"), and [Reg McPhee](Reg%20McPhee.md "Reg McPhee")), which they quickly accept. She has an arranged marriage with [Monty](Monty%20TV%20series.md "Monty (TV series)"), a resident of [Vault 32](Vault%2032.md "Vault 32"). After they consummate, Lucy hears screams outside her room. Alarmed, she scans Monty with her Pip-Boy and realizes he is irradiated and must be a raider from the surface. Lucy survives his attack, eventually stabbing him in the neck, leaving him unconscious. She heals herself with a [stimpak](Stimpak.md "Stimpak") and arms herself with a tranquilizer gun during the raider attack. During the fight in the atrium, Lucy hides Norm in a floor storage space and watches her father drown Monty to death in the apple-bobbing tub. [Lee Moldaver](Lee%20Moldaver.md "Lee Moldaver"), posing as the overseer of Vault 32, abducts Hank.

Lucy proposes organizing a search party to rescue her father during an emergency council meeting. The Vault council disapprove of opening the Vault door, as they don't want to give up their new authority. Rather than stay put, Lucy takes the opportunity to leave when the others are not around. She enlists help from Norm, who acts as a lookout, and Chet, who unlocks the elevator to the Vault door and helps unseal the exterior door from the inside. Chet insists on coming with Lucy since the outside world is dangerous, but she tranquilizes him. She hugs Norm goodbye and leaves the Vault, ignoring Reg and [Davey](Davey%20TV%20series.md "Davey (TV series)")'s shouts for her to stop.

#### 

After a day traveling through the Wasteland, Lucy stops to rest for the night. While sleeping by a campfire, Lucy encounters [Siggi Wilzig](Siggi%20Wilzig.md "Siggi Wilzig"), an Enclave defector transporting a valuable artifact to Moldaver for a [cold fusion](Cold%20fusion.md "Cold fusion") project. He tries to warn her about the dangers of the wasteland and urges her to go home before disappearing into the night.

The next day, Lucy makes it to the settlement of [Filly](Filly.md "Filly"), where she is laughed at and looked down on by [Ma June](Ma%20June.md "Ma June"), a storekeeper who views Vault dwellers as naive. As Wilzig arrives in town and has another exchange with Lucy, they are then confronted by [the Ghoul](The%20Ghoul.md "The Ghoul"), who is tracking Wilzig for a bounty. A shootout ensues as the Ghoul shoots Wilzig's left foot off, then kills several gunfighters who try to take him down. After Wilzig's dog [CX404](CX404.md "CX404") is stabbed trying to defend her master, Lucy unsuccessfully tries to deescalate the situation peacefully. When the Ghoul takes a step towards her, she shoots him with a tranquilizer dart, which proves ineffective, though [Maximus](Maximus.md "Maximus") soon arrives in power armor, tackles Lucy out of the way as the Ghoul shoots at her, and keeps him at bay while Lucy and Ma June try to save Wilzig.

When the situation with The Ghoul is resolved, Ma June reveals to Lucy that Moldaver is her client. Lucy attempts to take an injured Wilzig across the wasteland, but he succumbs to his leg injury and takes a cyanide pill. At his request, once he dies, Lucy decapitates him with a chainsaw and takes his head.

#### 

Lucy travels with Wilzig's head and stops along the flooded [Hollywood Boulevard](Hollywood%20Boulevard.md "Hollywood Boulevard"). She briefly feeds a fawn, but it is devoured by a [gulper](Gulper%20TV%20series.md "Gulper (TV series)"). The gulper attacks Lucy, but she manages to free herself by using her tranq gun. The gulper swallows the head and swims away. Before she can pursue him, the Ghoul (who has been using CX404 to track Wilzig's scent) catches up to her and captures her. He ties Lucy to a waterside pulley and repeatedly dunks her in the water as bait to draw out the gulper. They successfully do so, but the gulper attacks too quickly for them to handle, and Lucy accidentally destroys the Ghoul's [vials](Vial.md "Vial") when she uses his saddle bag to stop the gulper from swallowing her.

Angered by her actions, the Ghoul takes Lucy prisoner and drags her away due to her needing to find new vials, reasoning that they will be able to return before the gulper fully digests the head.

#### 

On their journey, Lucy and the Ghoul encounter a friend of the latter's, [Roger](Roger%20TV%20series.md "Roger (TV series)"), a fellow ghoul who is on the verge of turning feral. Realizing that his friend is beyond the point of saving, the Ghoul gets him to reminisce about happier times before shooting him dead. As Lucy gets on his case about his cruelty, she reveals that her last name is 'MacLean', causing the Ghoul to realize that she's the daughter of [his wife's](Barb%20Howard.md "Barb Howard") pre-War assistant. He also forces her to carve up Roger's body for meat.

Sometime later, Lucy drinks some irradiated water out of desperation. While the Ghoul is having a coughing fit, she attempts to flee, but he captures her after she unwisely decides to stand in the same spot. She bites his right index finger off, and he cuts off her right index finger to make things even. The Ghoul soon drags Lucy to the [Super Duper Mart](Super%20Duper%20Mart%20TV%20series.md "Super Duper Mart (TV series)"), where he offers her to [Snip Snip](Snip%20Snip.md "Snip Snip") in exchange for two months' worth of vials, assuring the Mister Handy that she is in pristine condition, apart from her missing finger.

Snip Snip acts courteously to Lucy, and surgically reattaches a decaying index finger to replace Lucy's missing digit. Lucy tries to beg him for help, saying that she feared the Ghoul was sending her to become a sex slave, to which Snip Snip reacts with shock and disgust; he intends to harvest her organs instead. Before Lucy can do anything, Snip Snip fires a tranquilizer dart at Lucy's chest, knocking her out cold.

Snip Snip straps Lucy to a gurney and brings her to [Huey](Huey.md "Huey") and [Squirrel](Squirrel%20TV%20series.md "Squirrel (TV series)"), informing them of the transaction. They tell him to go through with it. He brings Lucy into the operating room and prepares to use his buzzsaw to cut her open, saying that it will be painless, with only a "snip-snip" to be done. She can escape from the gurney; however, she uses a nearby defibrillator to shock Snip Snip and tries using him as leverage to free herself and the captive ghouls. Huey and Squirrel, however, regarded Snip Snip as nothing more than an oversized air conditioner, but allowed her to go when she threatened them with a makeshift weapon, as well as meeting her demand of freeing the ghouls.

They complied, freeing those who hadn't lost their cognitive functions, but this didn't satisfy Lucy, and she threatened to kill the pair with a poisonous cleanser, which she fired at their couch and began to dissolve the material. They then comply and release the others, but Lucy almost immediately regrets this decision, witnessing their feral nature as they charge toward her and the others. She hides from the ferals, who kill Huey and Squirrel before leaving. Afterward, only one ghoul named [Martha](Martha%20TV%20series.md "Martha (TV series)") remains, but she nearly attacks Lucy, leading her to pick up a [10mm pistol](10mm%20pistol%20Fallout%204.md "10mm pistol (Fallout 4)") to kill her.

Outside, she finds the Ghoul on the ground, needing the vials inside the market. Despite his prior mistreatment of her, she decides that she's not going to let him meet the same fate as the ghouls in the mart, and gives him the vials before walking off, showing how she is learning how to blend her ideals with the demands of reality.

#### 

Following her liberation from the Ghoul and nearly having her organs harvested, Lucy comes across Maximus once more, who was imprisoned in his own armor by his squire [Thaddeus](Thaddeus.md "Thaddeus"), who removed his [fusion core](Fusion%20core.md "Fusion core"). Several [radroaches](Radroach.md "Radroach") crawled upon his armor, leading her to blast them off with her handgun. She can lift his helmet's visor, but suffers from radiation sickness, leading him to offer her his [RadAway](RadAway.md "RadAway") if she frees him. With that, she frees him and collapses from her illness. True to his word; however, Maximus treats Lucy's radiation sickness, and both decide to work together to find Thaddeus and regain the head the Gulper had coughed up.

While tracking Thaddeus, Lucy chats with Maximus about the state of affairs on the surface, asking what has happened since the Vault dwellers went underground. Reaching a bridge, they come across two [cannibals](Cannibalism.md "Cannibalism"), [Javin](Javin.md "Javin") and [Rink](Rink.md "Rink"), who attempt to kill them, but Maximus takes Lucy's gun and kills the pair, though he is shot in the process. Sometime later, they come across the ruins of Shady Sands, which Maximus reveals was once his home before the Brotherhood took him in. Lucy is shocked to hear that civilization has returned to the surface without the Vault dwellers and struggles to reconcile this with the stated purpose of Vault 33, which is to come to the surface and rebuild civilization on its own. Soon, Lucy discovers Maximus' wound is more serious than he claims. She takes him looking for medical supplies, eventually coming across [Hawthorne Medical Laboratories](Hawthorne%20Medical%20Laboratories.md "Hawthorne Medical Laboratories"). While searching for supplies in the building, they both end up falling through a trap door, waking up inside [Vault 4](Vault%204.md "Vault 4").

#### 

After landing in Vault 4, Lucy and Maximus find themselves meeting with [Birdie](Birdie.md "Birdie") while the Vault's medical staff [Dr. Edmundson](Nose%20Edmundson.md "Nose Edmundson") and [Nurse Powell](Powell.md "Powell") treat Maximus' wound, extracting a rotten tooth and revealing that was what he was shot with. Birdie explains that they will be kept in quarantine for a while longer to protect the Vault from surface contaminants. While staying there, Maximus tells Lucy she smells nice, which she interprets to be an attempt at flirting, responding by inviting him to have sex with her. Maximus, who did not receive any sexual education in the Brotherhood, tells her that it seems weird. Though Lucy assures him that sexual responses are natural, he refuses, saying the Brotherhood discourages sexual relationships.

After leaving quarantine, they meet the overseer [Benjamin](Benjamin%20TV%20series.md "Benjamin (TV series)") and learn that the Vault is open to refugees from the destroyed Shady Sands. It's then they learn that all levels of the Vault are open, except for level 12. Maximus feels slightly uncomfortable with the Vault, believing it to be a cult, although Lucy shrugs it off. While there, however, Lucy becomes horrified by the vault's worship of someone called the "Flame Mother," who is revealed to be Lee Moldaver. She then decides to venture to the 12th level of the vault, learning of the vault's genetic experimentation. She is caught, however, and attempts to fight her way out, ultimately getting herself captured and imprisoned.

#### 

Following her capture, Lucy learns more about what transpired in the vault via [holotape](Holotape.md "Holotape") and is then brought before the others to receive punishment. Believing she is about to be executed, it's revealed she is merely expelled from the vault and released with some supplies. Then, Maximus, who had adjusted to life in the vault, swipes their fusion core, powers up his recently retrieved armor, and uses it to free Lucy. Lucy then explains they were letting her go, leaving Maximus to feel embarrassed about the whole thing. They then leave the vault, but not before Lucy convinces Maximus to leave the fusion core with them. Reluctantly, Maximus complies and tosses it down the trap door, where they fell into prior. After returning to the fusion core, Lucy invites Maximus to live with her in her vault after she rescues her father. Maximus then reveals his name isn't Titus, but Maximus, and that Knight Titus was a person he let die for threatening him and took his power armor afterward. Lucy admits that she has done some horrible things during her journey through the wasteland, so she doesn't hold Maximus' past against him. Lucy and Maximus remain together, continuing on their journey.

They finally meet up with Thaddeus, who's called for reinforcements and extraction. It's then that he gets defensive when the pair approach him, causing him to trigger a nearby booby trap, leaving Thaddeus with a hole in his neck, but not killed. After Thaddeus is instantaneously healed of his wound, Maximus suspects that Thaddeus may be turning into a [ghoul](Ghoul.md "Ghoul"). Thaddeus becomes in distress as the Brotherhood will surely kill him now. Maximus offers to return to the Brotherhood to prevent them from finding Thaddeus if he gives them Wilzig's head. Thaddeus agrees and thanks Maximus for his help, then leaves. Maximus and Lucy share a kiss before going in separate directions. Maximus creates a fake [decoy head](Critic.md "Critic") to pass off to the Brotherhood, while Lucy goes to Moldaver with the real head and rescues her father. Lucy tells Maximus to find her in Vault 33 someday.

#### 

Separated from Maximus, Lucy makes her way to the Griffith Observatory, Moldaver's base of operations. Arriving in the main operating room, she finds Moldaver waiting for her at a table next to a restrained feral ghoul, with her father caged nearby. She hands over the head and demands the release of her father, to which Moldaver agrees, but not before revealing why she kidnapped him. Moldaver reveals the truth about Vaults 31-33 and that Hank was an executive at Vault-Tec who was cryogenically frozen for two centuries. When Lucy's mother, Rose, found out, she escaped Vault 33 with Lucy and Norm, and they settled in Shady Sands with Moldaver. But when Hank found out, he orchestrated the destruction of Shady Sands, killing thousands of innocent people and taking Lucy and Norm back to Vault 33. Moldaver reveals the head contains a concealed capsule of cold fusion technology, part of research that Moldaver herself was working on, which allows for endless power. Hank was needed because he knew the access code to unlock it. Realizing Hank was responsible for her mother's fate—not death by starvation as she once believed, but the radiation from the bomb on Shady Sands had turned her into the very ghoul sitting at Moldaver's table—Lucy forces Hank to give Moldaver the code.

Once Moldaver gets the code, she attempts to activate her machine, only to realize the Brotherhood of Steel is coming into force. Lucy remains inside the observatory, where Maximus finds her and frees her father. She then reveals to him her father's involvement in destroying Shady Sands. In a rage, Maximus tries to attack Hank, only for Hank to knock him unconscious. Lucy then picks up his gun and aims it at Hank, heartbroken and angered at his actions, but hesitates to pull the trigger. The Ghoul arrives and grazes Hank with a bullet that cuts a gash across his left cheek, demanding to know where his wife and daughter are located. Rather than answer, Hank turns around and flies off. The Ghoul extends an olive branch to Lucy, inviting her to join him in tracking down Hank and, hopefully, his family; if she stays with Maximus, she will likely be killed by the Brotherhood of Steel. Lucy chooses to shoot her mother to end her suffering before following the Ghoul, leaving Maximus with the Brotherhood. Before leaving, Lucy tells an unconscious Maximus that she'll find him again.

Lucy and the Ghoul stop at the [Hollywood Forever](Hollywood%20Forever.md "Hollywood Forever") mausoleum, where they reunite with [Dogmeat](CX404.md "CX404") and she arms herself with a rifle before setting off in pursuit of Hank.

## 

Lucy is an active contributor to the well-being of her community, such as doing Vault maintenance using her repair skills, or gardening. She relishes being challenged, as seen with her participation in various physical activities. However, she downplays her skill with guns, claiming her aim is not always the best, even though she is depicted getting bullseyes. She enjoys reading, watching movies and learning about history. She has been described as being kind, charming, plucky, and strong. Whenever an issue arises, Lucy will bravely jump to the occasion to get things done, such as when she tries to resolve the violence in Filly by talking down the Ghoul, or when she investigates the restricted area of Vault 4 by herself against Overseer Benjamin's directions. This trait may have been the result of her father having been overseer. Lucy strongly believes in the "Golden Rule," which is to treat others as she wishes to be treated.

However, Lucy still displays the behaviors of someone who has been indoctrinated by Vault-Tec, such as when she bluntly asks Monty about his sperm count and when she abruptly asks Maximus if he'd like to have sex with her while the two of them are quarantined in Vault 4's intake room, blurring the lines of her being a sex positive character versus an indoctrinated one. More evidence of possible programming is her over-the-top bubbly personality; she says "Okie-dokie" in many situations, such as when she approves something, when she does not get what she wants, or even when doing something mentally challenging, such as preparing to posthumously behead Wilzig. While this shows her optimism, it may also hint at Lucy instinctively suppressing her true emotions or her method of coping. She also refuses to use harsh profanity as well at first, at least until after her first kill.

Lucy has lived a privileged life without needing to deal with the roughness of the wasteland. She is also said to be naive due to her upbringing, as her only experience with the outside world has been through what she learned in the Vault and read in books. This is one of Lucy's dangerous flaws, and nearly leads to her and Maximus being killed when they encounter a pair of [cannibals](Cannibalism.md "Cannibalism") at a railroad bridge, even injuring Maximus. Lucy learns that she needs to exhibit a healthy degree of caution around strangers, and why she can not always trust human beings to do the right thing. It is also shown Lucy is not immune to peer pressure, seen when she unzips her jacket a little when the Vault 4 dwellers undress entirely.

Lucy is confident that she can improve things, fueled by her optimism of the retro-America she was raised to admire. An optimistic, hard-working person, she has an inherent belief that humanity is generally good and that there is goodness in the world that can prevail over evil. However, she struggles to maintain this belief the more she explores the wasteland, becoming more cynical as she becomes aware of suffering, violence and death. Her personality goes through a transformation when she finds herself betrayed and attacked by humanity, making her tougher and wiser, even if she becomes emotionally overwhelmed sometimes.

Her breaking point takes place when she learns the truth about her father from Lee Moldaver, namely his involvement with Vault-Tec and in the destruction of Shady Sands, which killed thousands of innocent people. This only is worsened after realizing her mother was transformed into a ghoul in the process. Her entire world shatters, leaving her disgusted by her father and unable to see him as the same person she loved and wanted to rescue. She knows she can not rely on her father or Vault-Tec any longer.

Nevertheless, Lucy still retains her kindness and cares for those who are close to her, being worried for Maximus when Hank knocks him out, going as far as grabbing Maximus' gun to hold her dad at gunpoint. She later doubts leaving Maximus to go with the Ghoul, however at the last moment she chooses to follow him to catch her dad and meet the people of Vault-Tec and before leaving, she ultimately kills her mother out of mercy and compassion. This contrasts her upset attitude when she saw the Ghoul kill Roger under similar circumstances, as Lucy now understands he did it to spare Roger from a fate like that of her mother.

While she initially refused to use violence, Lucy's time in the wasteland has made her more open to attacking and harming others. However, she still refuses to kill in cold blood, which is seen during her interaction with the [Great Khans](Great%20Khans.md "Great Khans"), politely asking for a solution that doesn't involve violence and murder, but resorting to inflicting non-fatal injuries when negotiations fail. She explains to the Ghoul that she wants to set the example in doing things in a different way in the wasteland, wanting to arrest her father and bring him to justice rather than kill him for revenge like the Ghoul prefers to do.

Furthermore, Lucy still tries to talk things through first, as seen when tries to negotiate with the Khans and when she approaches [Old Woman Gretch](Old%20Woman%20Gretch.md "Old Woman Gretch") peacefully, in contrast to the Ghoul, who's willing to pull a gun on her. Lucy's time outside of the vault has slowly made her more tolerant of the outside world's cruelty, being able to at least endure being near blood or dismembered bodies, remaining calm when the head of a man victimized by her father explodes, which only fuels her desire to make her father face justice.

This contradicts the series' starting date of 2296; if Lucy was born in 2278, she would be 18 or 19 years old, not 20. Given that this birthdate is not actually shown anywhere in the series, and only appears in a behind-the-scenes photograph of a prop, it is not a definitive confirmation of Lucy's birthday.
  + The February 15 birthday was later reused for [Steph Harper](Steph%20Harper.md "Steph Harper")'s date of birth (February 15, 2045), which is shown in the series (unlike Lucy's birthday, which is never seen on-screen) in the episode "[The Demon in the Snow](The%20Demon%20in%20the%20Snow.md "The Demon in the Snow")."
* In the Lucy Dweller's Kit promotional item, it is shown that before leaving Vault 33 that Lucy drew devil horns and a devil tail on Monty on their wedding photo, and mentioned it was not a great day.
* According to Ella Purnell's talent agency, she was going to portray a character called Jean in the TV series. It is unclear if this was an error or if Jean was Lucy's original first name.

### 

* Lucy's character was pitched to Ella Purnell before she had a chance to read the script. According to Purnell, Lucy was described to her as "Leslie Knope meets Ned Flanders."
* Purnell plays video games and played some of *[Fallout 4](Fallout%204.md "Fallout 4")* to prepare for her role. She said that she did not get very far and struggled with trying to dismiss [Dogmeat](Dogmeat%20Fallout%204.md "Dogmeat (Fallout 4)") and leaving him at the [Red Rocket truck stop](Red%20Rocket%20truck%20stop.md "Red Rocket truck stop"). However, she did also watch gameplay footage of the video games, though she did not specify how much and which games. Purnell said that she only had "a couple months maybe" to prepare for the role. She also spent some time on the [Fallout subreddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/Fallout/) and the [Fallout Wiki](Fallout%20WikiAbout.md "Fallout Wiki:About") to increase her understanding of the series lore.
* In an interview with *[Refinery29](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Refinery29 "wikipedia:Refinery29")*, Ella Purnell specifically criticized notions of the series being "woke" due to its focus on a female protagonist, saying the *Fallout* games are inherently political and that analyzing society and its different groups is part of the DNA of *Fallout*. Purnell said she loves seeing women in survival dramas and seeing the brutality and violence women are capable of when they are hungry and in survival mode, and that it breaks stereotypes of what women have to be portrayed as in media, commenting, *"You're not this together, pretty picture that films originally depicted women as."* The *Fallout* series has portrayed this idea with characters such as [Carolina Red](Carolina%20Red.md "Carolina Red") in *[Fallout 3](Fallout%203.md "Fallout 3")*, [Violet](Violet.md "Violet") in *[Fallout: New Vegas](Fallout%20New%20Vegas.md "Fallout: New Vegas")*, [Dixie](Dixie.md "Dixie") in *[Fallout 4](Fallout%204.md "Fallout 4")*, and [Dagger](Dagger.md "Dagger") in *[Fallout 76](Fallout%2076.md "Fallout 76")*.
* Purnell said one of the worst parts about portraying Lucy was having her right pointer finger painted before filming all the time, calling it the "poop finger" and said it was the bane of her life.

* Lucy shares similarities to [Audrey Stolz](Audrey%20Stolz.md "Audrey Stolz") from *Fallout 76*. For example, they both lived in Vaults for much of their lives (Vaults 33 and [63](Vault%2063.md "Vault 63")). Their fathers worked for Vault-Tec and have four-letter names beginning with H (Hugo, Hank). Their fathers betrayed communities by leaving them to die by nukes. Audrey and Lucy later grow desires to kill their fathers. Both Audrey and Lucy were separated from their lovers (Maximus and Alex), have mothers who became ghouls, and have cousins who live in their Vaults. Audrey and Lucy are both shown to be interested in science, even if Lucy downplays her science skills.

### 

* Lucy is the only character appearing in all episodes of the series, as Maximus is absent in "[The Ghouls](The%20Ghouls.md "The Ghouls")" and "[The Innovator](The%20Innovator.md "The Innovator")" and the Ghoul is absent in "[The Past](The%20Past.md "The Past")".
* Lucy appears to be the first character in the entire *Fallout* series shown to visually shed tears while crying, as opposed to a text description or an animation without tears.
* In localized versions of the series, Lucy's voice is dubbed by Zina Khakhoulia (European French), Ludivine Reding (Canadian French), Miyuki Sawashiro (Japanese) and Monserrat Mendoza (Latin American Spanish).
