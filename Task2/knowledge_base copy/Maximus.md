# Maximus

**Knight Maximus** is a member of the [Brotherhood of Steel](Brotherhood%20of%20Steel.md "Brotherhood of Steel") chapter known as the [Knights of San Fernando](Knights%20of%20San%20Fernando.md "Knights of San Fernando") in the [*Fallout* TV series](Fallout%20TV%20series.md "Fallout (TV series)"). He is one of the three main protagonists, the other two being [Lucy](Lucy%20MacLean.md "Lucy MacLean") and [The Ghoul](The%20Ghoul.md "The Ghoul"). During his childhood, he was known as **Max**, though the extended name of Maximus was given to him by the Brotherhood.

Maximus is a rookie soldier who grew up above ground but, like Lucy, was also raised in a cloistered "family" of sorts: the Brotherhood of Steel. However, unlike Lucy, he has a cynical sense of self-preservation and does not always act honorably.

In the beginning, Maximus has recently been elevated to the rank of [squire](/wiki/Brotherhood_of_Steel_ranks#Squire "Brotherhood of Steel ranks"). In [2296](/wiki/Timeline#2296 "Timeline"), Maximus is sent by the Brotherhood with his [knight](Knight.md "Knight") as an expendable assistant. His mission leads him towards an [artifact](Cold%20fusion.md "Cold fusion") from an enigmatic researcher called [Siggi Wilzig](Siggi%20Wilzig.md "Siggi Wilzig"), causing his path to collide with those of Lucy and the Ghoul.

**How does Maximus's cynical sense of self-preservation impact his actions in the series?**

Maximus' desire to survive and make something of himself lead him to take his Knight's power armor for himself and fight to retrieve the head that is the objective of the Brotherhood drag net. Rising through the ranks is seen by him as the only way to make something of himself, although by the end of the season his fervent devotion to the Brotherhood's mission is severely tested.

## 

A former citizen of the [New California Republic](New%20California%20Republic.md "New California Republic") born in [2277](/wiki/Timeline#2277 "Timeline"), Max had the misfortune of living in [Shady Sands](Shady%20Sands.md "Shady Sands"). The NCR's capital [was destroyed](Fall%20of%20Shady%20Sands.md "Fall of Shady Sands") with a nuclear weapon when he was 6 years old. Although he was on the edge of the crater, he survived thanks to his father [Joseph](Joseph%20TV%20series.md "Joseph (TV series)"), who placed him inside a refrigerator in their house; his father and his mother [Julia](Julia%20TV%20series.md "Julia (TV series)") both perished in the blast.

After leaving his hideout, Max saw the still-burning crater and charred bodies of other NCR citizens before coming face to face with an [armed Brotherhood knight](/wiki/File:Shady_Sands_Knight_and_Maximus.png "File:Shady Sands Knight and Maximus.png") in [power armor](T-60%20power%20armor%20TV%20series.md "T-60 power armor (TV series)"). The memory was seared into his mind, defining the trajectory of his life.

Orphaned in the nuclear attack, the boy was taken to the Knights of San Fernando's [base and training camp](Brotherhood%20base.md "Brotherhood base") in [New California](New%20California.md "New California"). He was given a new name, Maximus, as well as food, shelter, education, and a chance to rise through the ranks of the Brotherhood. From the outset, he was an outsider, targeted by [Thaddeus](Thaddeus.md "Thaddeus") for bullying. It wasn't personal for Thaddeus, just a matter of deflecting the abuse Thaddeus was receiving onto a new, more attractive target. This gambit worked, and so Maximus became the underdog. Shunned by most of his fellow aspirants, he became close friends with only one: [Dane](Dane%20TV%20series.md "Dane (TV series)").

While he developed an impressive physique and endurance, allowing him to withstand regular beatings and even strike back at the bullies, he struggled with more cerebral subjects. In particular, technical and scientific subjects proved challenging, earning him the ire of [clerics](Cleric.md "Cleric") teaching the subjects, such as [Cleric Felix](Felix%20TV%20series.md "Felix (TV series)"). Despite that, he persevered, hoping to one day be able to hurt those who hurt him, like those who destroyed Shady Sands. The Brotherhood earned his loyalty and Maximus developed a steadfast belief in its mission and values, not yet tempered by the reality of the wastelands or the Brotherhood's method of operation. It is his sincere belief that "a knight's duty [is] to better this fallen world." However, his lack of life experience makes him prone to hasty decisions and mistakes, some of them very consequential.

Due to his traumatic childhood and hard life in the Brotherhood, some of these gaps have left him unaccustomed to creature comforts, unaware that humans can still enjoy a good quality of life in the post-apocalypse. The biggest gap in his experience is tied to sex. The Brotherhood does not have a sexual education curriculum, and so Maximus is not entirely familiar with the concept of intercourse, ejaculation, or masturbation. He is actually somewhat repulsed by sex, either believing or excusing himself by claiming that Brotherhood knights are supposed to be celibate.

Maximus is beaten up by Thaddeus and his group of ["minion" aspirants](Thaddeus%20minion.md "Thaddeus' minion") at the Brotherhood base, seemingly for no other reason but bullying. Once the group leaves, chased away by the arrival of some [officers](Brotherhood%20officer.md "Brotherhood officer"), Dane arrives to check on Maximus' condition.

One day, Dane is promoted to [squire](Brotherhood%20of%20Steel%20ranks.md "Brotherhood of Steel ranks") instead of Maximus, which frustrates Maximus. However, Dane's foot is maimed while getting out of bed, causing suspicion to fall upon Maximus.

Maximus is escorted into a repurposed aircraft by [Petty Officer Shortsight](Shortsight.md "Shortsight"), where he is to be interrogated by [Elder Cleric Quintus](Quintus.md "Quintus") and a Brotherhood knight who intimidates Maximus to speak truthfully. When Quintus questions Maximus why he joined the Brotherhood of Steel, Maximus says, "To hurt the people who hurt me." Quintus informs Maximus that his fellow aspirants have accused him of orchestrating Dane's maiming. Maximus denies it, but Quintus asks him if he has the ambition to advance in the Brotherhood and why he would not desire such an outcome on a rival. Maximus admits that he did want Dane to be injured so he could take their spot, but he maintains that he did not do it.

Quintus lectures Maximus that violence against a fellow brother is a sign of weakness and asks Maximus if he is weak. Maximus replies that he doesn't want to be. When given a chance to give any statements in his defense, and feeling that his defense may not be enough to be found innocent, Maximus thanks the Brotherhood for his guidance, giving him a home and a place in the world. Maximus looks at Quintus and says: "If I can help the Brotherhood make it [the world] better... Eden or whatever... even if it means giving my own life... I'll do it. And I'd be grateful to the Brotherhood for giving my life meaning." Impressed by Maximus' character, Quintus believes his defense, clearing him of these charges, and promotes him to be [Knight Titus](Titus.md "Titus")' squire in Dane's place.

Afterward, Maximus checks on Dane at the medical tent. Dane reassures Maximus that their circumstances aren't so bad. When Maximus asks if they suspect anyone of causing their injury, Dane replies they've narrowed it down to any of their bullying peers. Dane informed Maximus that they knew he took their spot, and they both confirmed that they'd both been questioned about whether there was any credibility to the rumor that Maximus was the perpetrator of Dane's maiming. Dane, however, tells Maximus that they told their interrogators that they believed that Maximus wouldn't hurt a fly.

Maximus and Knight Titus are assigned the mission of searching for [Siggi Wilzig](Siggi%20Wilzig.md "Siggi Wilzig"), a member of the [Enclave](Enclave.md "Enclave") on the lam, who also has an object that can change the balance of power in their nation.

Maximus soon learns that Knight Titus is callous and a bully since he would order Maximus to perform demeaning work, such as cleaning his codpiece. While en route to their ordered destination of [Filly](Filly.md "Filly"), Titus spontaneously orders the [vertibird](Vertibird.md "Vertibird") they were flying in to drop them off midflight because he is bored and wants to kill something. Despite straying from their objective, Titus and Maximus stumble onto one of Wilzig's camps near a [yao guai](Yao%20guai.md "Yao guai")'s cave. Despite Titus being better equipped to handle a Yao Guai, Titus orders Maximus to delve deeper into the Yao Guai's den ahead of him, only for the Yao Guai to sneak behind Titus and attack. Maximus was frozen in shock as Knight Titus was overwhelmed by the Yao Guai, and after being disarmed by the Yao Guai, Titus fled the cave in terror until he tripped and smashed his head on a rock. The Yao Guai proceeded to maul Titus until Maximus composed himself and shot the Yao Guai dead.

Titus orders Maximus to remove his helmet, then ruthlessly insults and blames him for his sustained damages. Titus reveals he is contemptuous of the Brotherhood of Steel's purpose and his superiors; he complains of being sent to face the dangers of the [wasteland](Wasteland.md "Wasteland") for a "toaster oven." Titus orders Maximus to fetch him a [stimpak](Stimpak.md "Stimpak") and threatens Maximus with a torturous death for failing his duties as a squire. Maximus judges Titus antithetical to the Brotherhood's purpose of bettering their fallen world and deems him unworthy of his power armor. Thus, Maximus withholds the stimpak from Titus. Although Titus threatens Maximus by saying that the Brotherhood would kill him, Maximus asserts he won't be punished if he returns with the target. Maximus then watches Titus die of his wounds.

Maximus takes Titus' power armor and assumes his identity. While playing with the power armor and testing its capabilities, Maximus interrupts a scuffle between a [chicken collector](Chicken%20collector.md "Chicken collector") and a [snake oil salesman](Snake%20oil%20salesman.md "Snake oil salesman"). Maximus used the strength of his power armor to subdue the attacking chicken collector, while the snake oil salesman smugly escaped. Maximus then learned that the reason the chicken collector was attacking the snake oil salesman was because he was having sex with the chicken collector's chickens. Maximus was perturbed by such strange behavior but acted as if the matter was resolved satisfactorily.

When Maximus arrives at Filly, he finds that The Ghoul has shot up the town to obtain the bounty on Wilzig and that Lucy MacLean has stood up to The Ghoul to protect Wilzig. Maximus intercedes to shield Lucy from being shot by The Ghoul and tells Lucy that Wilzig is important to him and that he will cover their escape by keeping The Ghoul busy. Maximus' power armor could throw The Ghoul around the town for a while, but The Ghoul used the heavy weight of the armor and the fragility of the town's structure to outmaneuver Maximus. Maximus gets his foot stuck on a fragile platform, immobilizing him. The Ghoul mocks Maximus' inexperience with piloting the power armor and advises him to read its manual before going around him and using his knife to break some of the armor's components. This causes his power armor to malfunction, and although he escapes his immobility by attempting flight, Maximus' malfunctioning suit causes him to fly clumsily. Coupled by The Ghoul using a towing cable to rope Maximus and disrupt his flight pattern, Maximus loses control; he flies and crash-lands somewhere out of the town. Nevertheless, his distraction allowed Lucy to escape town with Wilzig.

Repeating his former knight's actions, he orders Thaddeus to clean his power armor's codpiece. Thaddeus relays information about the fact that they "are not the only ones looking for the target," and is told by the Brotherhood to kill anybody that stands in their way.

After leaving [Filly](Filly.md "Filly"), Maximus continues to demean Thaddeus, ordering him to climb a dead tree and look for apples, even though there are obviously none. Thaddeus falls to the ground, much to Maximus's amusement. Thaddeus suggests repairing the power armor's radio to contact the Brotherhood for reinforcements, to which Maximus gets mad and tells him that he will retrieve the target by himself. Thaddeus approves of his knight's idea to let The Ghoul live since all wastelanders carry radiation, which they can trace. Maximus takes credit for this idea, even though he had never considered it.

While following The Ghoul's trail, they stumble upon the rest of Wilzig's headless body. They mistakenly credit the decapitation to be The Ghoul's doing. Thaddeus suggests retrieving the rest of the body, but Maximus thinks the body is useless to them, as whatever was valuable about Wilzing must have been in his head. After realizing Thaddeus was trying to fulfill his duties as a squire, he sits down and allows him to refill it. The two of them converse, with Maximus asking him about his thoughts on Knight Titus' apparently dead squire (himself). Thaddeus starts off by talking positively about him, but Maximus orders him to say something negative about him. He discovers that Thaddeus had only been bullying him because he had been bullied before Maximus' arrival and wanted to redirect the bullying on him instead, making him popular. Thaddeus comments that he wished Maximus had lived long enough to redirect the bullying on someone else himself. Maximus begins to empathize with Thaddeus.

Afterward, they continued following the tracks of The Ghoul, leading them to Hollywood Boulevard, which had been flooded with radiated water and inhabited by a [gulper](Gulper.md "Gulper"). Maximus rebuked Thaddeus for leading them to danger but ordered him to run to safety while he personally dealt with the Gulper. While attempting to take the creature down, Maximus runs out of ammo from his pistol and prepares to fight in close-quarter combat; Thaddeus, however, aids him and shoots at the Gulper with a rifle. However, this alerts the gulper to Thaddeus' location, and he is chased down and dragged by the beast. The gulper nearly swallows Thaddeus, but Maximus comes to aid Thaddeus by grabbing him and pulling him out of the beast's mouth from the opposite side. The tug of war causes the gulper to vomit and die. While recovering from the situation, they find the target (Wilzig's head) to be among the things the gulper vomited and being licked by [CX404](CX404.md "CX404"), who had been searching for Wilzig as well. They celebrate at the sight of this and prepare to return the head to the Brotherhood.

He survives this encounter after the radroaches are shot down by Lucy MacLean, who opens up his faceplate and recognizes Maximus as the knight back in [Filly](Filly.md "Filly"). She asks if he remembers [the man she left with](Siggi%20Wilzig.md "Siggi Wilzig"), who Lucy describes as "with the glasses and the whole body." Lucy is once again hesitant, telling Maximus that practically every person she has met on the surface has attempted to kill her. Lucy asks him what he thinks are in the vaults, if not people, to which Maximus replies with "monsters." Lucy claims that vault dwellers like her are honorable and that she comes from a place where the worst anyone can do to someone is "forget to say thank you." She claims that she knows about the [Great War](Great%20War.md "Great War"), the bombs falling, and the 320 years of American history before then, yet she needs help with the last 200 years "after the bombs fell." Maximus responds that the bombs fell when he was a kid, to which Lucy asks if that's what they tell him in the Brotherhood. Lucy then offers a peaceful solution: both groups would simultaneously cross the bridge with their hands up. Midway through the crossing, however, the travelers shoot at them. While in quarantine, Maximus mentions to Lucy that she smells good. Noticing that Maximus is attracted to her, Lucy invites Maximus to have sex with her. Having never received any sex education, Maximus grew up being afraid to use his genitals, as he came to believe that sexual responses like arousal and ejaculation were likened to a pimple popping and were considered weird. Lucy trusts them since a lot of them are survivors of Shady Sands. She also reminds Maximus that he needs time to recuperate, so they can't leave. After discovering that a fusion core powers Vault 4, Maximus attempts to steal it to power his power armor. During his attempted theft, he is interrupted by Birdie, who says that he may be having trouble adjusting to a safe environment as he has been conditioned to think that the cutthroat lifestyle needed to survive the surface is the only norm. She gives Maximus access to his own room, Unit 428, and advises him to enjoy its comforts to aid in his transition. Maximus experiences the luxurious comforts of vault life for the first time and finds it pleasing. Maximus leaves the vault with Lucy and his power armor, but Lucy stops them to mention that without Vault 4's fusion core, Vault 4 can only survive on auxiliary power for a few days. Lucy thinks they should return the fusion core, but Maximus is reluctant to do so because without it, his power armor would be useless, and he wouldn't be able to be a heroic knight or help Lucy rescue her father. Lucy argues that her father wouldn't want to be rescued at the expense of innocent people. Ultimately, Maximus and Lucy return the fusion core to Vault 4 together.

Lucy tells Maximus that he is the best stranger she has ever met and that he is a good person. After rescuing her father, she invites Maximus to live with her in [Vault 33](Vault%2033.md "Vault 33"). Maximus discloses that he isn't really Knight Titus and reveals his real name to her. He explains that Knight Titus was a person he let die because he was threatening him and took his armor for himself afterward, and so admits he isn't the good person she believes him to be. Lucy admits that she threw acid at an innocent man's face during her investigation of Vault 4, and she has only been in the wasteland for two weeks. She chooses not to hold Maximus' past against him and invites him to live with her in her vault again. This time, Maximus happily accepts.

Maximus and Lucy eventually catch up to Thaddeus at the KPSS radio station in Hollywood Hills. As the two approach Thaddeus, Thaddeus shoots at them but misses every shot. Maximus and Lucy approach him peacefully with their hands up and explain they only want the head. Thaddeus steps on one of the traps littered throughout the radio station and gets a crossbow bolt lodged in his neck. Surprised that he hasn't died, Thaddeus takes out the crossbow bolt, only for his wound to be instantaneously healed. Maximus suspects that Thaddeus may be turning into a [ghoul](Ghoul.md "Ghoul"), causing Thaddeus distress as the Brotherhood of Steel will surely kill him now. With the Brotherhood already en route to him, Maximus instructs Thaddeus to give him the head, and he'll return to the Brotherhood to slow them down so Thaddeus can escape to safety. Thaddeus thanks Maximus and gives the two abrupt apologies and farewells. Maximus then gives Lucy Wilzig's head so she can use it to free her dad and go home, while he takes a disfigured, fake head from a [nearby corpse](Critic.md "Critic") and passes that one off as Wilzig's head to the Brotherhood. Although Lucy is reluctant to leave Maximus, they kiss and part ways. She tells Maximus to find her and to seek out Vault 33 someday.

#### 

Maximus is transported to Filly, which the Brotherhood of Steel has recently conquered. Once there, he is brought before Quintus, who notes that he is wearing Knight Titus' clothes. Maximus reveals that Knight Titus is dead and presents Quintus with the fake head. Quintus has his clerics examine the head, and they discover it's a fake since it doesn't have the artifact. Quintus accuses Maximus of lying about the artifact, as he lied about his culpability in Dane's maiming. The Brotherhood knights restrain Maximus and point a gun to his head, with Quintus demanding he confess. Maximus implores Quintus to spare his life, promising to lead the Brotherhood to the artifact's location if they do so. Dane then interjects, confesses that their injury was self-inflicted, and pleads for Quintus to listen to Maximus for the sake of the Brotherhood. Quintus commends such a rare display of loyalty and orders the knights to release Maximus.

Quintus and Maximus have a private talk, where Quintus asks Maximus about the circumstances of Titus' death to which the squire coldly reveals that Titus died running. Seemingly unsurprised by this revelation, Quintus disappointedly admits that the Brotherhood had lost its way before reminiscing about when they ruled the wasteland on the West Coast. He adds that power must be taken and not given, which is a lesson that he believes Maximus has learned and experienced well. He continues by saying that by leading the Brotherhood to the relic, the two of them will be able to take power, allowing them to start a new Brotherhood, with Quintus as its leader and people like Maximus as its sword. He then reminds Maximus about his lifelong search for a home and offers him a chance to build one together.

Maximus prepares to fight at [Griffith Observatory](Griffith%20Observatory.md "Griffith Observatory") and thanks Dane for speaking in his defense. Maximus is surprised to find that Dane will be fighting alongside him. Dane explains it's punishment for maiming themself and apologizes to Maximus for their deception, as they had no idea he would be blamed for it. Maximus tells Dane there's nothing to apologize for as he believes he was meant to go on that mission, elaborating that he met Lucy because of it. He's not only going to fight the upcoming battle to rescue her, but also to leave with her to live in her vault. Maximus describes her vault as a "... place that's peaceful... and safe. Where nobody ever goes to war." Dane replies that there is nowhere that's safe, and there is no leaving the Brotherhood, even though they wish there were.

Maximus fights in the [battle to take the observatory](Battle%20of%20Griffith%20Observatory.md "Battle of Griffith Observatory"). After defeating [Lee Moldaver](Lee%20Moldaver.md "Lee Moldaver")'s [NCR](New%20California%20Republic.md "New California Republic") forces in the courtyard, during which Maximus shoots at least one [NCR soldier](NCR%20soldier%20TV%20series.md "NCR soldier (TV series)"), Maximus' squadron breaches the observatory. Once inside the observatory, Maximus' squad is ambushed by the Ghoul and suffers heavy casualties; only Maximus is shown to escape, and the others' fates are left unseen. One of the knights the Ghoul decapitates inadvertently breaks through the control room doors where Lucy and her father, [Hank MacLean](Hank%20MacLean.md "Hank MacLean"), are being held. Maximus enters the room, frees Lucy's father from his cage, and proceeds to try to shepherd Lucy to an escape from the battle. Lucy stops him as she refuses to return with her father, who she reveals was responsible for the bombing of Shady Sands. Enraged by this information, Maximus attempts to attack Hank, but Hank has equipped the fallen Brotherhood knight's power armor and knocked Maximus out.

When Maximus regains consciousness, he notices that Lucy and her father have disappeared, Lucy having chosen to leave with the Ghoul to pursue Hank. Moldaver, who was grievously injured by gunfire during the battle, arrives to activate the artifact and uses it to create [cold fusion](Cold%20fusion.md "Cold fusion"). With the cold fusion reactor activated, Maximus and Moldaver watch as it powers [Los Angeles](Boneyard.md "Boneyard") with unlimited energy. Moldaver insinuates that the Brotherhood of Steel will abuse the unlimited energy that they will acquire after the battle. She warns Maximus about what the Brotherhood would do with unlimited energy and entreats him to try stopping them if they are corrupted by their newfound power, but is uncertain if he will succeed. Ultimately, she concludes that his trying is all anyone can expect of him. Moldaver succumbs to her wounds and dies. Dane finds Maximus with Moldaver's corpse and asserts that he killed her. Maximus attempts to correct Dane, but they have already started spreading the narrative to the rest of the Brotherhood forces present and begin calling for Maximus to be promoted to Knight Maximus for the feat. Hailing Maximus as a hero, the Brotherhood forces begin chanting for all to hail "Knight Maximus," though he is no longer happy about it like he may have been in the past.

Maximus has a flashback to his childhood, remembering the day he lost his parents and his home upon the destruction of [Shady Sands](Shady%20Sands.md "Shady Sands") in [2283](/wiki/Timeline#2283 "Timeline"). In the present, in his new station as a knight of the San Fernando chapter, he leads a two-man mission with a fellow knight as both storm a [shadowy pre-War facility](Shadowy%20facility.md "Shadowy facility") in T-60 suits, eliminating several [feral ghouls](Feral%20ghoul%20TV%20series.md "Feral ghoul (TV series)") to obtain a [control panel](Control%20panel.md "Control panel").

Returning to the *[Caswennan](Caswennan.md "Caswennan")*, Maximus is welcomed by the celebrations of his chapter and meets with Quintus on the flight deck as he commends him on his success in retrieving "the key to [the Brotherhood's] new home." While scribes work with the control panel, Quintus tells Maximus his belief that the present-day Brotherhood lacks the strength to unite the wasteland due to the isolation and factionalism separating its many individual chapters; he intends to start the process of reunification with the Knights of San Fernando at the lead, first by carrying out their latest mission to obtain "the mightiest arsenal in history."

The *Caswennan* arrives at an abandoned playground, where two scribes confirm with the flight deck squire via reflected-light coding that everything is ready. One of the scribes then activates the [fusion core](Fusion%20core.md "Fusion core")-powered control station inside the playground structure. Maximus witnesses the process of reactivating a dormant array of wind turbines that rise up from their underground siloes and blow away the immense layer of sand covering the structure underneath, revealed to be the pre-War military base of [Area 51](Area%2051.md "Area 51"). Quintus tries to reinforce Maximus' trust in the Brotherhood's future, emphasizing they are going to make better their "fallen world" and referring to him as "[his] son."

On the ground at Area 51, an exploratory group of the Knights of San Fernando including Maximus enters the central warehouse to explore and begin uncovering its contents, with the lead knight at one point reprimanding [Knight Riley](Riley%20TV%20series.md "Riley (TV series)") for playing hot potato with a plasma grenade. Maximus later overseers the setup of the cold fusion technology, transported to Area 51 from its previous location at Griffith Observatory, as Dane observes him from afar and growing concerned about Quintus' motives.

Maximus goes to wash his face in the barracks, helped by a [young aspirant](Young%20aspirant.md "Young aspirant") whom he in turn educates with his take on the Brotherhood's philosophy. Once alone, Maximus tries to polish his T-60 suit, lingering on the claw marks left by the [yao guai](Yao%20guai%20TV%20series.md "Yao guai (TV series)") attack that killed its previous wearer, [Titus](Titus.md "Titus"). He is interrupted by Dane, who questions their friend about his recent shift in focus and how he is now helping the Brotherhood progress and especially adhering to the tenets of the [Codex](Codex.md "Codex"), which they both once regarded as a joke. Maximus counters that he has realized the struggle of living in the wasteland and now feels he has to make the Brotherhood better. Dane brings up his former goal to have a life with Lucy MacLean, but Maximus says she abandoned him and now he has to fulfill his duties as a Brotherhood knight. Dane drops the matter and changes to the subject of a meeting that Quintus deliberately excluded the Commonwealth chapter from. Dane fears this will have serious repercussions, as the Commonwealth chapter would not let the elder cleric seize power without resistance. The base's alarms blare as Maximus assures his friend that he is going to do what he can to help the Brotherhood's mission if it means preventing another [apocalypse](Great%20War.md "Great War"). Outside the base, the San Fernando chapter witnesses the arrival of three other airships coming from various directions, carrying other chapters of the Brotherhood.

Later on, all four chapters have assembled around a feasting table with Quintus and the three elders representing their respective chapters from the [Grand Canyon](Grand%20Canyon.md "Grand Canyon"), [Yosemite](Yosemite.md "Yosemite"), and [Coronado](Coronado.md "Coronado"). As Quintus props up their chances of defeating the Commonwealth now that his chapter has acquired the cold fusion technology and sways the elders to join his rebellion, Maximus is discreetly warned by Dane to stop [Gnatius](Gnatius.md "Gnatius") and another knight as they are playing with a plasma grenade like a toy.

As the Brotherhood chapters celebrate their unification with a violent spectacle of two knights in T-60 suits boxing each other with the elders watching, Maximus, weary of the barbarism on display, tries to walk away but is intercepted by a [burly man](Coronado%20knight.md "Coronado knight"), one of the [Coronado chapter](Coronado%20Brotherhood%20of%20Steel.md "Coronado Brotherhood of Steel")'s knights. The man asks if he was the one who dealt the killing blow to the "[NCR](New%20California%20Republic.md "New California Republic") bitch" [Moldaver](Lee%20Moldaver.md "Lee Moldaver") (whom he refers to as "Mold Over"). Once Maximus confirms it, the knight challenges him to a fight of their own; Maximus tries to get Quintus to intervene, but the elder silently urges him to accept the challenge. Maximus' request to get his armor is rebuked with the knight stating they will be fighting "bareback:" no power armor, just fists and raw strength and skill.

The two enter the ring formed by the crowd of onlookers, where the knight lands a cheap shot on Maximus while he is removing his jacket. Maximus is brutalized for a while, realizing nobody is going to intervene for him. He uses an opening to punch the knight's crotch, then throws his jacket in his enemy's face to stun him as he retaliates and kicks him back. The knight recovers and decides to increase the threat level by taking out a [combat knife](Combat%20knife%20TV%20series.md "Combat knife (TV series)"). Maximus looks to Quintus again to stop the fight, but the elder leaves him to fend for himself. After dodging a few slashes, Maximus grapples with the knight and maneuvers him into stabbing himself in the abdomen. The two wrestle to the ground, where Maximus finally overpowers his opponent and fatally stabs the knight in the chest. Despite the cheers from the crowd, including the young aspirant from before, and Quintus giving silent approval, Maximus notices Dane giving him a somber gaze before turning and walking away.

Right then, the celebration is interrupted by the arrival of a [vertibird](Vertibird%20TV%20series.md "Vertibird (TV series)") as it flies directly into the warehouse and lands nearby. A man in a heavy coat jumps off and approaches the crowd, revealing himself to be [Paladin Xander Harkness](Xander%20Harkness.md "Xander Harkness"), a liaison from the Commonwealth Brotherhood. He also reveals that the Commonwealth is aware of the West Coast Brotherhood's plans to start a civil war, which Quintus coldly acknowledges. Harkness then notices the bloodied Maximus standing over his fallen opponent and gives him an acknowledging nod.

#### 

in the aftermath of Harkness' arrival, Quintus fields a private meeting with his fellow [clerics](Cleric.md "Cleric") and the [Yosemite elder](McRae.md "McRae"), with **Maximus** in attendance, where the latter reveals that the alliance of the four West Coast chapters has been shaken due to renewed fears of reprisal by the Commonwealth Brotherhood. The elder cleric tries to retain her allegiance by invoking the name and story of [Roger Maxson](Roger%20Maxson.md "Roger Maxson")'s rebellion against the pre-War U.S. that led to the Brotherhood's creation, but the Yosemite elder refutes him and declares her chapter is abandoning the alliance, citing the [Codex](Codex.md "Codex") and its prohibition of violence between brothers, and leaving after a final warning to hand over the cold fusion relic to the Commonwealth. In the silence after, Maximus also tries to use Roger Maxson's story to suggest that they eliminate Xander for their "righteous" cause, but Quintus mocks his shortsightedness since Xander's death would in all likelihood ignite war with the Commonwealth, then furiously dismisses him.

As Maximus walks through the base, angered and conflicted by his thoughts, Xander calls out to him. The paladin remarks on the San Fernando chapter's abundance of clerics, and how his chapter does not use them in a leadership position. He then emphasizes to Maximus that Quintus' plans of rebellion will be nothing but chaos, for both the Brotherhood and the wasteland, and tries to sway the knight to his view of things, saying that soldiers like them, who understand the real cost of a battle, are the ones who lead back in the Commonwealth and make an example for the Brotherhood. Maximus expresses his uncertainty, but Xander reassures him, stating that the current situation is [not the first time](Schism.md "Schism") that the Brotherhood has faced civil war and made room for ["cooler heads"](Arthur%20Maxson.md "Arthur Maxson") to step up and take command. Though Maximus remains reserved about his loyalty to his chapter, he agrees to discuss the topic more with Xander.

Later, Maximus and Xander approach Xander's [vertibird](Vertibird%20TV%20series.md "Vertibird (TV series)"). Maximus questions why they do not have a pilot, but Xander reveals he flies it himself. He offers to let Maximus use his spare suit of T-60 power armor, surprising Maximus that the Commonwealth chapter can grant its members spare power armor. During the flight over the wasteland, the two start talking as Xander lightens the mood by offering to let him take the controls of the vertibird, which the inexperienced Maximus declines. The conversation turns to the subject of Quintus' rebellion as Xander admits that if nothing is done to get him to turn over the relic, both of them will most likely be facing each other in battle in the future. He reveals to Maximus that his arrival was two-fold, as he also came to get the relic because it might prevent the Commonwealth chapter's own collapse back on the [East Coast](East%20Coast.md "East Coast"). Xander emphasizes to Maximus that he will have to be the one to make a choice, but he trusts Maximus' judgment and assures that Maximus himself must believe that too. They are alerted to the vertibird's sensors picking up the power signature of a nearby complex. Maximus wonders why Xander would care about that when it is happening far outside his home territory, but Xander rallies him by saying they have the obligation as members of the Brotherhood to protect the wasteland, which feeds into Maximus' idealism.

The T-60-outfitted Xander and Maximus break into the complex with the power signature they tracked. As they get deeper, they stop upon hearing the stuttering, crackled voice of an [extremely broken Securitron](Extremely%20broken%20Securitron.md "Extremely broken Securitron"). The malfunctioning robot detects them and fires at them with its SMG arm, forcing the two into cover. Xander excitedly pulls out his [super sledge](Super%20sledge%20TV%20series.md "Super sledge (TV series)"), which Maximus does not recognize, and charges into the storm of bullets to attack it directly. The pair work together to disable the robot's weapons, allowing Maximus to punch his gauntlet through the front screen and extract its [fusion core](Fusion%20core.md "Fusion core"). As the two celebrate their victory and Xander offers the use of his super sledge to Maximus, they are drawn to a sound in a nearby room. They walk into the bottling floor housing Thaddeus' cap collecting operation, where Xander notices a [ghoul](Ghoul.md "Ghoul") child, [Perry](Perry.md "Perry") hiding under nearby machinery and demands he come out, then refers to him as a "baby abomination" upon seeing his mutated appearance. Seeing this, Maximus immediately starts to get uneasy, while Xander orders the rest of the children out of hiding.

As the knight tries to reassure the scared children, Xander asks all the ["smooth skin"](Smoothskin.md "Smoothskin") humans to move to one side of the room, separating them from the ghouls. Maximus immediately realizes his intentions and tries to tell him to stand down. They are then interrupted by Thaddeus emerging from his hiding place as he tries to plead for the kids' safety, but Xander remains firm in obeying the [Codex](Codex.md "Codex") and eliminating the "abominations," including Thaddeus. Both Maximus and Thaddeus continue to beg Xander not to attack the children until Maximus makes the decision to swing the super sledge into the back of Xander's helmet, crushing it and killing him instantly. Maximus reveals himself to Thaddeus and laments that he just started a war.

Shortly after killing Xander, Maximus tells Thaddeus he has to help him cover it up from the Brotherhood, right as a vertibird arrives that was tracking them. Despite initial reluctance and claiming he prefers to run away, Thaddeus agrees to help after the knight points out the Brotherhood would discover the kids in the process of looking for him, killing the ghouls and recruiting the humans, if they did not create a distraction. Maximus exits the factory to meet the armored knight, who blows off his excuse of Xander's vertibird running out of fuel and demands to know where the paladin is. Both look on as Thaddeus (disguised as Xander) fumbles trying to get through a normal-sized door, and Maximus sighs knowing how risky his plan is going to be.

When the two are brought back to the airship's flight deck, Maximus is met by a worried Dane saying Quintus is furious with him for going off on his own with the Commonwealth's liaison. Dane then sees "Xander" awkwardly moving around in his power armor and deduces right away that something went wrong, which Maximus confirms that he killed the real Xander and Dane wryly responds that the Commonwealth will most certainly be gunning for the West Coast chapters as a result. Maximus tries to reassure his friend that he has a plan to prevent conflict by killing Quintus and stealing the cold fusion relic, which does not ease Dane's fears at all.

In the meeting room, Maximus is left alone with Quintus once the elder cleric dismisses his subordinates. He figures out that the incident with Xander went wrong, correctly deducing that the paladin was killed, which Maximus confirms, and that the knight intends to kill him too as Maximus draws his [laser pistol](Laser%20pistol%20TV%20series.md "Laser pistol (TV series)") and aims it at the elder cleric. Quintus however is unfazed, walking right up to Maximus, who hesitates to shoot him, while lambasting the knight for his weak will and failure to think about what his actions would do to the Brotherhood and the future they are building, questioning the fearful Maximus what led him to turn against the man who saved him from a rough life in the wasteland.

Quintus is perturbed to hear Maximus explain how he killed Xander to stop him from killing kids, then is shocked to find out the kids Maximus protected were ghouls. However, his shock quickly turns to anger as he launches into a tirade about his subordinate protecting "abominations" in defiance of the Codex's tenets, spitefully telling Maximus he should have never helped him. The elder cleric draws a [10mm pistol](10mm%20pistol%20TV%20series.md "10mm pistol (TV series)") from a hidden drawer and tries to attack Maximus, loudly decrying him as a "heretic," but fails to hit him before the ex-knight escapes the room and chains the door shut, trapping Quintus inside.

In the hallway, Maximus realizes that war has already broken out as the chapters are fighting with each other. He sees Dane running in and reveals with sadness that he could not bring himself to kill Quintus, but Dane consoles their friend saying he should not be sad over refusing to take a life. Dane reveals they stole the relic that kicked off the surrounding battle and places it in Maximus' hands, telling him to take it and run far away from the Brotherhood. As he goes to leave with Thaddeus, Maximus questions what Dane will do, which they respond is that they'll survive like they always have. The two flee from the base with the relic in Maximus' hands as the airship fleet opens fire on each other and sends one crashing into the desert behind them.

## 

Maximus is someone who needs something to believe in to keep him going. He is said to be someone who yearns to cling onto something powerful, such as the Brotherhood because it makes him feel more capable than he is on his own. He will do anything to further the Brotherhood's goals of bringing law and order to the wasteland. He is experiencing internal conflict and is said to be someone who has a war going on within himself. His biggest fear is weakness and he seeks power as a means to shield himself from the violence and dangers of the Wasteland.

Maximus has a tendency to think in the moment and not think critically in his situation or plan ahead. For instance disguising himself as Knight Titus rather than simply claiming he fell in battle, which leads to the assignment of his rival Thaddeus as Squire necessitating that he keep up the ruse. Or when he assumes that Lucy is to be executed in Vault 4 and he rushes to his power armor when she had simply been exiled. This could also be inferred from his belief that the Great War and its atomic bombings refers to the destruction of Shady Sands, and his misconceptions regarding sexual intercourse.

He also displays poor charisma on multiple occasions, such as failing to convince a repairwoman to fix a part at a cheaper price, or to convince Thaddeus to work with him on his impersonation of Knight Titus, or trying to proposition Lucy.

Despite holding up the Brotherhoods ideals, he admits his primary motivation for fighting for the Brotherhood is to 'hurt the people that hurt me', and displays an eagerness to escalate to violence. He deals with a lot of pent up anger as an initiate when doing latrine duties, refuses to de-escalate crossing paths with a pair of wastelander cannibals, and contemplates harming Thaddeus when he has been assigned his squire (under the guise of Knight Titus). While seeing Thaddeus grovel before him as squire, he reconsiders but continues to demean him with pointless tasks until Thaddeus reveals sorrow at Maximus' (informed) passing. However Maximus is still willing to attempt to murder Thaddeus, crushing his foot, when he fails to convince Thaddeus to maintain the fiction of his assumed identity, despite both having saved the others life in an encounter with a Gulper.

Perhaps correspondingly when shown the comfort, safety, and relative luxury of Vault life in Vault 4, he becomes willing to settle down and integrate into the community, abandoning his post with the Brotherhood and his mission. However when he thinks that Lucy's life is in danger he goes to intervene knowing that he will no longer be able to stay in Vault 4. He likewise displays a callousness to the survival of Vault 4 having stolen their Fusion Core and wanting to keep it to maintain his T-60 Power Armor after having been banished from the Vault, but Lucy still manages to convince him to return it.

However Maximus also demonstrates an altruistic side, idealizing Brotherhood Knights and their role in protecting the wasteland and being gravely disappointed by Knight Titus being unable to meet that standard. He intervenes when seeing one wastelander trying to kill a snake-oil salesman, and releases him when the confrontation passes. He also steps in to save Lucy from the Ghoul in Filly, shielding her with his power armor.
