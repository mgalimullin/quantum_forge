# Steph Harper

**Stephanie "Steph" Harper** is a resident of [Vault 33](Vault%2033.md "Vault 33") in the [*Fallout* TV series](Fallout%20TV%20series.md "Fallout (TV series)") and former pre-War [Vault-Tec](Vault-Tec%20Corporation.md "Vault-Tec Corporation") employee from [Vault 31](Vault%2031.md "Vault 31"). She later is appointed [overseer](Overseer.md "Overseer") of [Vault 32](Vault%2032.md "Vault 32").

## Contents

## 

Born on February 15, 2045, Steph was a citizen of [Canada](Canada.md "Canada") prior to its [annexation](United%20States%20annexation%20of%20Canada.md "United States annexation of Canada") by the [United States of America](United%20States%20of%20America.md "United States of America") in the 2070s. She was subsequently issued a U.S. temporary status ID listing her as a resident of the territory of Canada.

Steph was a Vault-Tec employee and a graduate of [Bud Askins](Bud%20Askins.md "Bud Askins")' executive assistant training program "[Bud's Buds](Buds%20Buds.md "Bud's Buds")," and was selected by Bud to participate in his Vault experiment: The creation of super-managers who would define the future of humanity once the [Great War](Great%20War.md "Great War") wiped the surface clean. They would be kept in [cryostasis](Cryonics.md "Cryonics"), periodically thawed out, and transferred to Vault 32 or 33 to manage the dwellers and participate in controlled breeding (eugenics) under Askins' supervision.

Reactivated sometime before 2294, Stephanie was transferred to [Vault 33](Vault%2033.md "Vault 33") and married [Bert](Bert%20TV%20series.md "Bert (TV series)") on March 17, [2294](/wiki/Timeline#2294 "Timeline"). She is fond of Bert, though her memory of their wedding night is spoiled by Bert's lack of stamina in bed. Regardless, she developed feelings for him (perhaps spurred by his love of shoe maintenance), and by 2296, they were expecting their first child. Steph kept active, participating in the Young Pipefitter's Association and keeping on top of her regular Vault duties.

Stephanie became [Lucy MacLean](Lucy%20MacLean.md "Lucy MacLean")'s best friend over the years. The two hoped to raise their children together once Lucy was married to a dweller from [Vault 32](Vault%2032.md "Vault 32") during the Triennial Trade in 2296.

## 

### 

#### 

Steph helps [Lucy MacLean](Lucy%20MacLean.md "Lucy MacLean") get ready for her wedding and shows Lucy where everyone writes their names and the date of their marriage. She and Lucy discuss the upcoming marriage, and Steph expresses excitement about raising their future children. Steph and other vault dwellers accompany Lucy and Hank to the connecting entrance between Vault 33 and 32, where Lucy and [Monty](Monty%20TV%20series.md "Monty (TV series)") first meet.

During the raid on Vault 33, Steph's husband Bert is killed in the cornfield. Steph cradles Bert's corpse before flying into a rage at the nearest raider. She is stabbed in the right eye with a fork during that fight, but she then uses a submachine gun to kill the raider who stabbed her before firing at several others. However, she is later captured by [Lee Moldaver](Lee%20Moldaver.md "Lee Moldaver")'s men, along with [Chet](Chet%20TV%20series.md "Chet (TV series)") and several other vault dwellers, and are held hostage when Hank and then Lucy confront them at the entrance to Vault 32. She escapes from the situation along with everyone else after Moldaver leaves with [Hank MacLean](Hank%20MacLean.md "Hank MacLean") and detonates a bomb at the connecting entrance between Vault 33 and 32.

#### 

After the Vault decides what to do about the raiders, Steph tells Norm she agrees with his proposal to execute the raiders, as she is still vengeful over Bert's death.

#### 

Steph is shown to have recovered from the fork in her eye, with an eyepatch over her wound. She turns to [Chet](Chet%20TV%20series.md "Chet (TV series)") for comfort after the death of her husband Bert. She goes to his apartment, gives him Bert's clothes, makes him try them on, and starts kissing him before her water breaks and she goes into labor.

#### 

Norm asks how Vault 31 is different. Steph replies, "What did your dad tell you?" which hints that she does not want to say anything to contradict Hank's claims. Steph claims she does not know, but says the mashed potatoes were better.

#### 

Steph discovers she will be staying in Vault 32 and moves in with Chet and her child. Before they leave, Betty announces that Steph has been granted the position of overseer of Vault 32.

### 

#### 

In Vault 32, Stephanie is still settling into her new office and new role as overseer. She is seen trying to figure out her terminal while [Davey](Davey%20TV%20series.md "Davey (TV series)") pesters her about being confused with Vault 32's layout. Exasperated, Steph asks what Davey wants her to do, and he eventually settles on asking her to commission a feasibility study for new signage before leaving.

Chet soon enters with Steph's child, asking if she will assign him a role. Steph refuses, delegating childcare responsibilities to him, though he protests that the child is not his and he is not married to her. Disgruntled, but in a measured manner, Chet points out his situation and specifically mentions that the child still doesn't have a name, mentioning that their neighbors now call the child Chet Jr. Steph, peeling and ripping apart an [orange](Orange.md "Orange") with a [Bowie knife](Bowie%20knife%20TV%20series.md "Bowie knife (TV series)"), accepts the name without a second thought, but Chet tries to protest, saying that the name Chet Jr. evokes unpleasant associations with his father's death during the Vault 33 famine of 2277. Steph suddenly stabs the knife into her desk, making it clear that she's not in the mood for an argument, and Chet reluctantly agrees to the name Chet Jr. Finally, Steph asks if Chet knows how to use computers, but he says that's [Norm](Norm%20MacLean.md "Norm MacLean")'s specialty. Stephanie decides to send a message to Vault 31, but finds that inter-Vault communications are unavailable (soon revealed to be the result of sabotage by Norm in Vault 31).

#### 

In the inter-Vault space, Steph meets with Betty, who investigated Vault 31 following the loss of communications. Betty informs her of Bud Askins' death and the seeming abandonment of Vault 31, with Norm being nowhere in sight. While initially surprised, Steph quickly comes to view this as an opportunity to consolidate her own leadership. When Betty asks Steph to have Vault 32 send some of its water supplies to Vault 33, Steph refuses, citing the needs of her Vault and her own child. She also rebuffs Betty's point that she should be more cooperative since Betty appointed her, hinting that she has lived through much worse circumstances than Betty had in the past. In the end, she agrees to consider trading water to Vault 33 if Betty agrees to bring her Hank MacLean's pre-War keepsake box. After being told that there won't be a Vault 33 if she doesn't help, she flips Betty's previous argument and tells her that the "experiment is over" and that they have to make their own decisions since [Vault-Tec](Vault-Tec%20Corporation.md "Vault-Tec Corporation") won't be here to save them.

Unbeknownst to either overseer, the end of their conversation was overheard by [Woody Thomas](Woody%20Thomas.md "Woody Thomas"). Following the Vault's guidelines to the letter, Woody meets Steph in her own apartment to report her own violation of the Vault-Tec handbook's policies (her meeting in the inter-Vault space), while Chet listens in from nearby as he folds laundry. Steph thanks Woody and claims she will "escalate" a report to investigate herself, and when Woody protests, she reinforces her authority by telling him to call her Overseer Harper. At the same time, Chet finds a secret compartment in one of Steph's drawers which contains her wallet; Chet opens it to find a pre-War U.S. temporary ID card, revealing to him that Steph is both from the pre-War world and Canadian.

Steph shuts down the discussion, and Chet puts her ID back while Woody heads for the door. Chet and Woody briefly make small talk, while Steph considers the situation with a troubled look. She then forces her way between Chet and Woody to open the door and make Woody leave. Naively, Woody further asks Steph about the experiment that she mentioned to Betty, which she downplays as "overseer stuff." When the door closes, she glares at Chet, who gives her a strained smile; Steph smiles in return.

## 

Steph is initially shown to be a typically upbeat woman who cares about Lucy and is excited about them raising their children together. When Bert dies, she seems to fall into grief and appears to use Chet to cope with Bert's passing. However, she is later shown to be somewhat cold after being assigned overseer of [Vault 32](Vault%2032.md "Vault 32"), not giving any thought to even naming her newborn child, using Chet simply for childcare, and forcefully shutting down any attempts by him to protest.

Steph is also shown to be highly vengeful and calculating, wishing death on the raiders who killed Bert. After the raiders are mysteriously killed because someone slipped rat poison into their food, Steph is not concerned at all about the occurrence. The actual culprit behind the raiders' deaths is not revealed. When Vault 33 begins to run out of water, she attempts to use it for her own gain, effectively holding the Vault hostage unless she is provided with Hank MacLean's keepsake box.
