# Stimpak

A **stimpak** (occasionally referred to as a **stimpack**, **stim pack** or simply **stims**), short for **stimulation delivery package**, is a type of hand-held medication used for boosting the body's own regenerative properties.

## Contents

## 

A wonder of pre-[War](Great%20War.md "Great War") science, stimpaks are typically syringes filled with a mixture of healing agents and stimulants, allowing the user to boost their own body's natural regenerative functions. In most cases, it leads to an almost instantaneous regeneration of wounds. The syringe is equipped with a gauge for quickly identifying the state of the contents and can be used either in an intravenous or intramuscular fashion. Although now commonly used for healing bullet holes and stab wounds, stimpaks were employed professionally through [Auto-Docs](Auto-Doc.md "Auto-Doc") (in conjunction with [Med-X](Med-X.md "Med-X") and other painkillers) for postoperative care and recovery, and occasionally see this prescribed use in more advanced societies. Despite the radical differences in physiology and biology, stimpaks are even capable of being used to treat injuries for animals such as [canines](Dog.md "Dog"), remaining just as effective as for [humans](Human.md "Human"). Wastelanders speak highly of their efficacy, often expressing gratitude that millions were produced before the Great War, still abundant several centuries after.

Stimpaks can also be used to treat physical conditions, such as a heart condition, but only temporarily. However, the basic stimpak is designed for use on moderate or severe wounds, as the soup of healing medication is ineffective at treating major trauma. A stimpak also does not prevent scarring from excessive third-degree burns or additional extensive scarring as it simply boosts natural regenerative functions. Expiration is a concern for those who use stimpaks, but other than giving a weaker boost to healing, they function just as an unexpired stimpak. Chemists with sufficient knowledge can create new stimpaks with [Broc flower](Broc%20flower.md "Broc flower") and [Xander root](Xander%20root.md "Xander root"), which are also used to make a common [tribal remedy](Healing%20powder.md "Healing powder") similar to a stimpak.

More advanced variants of the stimpak were developed. The super stimpak is the most common variant, containing an additional vial of more drastic healing chems, dramatically increasing the healing effect. However, the healing effect will eventually cause minor trauma to the tissues healed due to the potency of the drug.

Another advanced recipe was created by a scientist named [VanTudle](VanTudle.md "VanTudle"), though its specifics are unknown.

[Health dispensers](Health%20dispenser.md "Health dispenser") used in the [Anchorage Reclamation simulation](Anchorage%20Reclamation%20simulation.md "Anchorage Reclamation simulation"), manufactured by [Lee Rapid Pharmaceuticals](Lee%20Rapid%20Pharmaceuticals.md "Lee Rapid Pharmaceuticals"), have an identical delivery method, which hints that stimpaks are also a product of the company developed from previous health dispensers used in the [Sino-American War](Sino-American%20War.md "Sino-American War"). The fictional nature of the simulation should also be taken into consideration, however, as it is possible Lee Rapid and their dispensers never existed.

### 

Gameplay articles: [Fallout](Stimpak%20Fallout.md "Stimpak (Fallout)"), [Fallout 2](Stimpak%20Fallout%202.md "Stimpak (Fallout 2)"), [Fallout 3](Stimpak%20Fallout%203.md "Stimpak (Fallout 3)"), [Fallout: New Vegas](Stimpak%20Fallout%20New%20Vegas.md "Stimpak (Fallout: New Vegas)"), [Fallout 4](Stimpak%20Fallout%204.md "Stimpak (Fallout 4)"), [Fallout 76](Stimpak%20Fallout%2076.md "Stimpak (Fallout 76)"), [Fallout Tactics](Stimpak%20Fallout%20Tactics.md "Stimpak (Fallout Tactics)"), [Fallout: Brotherhood of Steel](Stimpak%20Fallout%20Brotherhood%20of%20Steel.md "Stimpak (Fallout: Brotherhood of Steel)"), [Fallout Shelter](Stimpak%20Fallout%20Shelter.md "Stimpak (Fallout Shelter)"), [Fallout: The Board Game](Stimpak%20Fallout%20The%20Board%20Game.md "Stimpak (Fallout: The Board Game)")

A stimpak is an autoinjector loaded with a variety of healing medications and stimulants. By injecting the stimpak, the user drastically increases their own recuperative functions and gains lost Hit Points almost instantly.

### 

Gameplay articles: [Fallout](Stimpak%20Fallout.md "Stimpak (Fallout)"), [Fallout 2](Stimpak%20Fallout%202.md "Stimpak (Fallout 2)"), [Fallout: New Vegas](Stimpak%20Fallout%20New%20Vegas.md "Stimpak (Fallout: New Vegas)"), [Fallout 76](Stimpak%20super.md "Stimpak: super"), [Fallout Tactics](Stimpak%20Fallout%20Tactics.md "Stimpak (Fallout Tactics)"), [Fallout: Brotherhood of Steel](Stimpak%20Fallout%20Brotherhood%20of%20Steel.md "Stimpak (Fallout: Brotherhood of Steel)")

The advanced version of the stimpak boasts stronger healing drugs, delivered both via the syringe and a tributary ampule, both connected to a leather strap allowing to securely affix the contraption to the injection area. The analogue gauge displaying information is on top of the ampule. These drugs were intended for use in managing chronic damage and suppressing pain. However, the powerful nature of the drugs used means that the delayed shock causes a numbing after-effect, dealing a minor amount of damage to the user after some time. It is a fairly rare item in the wasteland, usually only manufactured by high-tech settlements like [Vault City](Vault%20City.md "Vault City").

### 

Gameplay article: [Lonesome Road](Auto-inject%20stimpak.md "Auto-inject stimpak")

The auto-inject (super) stimpak is a modified stimpak which monitors vital signs, allowing for timely emergency treatment. It is used automatically from the player character's inventory when their Hit Points drop below 50% (25% [auto-inject super stimpak](Auto-inject%20super%20stimpak.md "Auto-inject super stimpak")) of the current maximum level.

### 

Gameplay article: [Wild Appalachia](Stimpak%20diffuser.md "Stimpak diffuser")

The stimpak diffuser releases a healing gas into the air which heals both the player character and their nearby teammates.

### 

Gameplay article: [Fallout 4](Curies%20healthpak.md "Curie's healthpak")

This variant of the stimpak was developed by the [synth](Synth.md "Synth") researcher [Curie](Curie.md "Curie") in [the Commonwealth](The%20Commonwealth.md "The Commonwealth") in [2287](/wiki/Timeline#2287 "Timeline"). Originating from a genetically bred strain of [mutfruit](Mutfruit%20Fallout%204.md "Mutfruit (Fallout 4)"), known as "strain 17," the healthpak utilizes the plant's naturally produced sugar compound to stimulate platelet production in the body, enhancing the regenerative qualities of the original stimpak formula and its ability to rapidly close open wounds. The only obstacle to its mass production is the fact that wild mutfruits are largely unviable due to evolving adaptations for living in the irradiated soil of the wasteland: they produce toxins as pest control that dilute the sugar compound and dull the efficiency of the produced formula. According to Curie, the only way to efficiently harvest the sugar compound is through careful cultivation of strain 17 in a fully sterile lab environment to ensure purity, though with very few locations around the Commonwealth able to provide this, results are slow to come.

### 

Gameplay article: [Nuclear Winter](Stimpak%20Nuclear%20Winter.md "Stimpak (Nuclear Winter)")

This stimpak has not been stored in proper conditions and is thus not as effective centuries after it was manufactured.

### 

Gameplay article: [Operation: Anchorage](Health%20dispenser.md "Health dispenser")

Health dispensers are stationary health stations found in the [Anchorage Reclamation simulation](Anchorage%20Reclamation%20simulation.md "Anchorage Reclamation simulation"). Although immobile, they are reusable and more powerful than regular stimpaks, as they restore health to full no matter how low it is.

## 

### 

Gameplay article: [Fallout Tactics](Ultra%20stimpak.md "Ultra stimpak")

The ultra stimpak is an advanced version of super stimpak and a highly powerful healing chem, providing immediate healing of major wounds and boosting the body's regenerative functions over time. Due to the very powerful nature of the chemicals used, ultra stimpaks will cause damage after a period of time.

The ultra stimpak is made up of two regular stimpaks, with one stimpak connected to a wrist belt for immediate injection, and one connected to a breathing mask, allowing for the healing drug to be administrated as gas.

## 

The term "stimpack" appears in other games, such as *[StarCraft](https://starcraft.fandom.com/wiki/stimpack "w:c:starcraft:stimpack")* and *[Doom](https://doom.fandom.com/wiki/stimpack "w:c:doom:stimpack")*. In the former, it serves a role roughly similar to [Psycho](Psycho.md "Psycho"), boosting damage and speed at the cost of health, while in the latter it's a straightforward healing item.
