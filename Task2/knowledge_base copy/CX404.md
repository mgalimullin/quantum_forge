# CX404

**CX404**, also nicknamed **Four** and later **Dogmeat**, is a [dog](Dog.md "Dog") that appears in the [*Fallout* TV series](Fallout%20TV%20series.md "Fallout (TV series)").

## Contents

### Quick Answers

**What role did CX404 play in Siggi Wilzig's research in the Fallout show?**

CX404 was part of an Enclave behavioral research project undertaken by Siggi Wilzig at the Enclave research colony, separately from his cold fusion research.

Provided by: Community



**How did CX404 become known as Dogmeat in the Fallout TV series?**

CX404 is a reference to the original Dogmeat, who was in turn a reference to the canine companions of Mad Max 2 and A Boy and His Dog.

Provided by: Community



**What kind of dog is CX404, also known as Dogmeat, in the Fallout TV show?**

In the Fallout TV series, CX404, also referred to as Dogmeat, is portrayed by Lana 5, a Belgian Malinois. Lana 5 has also featured in the 2022 ''Dog'' movie.

Provided by: Community

## 

CX404 was originally an experimental dog raised by the [Enclave](Enclave.md "Enclave") at a [research facility](Enclave%20research%20colony.md "Enclave research colony") in an unknown location some years before [2296](/wiki/Timeline#2296 "Timeline"). She was saved by [Siggi Wilzig](Siggi%20Wilzig.md "Siggi Wilzig") from incineration due to being underweight at birth, and Wilzig subsequently raised her. She aided and accompanied Wilzig during his escape from the Enclave, but was later separated from him at [Filly](Filly.md "Filly") near the ruins of [Los Angeles](Los%20Angeles.md "Los Angeles"). She has since been accompanying [the Ghoul](The%20Ghoul.md "The Ghoul"), as well as [Lucy MacLean](Lucy%20MacLean.md "Lucy MacLean") later on.

## 

### 

#### 

In a flashback, CX404's history at the Enclave research facility is seen. As a newborn puppy, she was slated for incineration for being underweight, but Siggi Wilzig rescued her by falsifying her weight. He kept her hidden in an alcove behind a chalkboard in his laboratory while training her and working on [Lee Moldaver](Lee%20Moldaver.md "Lee Moldaver")'s [cold fusion](Cold%20fusion.md "Cold fusion") technology. When Wilzig and CX404 were discovered by an [administrator](Enclave%20administrator.md "Enclave administrator"), CX404 killed the man, though not before he pulled an alarm. The pair were forced to flee the base, managing to evade the fire of an [automated turret](Machinegun%20turret%20TV%20series.md "Machinegun turret (TV series)"). Afterward, a bounty was placed on Wilzig's head, CX404 being listed as a companion.

Some time later in their journey, they have their first encounter with [Lucy MacLean](Lucy%20MacLean.md "Lucy MacLean") at her makeshift campsite somewhere in greater [Los Angeles](Los%20Angeles.md "Los Angeles"), where CX404 attacks and devours a [radroach](Radroach%20TV%20series.md "Radroach (TV series)") that was sneaking up on Lucy. They later briefly stop in [the Wilds](The%20Wilds.md "The Wilds"), where CX404 wanders into a nearby [yao guai](Yao%20guai%20TV%20series.md "Yao guai (TV series)") den, retrieving a severed human hand and bringing it to Wilzig. They manage to escape again.

CX404 remains with Wilzig up until they reach [Filly](Filly.md "Filly"). When Wilzig gets his foot shot off by [the Ghoul](The%20Ghoul.md "The Ghoul"), she attacks the Ghoul in turn but he stabs and incapacitates her. After Lucy helps Wilzig escape, the Ghoul takes pity on the injured dog and brings her into [Ma June](Ma%20June.md "Ma June")'s shop. He uses a [stimpak](Stimpak%20TV%20series.md "Stimpak (TV series)") to heal her and starts using her nose to track Wilzig down for the bounty.

#### 

CX404 continues traveling with the Ghoul, when they locate Wilzig's headless body after Lucy beheaded him at his request. The two later catch up to Lucy near the flooded [Hollywood](Hollywood.md "Hollywood") Boulevard. The dog is distressed and barks in protest at the Ghoul's cruelty in using Lucy as bait to lure in the [gulper](Gulper%20TV%20series.md "Gulper (TV series)") that has swallowed Wilzig's head. CX404 is left behind at the lake after Lucy destroys the Ghoul's supply of [chems](Chems.md "Chems") while defending herself, and he takes Lucy away with him to sell her to [Snip Snip](Snip%20Snip.md "Snip Snip")'s handlers.

#### 

[Thaddeus](Thaddeus.md "Thaddeus") encounters CX404 sometime after he abandons [Maximus](Maximus.md "Maximus") and obtains Wilzig's head. The dog follows him, pawing at his catch until they reach an abandoned [Red Rocket station](Red%20Rocket%20TV%20series.md "Red Rocket (TV series)"). The squire verbally belittles her before locking her in a [Nuka-Cola](Nuka-Cola.md "Nuka-Cola") cooler to stop her from following him.

Later, the Ghoul finds his way to the gas station as well and rescues CX404 from her prison, causing her to become attached and start following him permanently. While they are camping in the night, the Ghoul renames her "Dogmeat" while sadly remarking that though he has a soft spot for her, her presence is no replacement for [Roosevelt](Roosevelt.md "Roosevelt") from his pre-[War](Great%20War.md "Great War") days.

#### 

The Ghoul leaves Dogmeat behind before heading into the [Griffith Observatory](Griffith%20Observatory.md "Griffith Observatory"), where he fights past the [Brotherhood of Steel](Brotherhood%20of%20Steel.md "Brotherhood of Steel") and attacks [Hank MacLean](Hank%20MacLean.md "Hank MacLean"). She later appears in the cemetery, accompanying Lucy and the Ghoul on their journey to find the escaped Hank.

### 

#### 

Dogmeat continues to accompany Lucy and the Ghoul as they cross the [Mojave Wasteland](Mojave%20Wasteland.md "Mojave Wasteland") heading to [New Vegas](New%20Vegas.md "New Vegas") in pursuit of Hank.

In [Novac](Novac.md "Novac"), she stays with Lucy while the Vault dweller is positioned in the [Dinky the T-Rex](Dinky%20the%20T-Rex.md "Dinky the T-Rex") statue during the fight with the [Great Khans](Great%20Khans.md "Great Khans").

Later on, Dogmeat runs ahead as Lucy and the Ghoul reach the [Starlight Drive-In Theatre](Starlight%20Drive-in%20Theatre%20TV%20series.md "Starlight Drive-in Theatre (TV series)"), where Lucy notices her finding and going through the open blast door entrance to [Vault 24](Vault%2024.md "Vault 24").

#### 

Dogmeat hears the cries of the [wounded woman](Wounded%20woman.md "Wounded woman") inside the ruins of [Affordable Al's Discount Hospital](Affordable%20Als%20Discount%20Hospital.md "Affordable Al's Discount Hospital") first and is followed by Lucy and the Ghoul. She is also the first one to notice the baby [radscorpions](Radscorpion%20TV%20series.md "Radscorpion (TV series)") scuttling through the ventilation and runs out of the room right before her companions are attacked by both them and the mature radscorpion.

## 

CX404 appears in the [*Fallout* TV series](Fallout%20TV%20series.md "Fallout (TV series)"). She also appears in *[Fallout Shelter](Fallout%20Shelter.md "Fallout Shelter")* and in the *Hollywood Heroes* set of miniatures for *[Fallout: Wasteland Warfare](Fallout%20Wasteland%20Warfare.md "Fallout: Wasteland Warfare")* and *[Fallout: Factions](Fallout%20Factions.md "Fallout: Factions")*, as well as the *[Fallout 2d20: NPC Pack - Hollywood Heroes](Fallout%202d20%20NPC%20Pack%20-%20Hollywood%20Heroes.md "Fallout 2d20: NPC Pack - Hollywood Heroes")* expansion pack for *[Fallout: The Roleplaying Game](Fallout%20The%20Roleplaying%20Game.md "Fallout: The Roleplaying Game")*.

## 

* Three dogs are known to have portrayed CX404: [Lana 5](Lana%205.md "Lana 5") (from Anchor Pet Supply), [Nunaya Business de la Forge](Nunaya%20Business%20de%20la%20Forge.md "Nunaya Business de la Forge"), and [Gotcha de la Forge](Gotcha%20de%20la%20Forge.md "Gotcha de la Forge") (both from La Forge Malinois).
* According to La Forge Malinois, Nunaya and Gotcha acted as CX404 from the end of "The Head" onwards in [season 1](Fallout%20TV%20series%20season%201.md "Fallout TV series season 1"). This indicates Lana 5 may have been used in "The Target" and the first half of "The Head." Nunaya was reportedly used in promotional posters and the aforementioned episodes following "The Head," while Gotcha was used for some minor scenes, to a lesser degree than Nunaya. However, no specific credits are given for any of the dogs in the series itself.
  + Gotcha previously appeared in season 5 of [Jonathan Nolan](Jonathan%20Nolan.md "Jonathan Nolan")'s series *[Person of Interest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Person_of_Interest_(TV_series) "wikipedia:Person of Interest (TV series)")* as the dog [Bear](https://personofinterest.fandom.com/wiki/Bear "w:c:personofinterest:Bear"), alongside [Michael Emerson](Michael%20Emerson.md "Michael Emerson") as Harold Finch. Nunaya is Gotcha's offspring.
  + Lana 5 and Nunaya are female dogs, aligning with CX404 also being female, while Gotcha is a male dog.
* Like in season 1, the dogs portraying CX404/Dogmeat are not listed in the episode credits for [season 2](Fallout%20TV%20series%20season%202.md "Fallout TV series season 2"), either. However, Lana 5 is identified in Prime Video's X-Ray feature as the actor for Dogmeat during season 2.
