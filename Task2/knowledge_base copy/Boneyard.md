# Los Angeles

**Los Angeles**, abbreviated as **LA**, was one of the largest cities in the [United States](United%20States.md "United States"). Located in **Los Angeles County** in the pre-[War](Great%20War.md "Great War") state of [California](California.md "California"), it was home to major corporations across various industry sectors, including entertainment ([California Crest Studios](California%20Crest%20Studios.md "California Crest Studios")) and defense ([Vault-Tec Corporation](Vault-Tec%20Corporation.md "Vault-Tec Corporation")).

Following the war, the ruins of Los Angeles became known as the **Angel's Boneyard**, which was subsequently incorporated into the [New California Republic](New%20California%20Republic.md "New California Republic") as part of the **state of Los Angeles**.

## Contents

## 

### 

By the 21st century, Los Angeles was a huge, thriving city on the West Coast of the [United States of America](United%20States%20of%20America.md "United States of America") that exemplified the American Dream. By the mid-21st century, Los Angeles featured multiple districts with skyscrapers, a robust network of monorails connecting the vast city, and was home to numerous prominent companies. Film companies such as [California Crest Studios](California%20Crest%20Studios.md "California Crest Studios") had their main studios here in [Hollywood](Hollywood.md "Hollywood"), the film capital of America, producing blockbusters like *[The Man from Deadhorse](The%20Man%20from%20Deadhorse.md "The Man from Deadhorse")*, while the [Vault-Tec Corporation](Vault-Tec%20Corporation.md "Vault-Tec Corporation") had its original headquarters in the southern part of the city, beneath which it constructed the first [demonstration Vault](Los%20Angeles%20Vault.md "Los Angeles Vault"), winning the government bid for [Project Safehouse](Project%20Safehouse.md "Project Safehouse"). Subsequently open to the public, the Vault drove public enthusiasm and a junk bond drive to provide funds for constructing a nationwide network of Vaults.

Vault-Tec would eventually set up numerous Vaults across the greater Los Angeles area, including [Vault 4](Vault%204.md "Vault 4"), a refuge for scientists established beneath [Hawthorne Medical Laboratories](Hawthorne%20Medical%20Laboratories.md "Hawthorne Medical Laboratories") (a division of the corporation), and the [Three Vaults](Three%20Vaults.md "Three Vaults") ([31](Vault%2031.md "Vault 31"), [32](Vault%2032.md "Vault 32"), and [33](Vault%2033.md "Vault 33"), forming an experimental network), alongside the still-functioning original demonstration Vault. However, as Vault-Tec's influence grew, tapping stars like [Cooper Howard](Cooper%20Howard.md "Cooper Howard") to promote its products, opposition to the company and the increasingly ruthless American system grew. While nowhere near as radical as the opposition in [West Virginia](West%20Virginia.md "West Virginia"), which culminated in the brief, but destructive [automation riots](Automation%20riots.md "Automation riots"), there was nonetheless rising discontent and ostracism towards actors and figures associated with the company, Howard in particular. The entertainment companies retaliated by firings, and the script writer for *[The Man from Deadhorse](The%20Man%20from%20Deadhorse.md "The Man from Deadhorse")* was summarily dismissed as an alleged communist. With organized opposition difficult, people like [Lee Moldaver](Lee%20Moldaver.md "Lee Moldaver") preferred to operate smaller dissident groups, fomenting dissent and in her case, looking for a way to oppose Vault-Tec.

However, for anyone who toed the line, Los Angeles could be the American Dream come true. While Vault-Tec leadership plotted, with its [Southern California headquarters](Vault-Tec%20Industries%20headquarters%20Southern%20California.md "Vault-Tec Industries headquarters (Southern California)") hosting a secret conference to subvert Project Safehouse for the ends of the corporate elite, idyllic birthday parties still took place and the lucky rich could enjoy luxurious homes with stunning views of the Los Angeles skyline in high-end neighborhoods such as [Hollywood Hills](Hollywood%20Hills.md "Hollywood Hills"). On October 23, 2077, they received a front seat to the city's destruction, as [nuclear weapons](Nuclear%20weapons.md "Nuclear weapons") struck the city, with at least four falling in the densely built-up area from Hollywood to Long Beach, impacting downtown, mid-city, Koreatown, and Hollywood.

### 

The initial attacks left the city decimated, with much of the remaining population dying in the following weeks and months due to [radiation](Radiation.md "Radiation") poisoning, disease, famine, and collapsing social order. The city's skeletal skyscrapers, twisted and scorched by nuclear fire, were surrounded by skeletons of the dead, eventually gaining the name [Angel's Boneyard](Boneyard%20Fallout.md "Boneyard (Fallout)").

The [demonstration Vault](Los%20Angeles%20Vault.md "Los Angeles Vault") and other Vaults in the area were sealed and functioned as intended. The former opened fifteen years later, with the survivors founding [Adytum](Adytum.md "Adytum") in the Boneyard. Sometime after the sealing, the technocrats of [Vault 4](Vault%204.md "Vault 4") were also deposed, with the survivors of their experiments rising up against the scientists and killing them, establishing a communal society open to receiving survivors from the surface. However, no major population center emerged in the sea of ruins, with human habitation limited to a number of isolated communities, mostly dividing the ruins between themselves as factions warred for territory. Adytum remained a small, fortified semi-industrial town, with the location of its Vault fading from memory.

Over the years, the main power groups became the [Blades](Blades.md "Blades"), the [Rippers](Rippers.md "Rippers"), and the [Adytum](Adytum.md "Adytum"), with its [Regulators](Regulators%20Fallout.md "Regulators (Fallout)") providing the town with a measure of protection. The Rippers and Blades remained at each other's throats for decades, fighting a destructive war of attrition that ended up breaking both. Around 2131, the [Gun Runners](Gun%20Runners.md "Gun Runners") came to the Hub, originally attempting to establish their own turf, but quickly established a lucrative trade in weapons and ammunition, selling second-hand weapons, purchases from the Hub, and eventually own designs.

Sometime in the 2150s, the [Followers of the Apocalypse](Followers%20of%20the%20Apocalypse.md "Followers of the Apocalypse") formed out of survivors that migrated north away from [San Diego](San%20Diego.md "San Diego"), which became uninhabitable due to radioactive contamination drifting off from [Great Glow](Great%20Glow.md "Great Glow"). The Followers established their stronghold in the ruins of a great library in the center of the city, fortifying and restoring it to spread the word of peace. They established a reputation for being polite, helpful, but unaccustomed to the ways of the world.

The last group to emerge in the Boneyard were the [Children of the Cathedral](Children%20of%20the%20Cathedral.md "Children of the Cathedral"). They originated from a doomsday cult formed by [Morpheus](Morpheus.md "Morpheus") after he left the Rippers after ten years, and their development started when the Master's scouts found them in 2152. Rather than take and dip them, the Master allowed Morpheus to pledge himself and his followers to the Master's service, using them as spies. The capture of a group of Vault Dwellers still living in the demonstration Vault around 2155 led to the discovery of the Vault by the [Unity](Unity.md "Unity"), with the Children formed as a benevolent religion to provide convenient cover for the Unity's activities. The Boneyard became the hub for Unity and Children operations, with missionaries spreading slowly across [New California](New%20California.md "New California").

### 

By 2161, the gang war between the Rippers and Blades gave the Boneyard a reputation as a violent and inhospitable place, a place of hard life, and general desolation. However, it had a reputation for being "cool" among more daring merchants, like the [Crimson Caravan](Crimson%20Caravan.md "Crimson Caravan").

As a result, many avoided the ruined expanse, even though the derelict highways (or "black walkways" as some called them) made travel very easy. The only reason [The Hub](The%20Hub.md "The Hub"), took an interest in the city was because Adytum and the Gun Runners provided excellent trade opportunities. In 2158, Adytum came under the thumb of [Caleb](Caleb%20Fallout.md "Caleb (Fallout)") and his mercenaries, who became the Regulators and turned the town into a work camp, while squeezing the Gun Runners hard, extracting a high price for raw materials they needed to produce their weapons and ammunitions. Since the Gun Runners were unable to move due to the [deathclaw](Deathclaw.md "Deathclaw") infestation that emerged next door, after wiping out the bulk of the Rippers, they were stuck in a holding pattern.

The Rippers were reduced to a small, ultra-violent pocket camped out west of the Followers' library, while the weakened Blades fell prey to the Regulators. Caleb's men rounded Blades in monthly raids, taking them away to Adytum and putting them to work, with summary execution for anyone who resisted. Around 2161, [Razor](Razor%20character.md "Razor (character)") migrated into the city from the Hub, assuming leadership of the motley band of survivors, stuck between Adytum and the proliferating Deathclaws.

Meanwhile, in the southern part of the Boneyard, the Children expanded their power base centered at the [Cathedral](Cathedral.md "Cathedral"), with the Master protected in the demonstration Vault buried deep beneath the Cathedral. The faithful congregated on the surface (ranging from borderline mindless zombies, through thugs hired by the Children, to the [nightkin](Nightkin.md "Nightkin")), preparing for the Crusades to advance the Unity's agenda across [New California](New%20California.md "New California"). While most denizens of the region dismissed them as just creepy cultists, the [Followers](Followers.md "Followers") were the only group to realize that the Children were not what they were on the surface and planned to strike against them.

### 

The Vault Dweller entered the Boneyard in 2162, finding a desolate city. They found many enemies and a few friends in the Boneyard, killing when necessary. Eventually, the dweller infiltrated and confronted the Master beneath the Cathedral on March 3, 2162 and slew him. The Cathedral was engulfed by nuclear fire, wiping out the Children and destroying the Vault together with the Unity's leader. The experience left the Dweller permanently scarred, unable or unwilling to describe his discovery even decades after the fact. The Boneyard thrived in subsequent years, after Caleb's Regulators were deposed (likely with the Dweller's aid), with ghouls who settled down in [Dayglow](Dayglow.md "Dayglow") eventually forming profitable partnerships with its companies after starting salvage operations in [The Glow](The%20Glow.md "The Glow").

In [2189](/wiki/Timeline#2189 "Timeline"), the Boneyard became one of the five founding states of the [New California Republic](New%20California%20Republic.md "New California Republic"), as the capital of the state of Los Angeles. What was once a vast sea of ruins saw major recovery, with the Republic placing the [Republic Reserve](Republic%20Reserve.md "Republic Reserve") in what became formally known as the Angel's Boneyard, while the Followers expanded their operations alongside the Republic, establishing, among others, the [Angel's Boneyard Medical University](Angels%20Boneyard%20Medical%20University.md "Angel's Boneyard Medical University"). They also offered work and education to everyone in need, especially survivors of raider attacks that were not uncommon during the rise of the Republic. [Edward Sallow](Edward%20Sallow.md "Edward Sallow") was a survivor of one such attack in [2228](2228.md "2228"), finding a haven and education at the Followers' library.

By [2281](/wiki/Timeline#2281 "Timeline"), the Boneyard was a vital element of the Republic, though it was not evenly restored. Those parts under firm NCR control and reclaimed offered a good quality of life and the ability for educated people to make a living plying their trade (e.g. as a family doctor). The bad parts of the Boneyard still experienced violence and gang warfare, with choices in life boiling down to joining a gang or the [NCR Army](NCR%20Army.md "NCR Army"). Furthermore, while the [Mojave Campaign](Mojave%20Campaign.md "Mojave Campaign") provided much needed power and water to the NCR heartland, the Boneyard also tied up much of the budget, as the lack of a definite resolution and annexation of [New Vegas](New%20Vegas.md "New Vegas") turned the Campaign into what denizens of the Boneyard saw as a money sink: Consuming money and lives of their relatives for no further gain. As a result, the [NCR Senate](NCR%20Senate.md "NCR Senate") was unwilling to divert funds away from LA and increase funding for military operations, especially with the 2281 elections coming up and many local representatives seeking re-election.

### 

The Boneyard's fortunes turned a dramatic turn after the destruction of [Shady Sands](Shady%20Sands.md "Shady Sands") following the [Second Battle of Hoover Dam](Second%20Battle%20of%20Hoover%20Dam.md "Second Battle of Hoover Dam"). The NCR was forced to retreat, and by [2296](/wiki/Timeline#2296 "Timeline"), the Republic's presence in the region was reduced to an outpost at [Griffith Observatory](Griffith%20Observatory.md "Griffith Observatory") led by [Lee Moldaver](Lee%20Moldaver.md "Lee Moldaver"), seen as just a local warlord. Without the NCR, the region experienced a power vacuum, with most of the territory left up for grabs. Settlements such as [Filly](Filly.md "Filly") and Vault 4 (which also received a substantial number of Shady Sands refugees) became independent, with varying levels of exposure to the outside world. Most people continued on with their lives, though one gang has declared itself as "[the governmint](Govermint.md "Govermint")" with the intent to spread its influence across the city.

In 2296, Moldaver led a group of [raiders](Vault%2032%20raider.md "Vault 32 raider") to infiltrate Vault 32 (whose inhabitants had seemingly wiped each other out circa two years back) and then Vault 33, with the intent of abducting [Hank MacLean](Hank%20MacLean.md "Hank MacLean"), the Vault-Tec employee responsible for the destruction of Shady Sands. As a Vault-Tec employee, he could provide the codes for Moldaver to activate the [cold fusion](Cold%20fusion.md "Cold fusion") reactor she built in Griffith Observatory, which would provide virtually unlimited energy and allow for the reestablishment of order under the NCR. A vital component was to be brought by [Enclave](Enclave.md "Enclave") defector [Siggi Wilzig](Siggi%20Wilzig.md "Siggi Wilzig"), though complications (such as his partial dismemberment and eventual beheading) led to the delivery being made by Hank's daughter, [Lucy MacLean](Lucy%20MacLean.md "Lucy MacLean"). With the delivered component and Hank's codes, Moldaver finally activated her cold fusion generator.

However, the [Knights of San Fernando](Knights%20of%20San%20Fernando.md "Knights of San Fernando"), also on the hunt for Wilzig and tracking his remains to Griffith Observatory, seized [Filly](Filly.md "Filly") and subsequently assaulted the observatory. Despite anti-aircraft defenses, the Brotherhood managed to land troops and fought a pitched battle with casualties piling on both sides. The NCR was wiped out at Griffith, Moldaver killed in action, and the cold fusion generator came under the control of the Brotherhood. Though Moldaver was briefly able to activate the reactor, bringing power back to much of the ruins of Los Angeles, the Knights of San Fernando subsequently took the cold fusion technology with them to [Area 51](Area%2051.md "Area 51") for their own purposes.

The following is a list of locations within Los Angeles and its greater metropolitan area.

### 

* City hall: It was the site of an anti-[RobCo](RobCo%20Industries.md "RobCo Industries") protest in pre-War times.
* [Griffith Observatory](Griffith%20Observatory.md "Griffith Observatory"): An observatory meant for observing space, it was sponsored by Vault-Tec and designated as a "Science & Friendship Center." By 2296, it had been turned into a headquarters and refugee camp by NCR remnants under Lee Moldaver. It was the site of a [major battle](Battle%20of%20Griffith%20Observatory.md "Battle of Griffith Observatory") between the NCR remnants and the Brotherhood in 2296, with the Brotherhood wiping out the NCR remnants.
* [Hawthorne Medical Laboratories](Hawthorne%20Medical%20Laboratories.md "Hawthorne Medical Laboratories"): A campus for a medical research division of the Vault-Tec Corporation.
* [Hollywood](Hollywood.md "Hollywood"): The center of pre-War America's film industry, the area suffered a red scare leading to the blacklisting of various industry figures. After the War, the area seems to be largely deserted; some sections have fully desertified, while others are green but heavily irradiated and flooded.
  + [California Crest Studios](California%20Crest%20Studios.md "California Crest Studios"): The filming lot of the eponymous film studio.
  + [Hollywood Boulevard](Hollywood%20Boulevard.md "Hollywood Boulevard")
  + [Hollywood Forever](Hollywood%20Forever.md "Hollywood Forever"): A cemetery and funeral home in Hollywood.
  + [Hollywood Hills](Hollywood%20Hills.md "Hollywood Hills"): A residential neighborhood located in the Hollywood area. While the main structures of the airport still stand by 2296, the area around it has largely desertified.
    - [KPSS radio station](KPSS%20radio%20station.md "KPSS radio station"): A radio station.
    - [Spencer residence](Spencer%20residence.md "Spencer residence"): The home of [Bob](Bob%20Spencer.md "Bob Spencer") and [Ronda Spencer](Ronda%20Spencer.md "Ronda Spencer") along with their son [Roy](Roy%20Spencer.md "Roy Spencer").
* [Howard residence](Howard%20residence.md "Howard residence"): The home of [Cooper](The%20Ghoul.md "The Ghoul") and [Barb Howard](Barb%20Howard.md "Barb Howard") along with their daughter [Janey](Janey%20Howard.md "Janey Howard").
* [Irvine](Irvine.md "Irvine"): a pre-War city in the Los Angeles metropolitan area.
* [Los Angeles International Airport](Los%20Angeles%20International%20Airport.md "Los Angeles International Airport"): a pre-War airport in the Los Angeles metropolitan area.
* [Santa Monica](Santa%20Monica.md "Santa Monica"): A coastal city northwest of Los Angeles proper, which was chosen as the location of [Vault 33](Vault%2033.md "Vault 33"), connected to Vaults 31 and 32. Its iconic pier has survived the ravages of the [Great War](Great%20War.md "Great War") and remained standing as late as 2296.
  + [BBQ Shack](BBQ%20Shack%20TV%20series.md "BBQ Shack (TV series)"): A restaurant. By 2296, it is the headquarters of a gang proclaiming itself as the new local "[Govermint](Govermint.md "Govermint")."
  + [Santa Monica Pier](Santa%20Monica%20Pier.md "Santa Monica Pier"): A beachside attraction and tourist site.
  + [Santa Monica Boulevard](Santa%20Monica%20Boulevard.md "Santa Monica Boulevard")
  + [Super Duper Mart](Super%20Duper%20Mart%20TV%20series.md "Super Duper Mart (TV series)"): A franchise supermarket. By 2296, it became a semi-automated drug trading and organ harvesting operation. It was protected by the "govermint."
* [Tarzana](Tarzana.md "Tarzana"): an area in greater Los Angeles.
* [Vault-Tec headquarters (Los Angeles)](Vault-Tec%20headquarters%20Los%20Angeles.md "Vault-Tec headquarters (Los Angeles)"): A Vault-Tec headquarters located within the city.
* [Vault-Tec Industries headquarters (Southern California)](Vault-Tec%20Industries%20headquarters%20Southern%20California.md "Vault-Tec Industries headquarters (Southern California)"): a Vault-Tec headquarters located within the city.
* Vaults
  + [Los Angeles Vault](Los%20Angeles%20Vault.md "Los Angeles Vault"): A demonstration vault meant to showcase what vaults were and sell them to the population.
  + [Vault 4](Vault%204.md "Vault 4"): A vault meant to perform various DNA experiments on humans and animals, led by scientists with unlimited authority.
  + [Vault 31](Vault%2031.md "Vault 31"): One of [three interconnected vaults](Three%20Vaults.md "Three Vaults") in the Santa Monica region of the city, meant to act as a cryogenics vault for the pre-war junior executives of Bud Askins breeding program to create a class of "super managers".
  + [Vault 32](Vault%2032.md "Vault 32"): One of three interconnected vaults in Santa Monica, meant to act as a breeding population for Vault 31.
  + [Vault 33](Vault%2033.md "Vault 33"): One of three interconnected vaults in Santa Monica, meant to act as a breeding population for Vault 31.

### 

* [Adytum](Adytum.md "Adytum"): Located on the north edge of Long Beach, a fortified settlement founded by denizens of the Los Angeles Vault, and a major trading center in the Boneyard.
* [Boneyard downtown](Boneyard%20downtown.md "Boneyard downtown"): An expanse of ruined buildings centered around a nightclub in south-west Compton.
* [The Cathedral](The%20Cathedral.md "The Cathedral"): A large church built by the [Children of the Cathedral](Children%20of%20the%20Cathedral.md "Children of the Cathedral") and secretly ran by the [Master](Master.md "Master"). * [Fortress](Fortress.md "Fortress"): The [Gun Runners](Gun%20Runners.md "Gun Runners")' headquarters, located in the northern part of Whittier.

### 

* [Filly](Filly.md "Filly"): A post-War settlement built out of an old junkyard containing old cars and plane parts. Despite the collapse of the NCR in Los Angeles, it was a thriving merchant community including Ma June's sundries. The area is split into a wooded outer area and a downtown built in a crater-like depression. It was forcefully occupied by the Brotherhood around 2296.

## 

* The map serving as the town image for the Boneyard in *Fallout* does not appear to be based on any single map, as it combines features of several, such as the 1950 Automobile Road Map of Metropolitan Los Angeles by the Automobile Club of Southern California (the road layout and land features, though it has differently aligned names of locations) and the 1934 Highway Transportation Survey of Los Angeles County (the coastal lines and the partially-finished Middle Seabreak). The map is distinct in that it also doesn't include the Long Beach Breakwater, making it no younger than 1941, before it was completed.
* For the *Fallout* TV series, [Rise FX](Rise%20FX.md "Rise FX") created a digital version of Los Angeles, mixing real buildings with computer-generated ones to create a retrofuturistic Los Angeles. Some of the features added include numerous skyscrapers and architectural details, a monorail network cutting across the city, and even vegetation. The destruction of Los Angeles seen in the first episode took numerous simulations of nuclear detonations to create a believable image of the apocalypse, with a custom sim for every impact. The slow motion shot posed its own challenges, due to limited reference material showing a nuclear explosion in a city.
