# Power armor

**Power armor**, also referred to as **powered armor**, **powered combat infantry armor**, and **powered infantry armor**, is a type of personal infantry suit intended for combat and heavy-labor roles, similar to that of an infantry fighting vehicle. Power armor development first began before the [Great War](Great%20War.md "Great War") in 2065 by [United States](United%20States%20of%20America.md "United States of America") defense contractors, including [West Tek](West%20Tek.md "West Tek"). Both before and after the war, power armor represents the pinnacle of personal defensive technology, providing its users with excellent energy, ballistic and even radiation protection, as well as drastically increased strength. Organizations such as the [Garrahan Mining Company](Garrahan%20Mining%20Company.md "Garrahan Mining Company") and the [Enclave](Enclave.md "Enclave") would also later develop their own models of power armor. Power armor is often described as a form of mechanized infantry.

In the games, power armor is often a valuable and relatively rare commodity, with only a limited number of available suits populating the game world. Few factions are capable of maintaining or fielding power armor in substantial numbers, fewer still being capable of manufacturing them. Some of the most prominent factions that use power armor are the [Brotherhood of Steel](Brotherhood%20of%20Steel.md "Brotherhood of Steel") and the [Enclave](Enclave.md "Enclave"). Other groups, such as the [NCR](New%20California%20Republic.md "New California Republic"), [Commonwealth raiders](Raiders%20Fallout%204.md "Raiders (Fallout 4)") and the [Gunners](Gunners.md "Gunners") are shown to possess and utilize power armor to a lesser extent.

## 

Due to severe resource constraints limiting the ability to deploy combat vehicles, including tanks and other armored vehicles, the [U.S. military](United%20States%20Armed%20Forces.md "United States Armed Forces") and defense contractors began developing self-powered combat suits in August 2065. Mechanized cavalry units provided with them would retain the mobility of a soldier moving on foot while giving them the ability to use heavy weapons with ease. Research proceeded rapidly. While early prototypes were unsuccessful, lessons learned in the process paved the way for future advances in military sciences, construction, and fusion power.

The most important achievement of this early stage of research was the development of a fusion cell. Crude but effective, the design was unveiled to the world in 2066. It further strained Sino-American relations after the United States refused to share its crude oil reserves with the Asian superpower. The subsequent Chinese invasion of Alaska in winter of 2066 forced the military and its contractors to begin producing the first sets of powered infantry combat armor. In 2067, the first [West Tek](West%20Tek.md "West Tek") production model of powered infantry combat armor, designated [T-45](T-45%20power%20armor.md "T-45 power armor"), was sent off to the front line to defend against the Chinese attackers. The T-45 and its later iterations thus became the first series of power armor to be used, successfully, in battlefield combat, greatly shaping the nature of warfare. Although lacking in mobility, this early model allowed soldiers to carry heavy weapons into combat, becoming key to countering masses of Chinese infantry and [tanks](Chinese%20tank.md "Chinese tank"). China rushed to develop [its own version](Communist%20power%20armor.md "Communist power armor"), but it was years behind the United States.

Anticipating this move, the military invested in the development of the [pulse gun](Pulse%20gun.md "Pulse gun") to counter the threat, should it arise. In a way, China engineered its own demise, as the deployment of the T-45 also led to the development of the West Tek [power armor frame](Power%20armor%20frame.md "Power armor frame"), the basis on which all subsequent armor was manufactured. West Tek continued developing a superior model, designated T-51. By 2069, the company was the single largest defense contractor for the United States government, with the next generation power armor its largest contract. However, the ambitious nature of the project led to many delays and it took nearly ten years to complete the development cycle. In the meantime, the T-45d became the symbol of American might. Various modifications were developed and tested using it as a basis, including the [MP-47A](Prototype%20medic%20power%20armor.md "Prototype medic power armor") unit, featuring an autonomous medical delivery system and rudimentary onboard AI.

In 2074, American T-45 model D (T-45d) power armor units, mechanized cavalry, and infantry divisions invaded mainland China. However, the invasion quickly bogged down as the T-45's proved insufficient to overcome Chinese defenders equipped with new weapons like the [Gauss rifle](Gauss%20rifle%20Operation%20Anchorage.md "Gauss rifle (Operation: Anchorage)"), and its aging design caused logistical issues, as the armor burned through energy cells at a rate that was alarming and unsustainable. Meanwhile the T-51 model power armor remained in development, with various production models being tested in combat conditions at [Fort Strong](Fort%20Strong.md "Fort Strong") in [Massachusetts](/wiki/State#Massachusetts "State"), in order to troubleshoot the design. Even this far into the development cycle, problems were still present, delaying deployment. For instance, the September 2075 iteration had the desired mobility and protection, but were far from stable, and soldiers tended to tip over on hard landings. Gyroscopic stabilizers and shock absorbers were subsequently added to address this issue.

Despite these setbacks, the armor's problems were eventually ironed out and the joint efforts of West Tek and Fort Strong resulted in the first production run of T-51 power armor being sent to China in June 2076. It outperformed the T-45d in every aspect, providing the United States with the means to overcome Chinese resistance on all fronts. Many units were sent to China, where the renewed American offensive finally sent the [People's Liberation Army](Peoples%20Liberation%20Army.md "People's Liberation Army") into retreat. In January 2077, [General Constantine Chase](Constantine%20Chase.md "Constantine Chase") completed the [Anchorage Reclamation](Battle%20of%20Anchorage.md "Battle of Anchorage"), using [winterized T-51b power armor](Winterized%20T-51b%20power%20armor.md "Winterized T-51b power armor") troops as spearhead. The completion of the Anchorage Reclamation also coincided with the introduction of the [T-60 power armor](T-60%20power%20armor.md "T-60 power armor"), developed first as an upgrade of the T-45 series, then spun-off into a separate series.

The [Great War](Great%20War.md "Great War") and the destruction of the [United States](United%20States%20of%20America.md "United States of America") ensured that the T-51 model B (T-51b) and T-60 power armor remained the most advanced designs to see mass production. However, there had also been limited production runs for other models, such as the [T-65 power armor](T-65%20power%20armor.md "T-65 power armor"), preserved and sold by the [Secret Service](Secret%20Service%20Fallout%2076.md "Secret Service (Fallout 76)") in [Vault 79](Vault%2079.md "Vault 79") and the [Enclave Hellfire armor](Enclave%20Hellfire%20armor.md "Enclave Hellfire armor"), prototyped by the [Enclave Research and Development Division](Enclave%20Research%20and%20Development%20Division.md "Enclave Research and Development Division") and later shipped to [Vault 51](Vault%2051.md "Vault 51") in 2080. In addition to these models there were also other advanced prototypes in development at the time the bombs fell: the [U.S. government](United%20States%20government.md "United States government"), sensing the impending arrival of nuclear war, had initiated the development of the next generation of power armor, designated X-01 for usage following the apocalypse. The complexity of the new armor, however, meant development progress was slow. Though a handful of functional prototypes were created before the war, they were buggy and deemed to not be combat-ready. Before the finishing touches needed to iron out the armor’s kinks were completed, the bombs would fall ensuring that very few intact examples of X-01 armor would survive the war. Among these was an experimental model given to the [Nuka-Cola Corporation](Nuka-Cola%20Corporation.md "Nuka-Cola Corporation") as part of a joint military project known as [Project Cobalt](Project%20Cobalt.md "Project Cobalt"). This program attempted to harness the power of Nuka-Cola’s newly created Strontium-90 based [Quantum](Quantum.md "Quantum") compound to help power the armor and fuel other military projects in exchange for giving [the company’s president](John-Caleb%20Bradberton.md "John-Caleb Bradberton") access to [experimental life extension technology](LEAP-X%20program.md "LEAP-X program"). Although both experimental projects were ultimately successful in achieving their goals, the bombs would fall before the Quantum data could be delivered to the military and the research on it was subsequently lost for over 200 years. Knowledge about the X-01 armor itself survived because the schematics for a nearly-functional prototype were sent to the Enclave oil rig by the [Enclave](Enclave%20Fallout%2076.md "Enclave (Fallout 76)") in Appalachia just prior to the bombs falling.

In the years that followed the bombs falling, power armor development was effectively halted. A few post-War organizations such as the [Brotherhood of Steel](Brotherhood%20of%20Steel.md "Brotherhood of Steel") and [Hubologists](Hubologists.md "Hubologists") would succeed in developing new variants of T-51 series armor in the form of [Ultracite](Ultracite%20power%20armor.md "Ultracite power armor") and [hardened power armor](Hardened%20power%20armor%20Fallout%202.md "Hardened power armor (Fallout 2)"). However, these models were only incremental improvements over the base model and never saw widespread deployment. It is known that knights of the West Coast Brotherhood of Steel are responsible for maintaining, along with manufacturing new weapons and power armor (presumably only pre-War designs). Among the benefits of power armor was its recycling system, processing urine into drinkable water, allowing a user to theoretically survive for weeks without water supplies. Water could also be manually replenished by someone outside the suit. Survivors in Pittsburgh would use leftover factory material to create the [Union power armor](Union%20power%20armor.md "Union power armor") sometime prior to 2103, with defenses comparable to pre-war armor, while Raider gangs were able to repurpose power-armor chassis' with makeshift power-armor providing weak protection.

The first true advancement in post-War armor development came in 2198, when the [Enclave](Enclave%20Fallout%202.md "Enclave (Fallout 2)") at the oil rig restarted work on developing advanced versions of power armor using the X-01 prototype as a base. Their initial progress was slow, but a Presidential Order mandating the development of an upgrade in 2215 altered things greatly. Five years later, the first [advanced power armor](Advanced%20power%20armor.md "Advanced power armor") was developed and mass-produced for the use of Enclave field units. While production of the new armor was gearing up, existing T-51 stock was used for training troops on [Control Station Enclave](Enclave%20Oil%20Rig.md "Enclave Oil Rig") and logistical duties.

At some point, the Enclave developed an equally durable but lighter model of armor called the [Advanced power armor Mk II](Advanced%20power%20armor%20Mk%20II%20Fallout%202.md "Advanced power armor Mk II (Fallout 2)") which used cutting edge composite material. However, it was only manufactured in small quantities due to the [destruction of the Enclave's oil rig](Destruction%20of%20Control%20Station%20Enclave.md "Destruction of Control Station Enclave") in [2242](/wiki/Timeline#2242 "Timeline"). The death of key personnel during the event, along with the loss of many Enclave resources was a heavy blow to the survivors. In the wake of the event, the survivors were forced to resort to using subpar models of [Enclave power armor](Enclave%20power%20armor%20Fallout%203.md "Enclave power armor (Fallout 3)") for a time that in many ways were inferior to pre-War models. This state of affairs would continue until segments of the Enclave eventually arrived in the [Capital Wasteland](Capital%20Wasteland.md "Capital Wasteland") and settled into [Adams Air Force Base](Adams%20Air%20Force%20Base.md "Adams Air Force Base"). In the [Capital Wasteland](Capital%20Wasteland.md "Capital Wasteland"), a reexamination of past combat data and older failed prototype armor was commenced by the [Enclave](Enclave%20Fallout%203.md "Enclave (Fallout 3)"), which also researched new materials. The result of this subsequent research was the next-generation [Enclave Hellfire armor](Enclave%20Hellfire%20armor.md "Enclave Hellfire armor"): the pinnacle of post-War power armor design.

## 

All power armor models in the United States share a common heritage and basic design. Each power armor is built around the West Tek power armor frame, with an internalized servomotor system, providing increased strength, resistance to trauma, and radiation protection. The operator controls the armor indirectly, using manipulators that steer the hands and feet while being safely isolated from the environment. Of note is integrated hydraulic shock absorption system which allows the operator to jump from major heights without damage. The safety of airborne deployments depends primarily on the model of the armor, with the survivable height for early T-60 models being about one hundred feet. The survivable fall height increased to more than three times the height in later versions.Naturally, the enhanced strength also allows the use of hard armored plating that would otherwise be impossible for the soldier to carry with ease.

The basic version of each power armor is designated with either model A or Mark I. Subsequent revisions and upgrades are distinguished using sequential letters: B, C, D, E, and F, or Mark II, III, IV, V, and VI. Each represents an incremental upgrade over the preceding model. Beyond general revisions, a large number of upgrades and custom internals has been developed for the various power armor units, to increase their flexibility and performance on the battlefield.

Introduced in *Fallout 4*, fusion cores were implemented as a power source for power armor. Fusion cores allows power armor to operate autonomously. Fusion cores go inside a fusion power plant integrated into the chassis of the suit, typically loaded on the factory floor with fuel to last a hundred years of moderate use. Salvaging power armor is also difficult, as the amount of firepower necessary to accomplish this feat renders the armor useless.

Although the United States military remained the principal operator of power armor, select private corporations also had access to the technology. One of these, the [Garrahan Mining Company](Garrahan%20Mining%20Company.md "Garrahan Mining Company"), used West-Tek’s power armor technology to create a competitive powered mining suit, the [Excavator power armor](Excavator%20power%20armor.md "Excavator power armor"), in a bid to defeat its competitors and secure lucrative contracts for exploiting [Appalachian](Appalachia.md "Appalachia") natural resources. Another Appalachian company, [Grafton Steel](Grafton%20Steel%20company.md "Grafton Steel (company)"), would attempt to develop a heat resistant armor based off of the T-51 for work in their steel mills but would fail to complete it before the bombs fell. Other private companies with access to power armor included both Nuka-Cola and its Maine based rival [Vim! Pop](Vim%20Pop%20Incorporated.md "Vim! Pop Incorporated"). With military permission both would use customized suits of armor for promotional purposes, but the former would take their military relationship one step further and actively contribute to military weapons and armor development via research into their Quantum compound.

The T-45 powered combat infantry armor was one of the earliest designs pressed into service in the Sino-American War. Created in January 2067 by West Tek, the T-45 was sent to [the Alaskan Front](Battle%20of%20Anchorage.md "Battle of Anchorage") to counter the Chinese offensive. The speed at which it had to be deployed, less than two years after the development of power armor began, resulted in the creation of a stop-gap model utilizing existing technologies and manufacturing techniques. However, its deployment changed the nature of modern warfare forever. Although intended to be temporary, the T-45 became the mainstay of American power armor units, especially as subsequent upgrades reduced the impact of the mobility problems that plagued the early production runs.

* [MP-47 prototype medic power armor](MP-47%20prototype%20medic%20power%20armor.md "MP-47 prototype medic power armor") - A power armor prototype that has the ability to administer [Med-X](Med-X%20Fallout%203.md "Med-X (Fallout 3)"), it also features an onboard voice module that is able to sound alerts if it detects an enemy presence. This armor was planned as an automatic medical supply dispenser, meaning troops would not have to be evacuated for medical treatment. It has the same appearance as the standard T-45d. It is also mentioned in its manual that it would administer a lethal dose of chemical substances to deserters, however, this function had not been implemented in the early prototype stages.
* [Tribal power armor](Tribal%20power%20armor.md "Tribal power armor") ([Ashur's power armor](Ashurs%20power%20armor.md "Ashur's power armor")) - A suit of power armor that was repaired and maintained over the years with parts from various sources, causing it to take a rusty yellow color. This results in a unique looking, patchwork suit of power armor.
* [Outcast power armor](Outcast%20power%20armor.md "Outcast power armor") ([Linden's Outcast power armor](Lindens%20Outcast%20power%20armor.md "Linden's Outcast power armor")) - a suit of power armor with a black matte and rust-red paint job, instead of the gray metallic color of ordinary T-45 units.
* [NCR salvaged power armor](NCR%20salvaged%20power%20armor.md "NCR salvaged power armor") - A damaged suit of power armor with its joint servomotors removed by NCR engineers. It is basically just an armored suit crafted out of power armor.
* [Scorched Sierra power armor](Scorched%20Sierra%20power%20armor.md "Scorched Sierra power armor") - The Scorched Sierra power armor is a [T-45d power armor](T-45d%20power%20armor%20Fallout%20New%20Vegas.md "T-45d power armor (Fallout: New Vegas)") that has been heavily modified by the [NCR](New%20California%20Republic.md "New California Republic") for its officers. Unlike [NCR salvaged power armor](NCR%20salvaged%20power%20armor.md "NCR salvaged power armor"), scorched Sierra power armor is still fully functional, retaining its servo-motors.

The T-60 series of powered combat infantry armor is an evolution of the [T-45 power armor](T-45%20power%20armor.md "T-45 power armor") design. Following quiet testing between the government and [West Tek](West%20Tek.md "West Tek"), as part of its long-standing defense contract, the T-60 entered service after the conclusion of the [Battle of Anchorage](Battle%20of%20Anchorage.md "Battle of Anchorage"), mere months before the nuclear apocalypse. The T-60 was used extensively by the U.S. Army in all theaters as well as on the domestic front, enforcing order in the nation.

By the time of the Great War it was considered to be the most advanced model of power armor to see extensive use.

### 

Gameplay article: [Wastelanders](T-65%20power%20armor.md "T-65 power armor")

The T-65 series is a unique set of power armor that saw development right before the Great War, but no production models are known to exist. Schematics were entrusted to the [Secret Service](Secret%20Service%20Fallout%2076.md "Secret Service (Fallout 76)") agents stationed at [Vault 79](Vault%2079.md "Vault 79"). T-65 series utilizes radically different protection principles from the previous power armor series with a departure from whole cast armor pieces to segmented armor layers notable in the torso piece and the pauldrons. This allowed the T-65 series to protect exposed frame sections and other areas that would be thinly armored on other power armor series and greatly increasing protection and survivability.

### 

Gameplay articles: [Fallout 4](X-01%20power%20armor%20Fallout%204.md "X-01 power armor (Fallout 4)"), [Fallout 76](X-01%20power%20armor%20Fallout%2076.md "X-01 power armor (Fallout 76)")

Commissioned by the [Joint Chiefs](Joint%20Chiefs%20of%20Staff.md "Joint Chiefs of Staff"), the development of the X-01 began shortly before the Great War. It represented a radically new approach to designing powered armor, eschewing traditional design philosophies that defined the T series. X-O1 was a high-profile research project meant to supersede previous power armor models. A single suit of X-01 was provided to the [Nuka-Cola Corporation](Nuka-Cola%20Corporation.md "Nuka-Cola Corporation"), as part of [Project Cobalt](Project%20Cobalt.md "Project Cobalt"). While the suit served as a successful proof of concept of using a strontium-90 based plating over the armor, this improvement was not adopted into the armor's schematics before the onset of the Great War. Further development was made impossible due to the outbreak of the Great War.

After the Great War, a select few suits were engineered further and employed by remnants of the U.S. military, like the [Enclave](Enclave.md "Enclave") and [Brotherhood of Steel](Brotherhood%20of%20Steel.md "Brotherhood of Steel"), as well as [the Institute](The%20Institute.md "The Institute"). The schematics for the armor were saved by the Enclave just before the bombs fell, with the organization keeping copies in the archives of their Poseidon and Whitespring facilities, including [Control Station ENCLAVE](Enclave%20Oil%20Rig.md "Enclave Oil Rig"). This allowed the Enclave to create a limited production run of the suits, and for work to eventually continue on the model's development.

Full development was restarted by the Enclave in [2198](/wiki/Timeline#2198 "Timeline"), although it wasn't until [2215](/wiki/Timeline#2215 "Timeline") that the research efforts began in earnest under the presidential mandate, resulting in the creation of the first model of [Advanced power armor](Advanced%20power%20armor%20Fallout%202.md "Advanced power armor (Fallout 2)") in [2220](/wiki/Timeline#2220 "Timeline"). Another group to develop upon the X-01 design, after the war, is the Institute. Despite lacking access to the prototype's schematics, by [2287](/wiki/Timeline#2287 "Timeline"), the Institute had developed a modified polymerized casting mix to coat the armor, as well as other modifications that enhance the user's intelligence. Variants include [prototype X-01 power armor](X-01%20power%20armor%20Fallout%2076.md "X-01 power armor (Fallout 76)"), [Quantum X-01 power armor](Quantum%20X-01%20power%20armor.md "Quantum X-01 power armor") and [X-01-I power armor](Institute%20paint.md "Institute paint").

## 

Power armor appears in every *Fallout* game. T-51b power armor and its hardened version appear in both *[Fallout](Fallout.md "Fallout")* and *[Fallout 2](Fallout%202.md "Fallout 2")* while the latter also introduces the Enclave's advanced variants.

In *[Fallout 3](Fallout%203.md "Fallout 3")*, the [T-45d power armor](Power%20armor%20Fallout%203.md "Power armor (Fallout 3)") is the general power armor that can be found, as most members of the Brotherhood of Steel wear it. The Enclave's redesigned [Advanced Power Armor MK II](Enclave%20power%20armor%20Fallout%203.md "Enclave power armor (Fallout 3)"), [Tesla armor](Tesla%20armor%20Fallout%203.md "Tesla armor (Fallout 3)"), and [Hellfire power armor](Enclave%20Hellfire%20armor.md "Enclave Hellfire armor") are also encountered. A pristine set of T-51b power armor is located in the [Fort Constantine](Fort%20Constantine.md "Fort Constantine") military base (but can only be obtained by doing a specific [quest](You%20Gotta%20Shoot%20Em%20in%20the%20Head.md "You Gotta Shoot 'Em in the Head")), and a [winterized version](Winterized%20T-51b%20power%20armor.md "Winterized T-51b power armor") of the same model can be found in the *Fallout 3* *[Operation: Anchorage](Operation%20Anchorage%20add-on.md "Operation: Anchorage (add-on)")* [add-on](Fallout%203%20add-ons.md "Fallout 3 add-ons"). *[Fallout: New Vegas](Fallout%20New%20Vegas.md "Fallout: New Vegas")* features the [T-51b](T-51b%20power%20armor%20Fallout%20New%20Vegas.md "T-51b power armor (Fallout: New Vegas)") and the [T-45d](T-45d%20power%20armor%20Fallout%20New%20Vegas.md "T-45d power armor (Fallout: New Vegas)"), while also [bringing back](Remnants%20power%20armor.md "Remnants power armor") the advanced power armor Mk I from *Fallout 2*; it also adds a new version, the [Tesla power armor Mk I](Gannon%20family%20Tesla%20armor.md "Gannon family Tesla armor").

*[Fallout 4](Fallout%204.md "Fallout 4")* features the [T-51](T-51%20power%20armor%20Fallout%204.md "T-51 power armor (Fallout 4)") and [T-45](T-45%20power%20armor%20Fallout%204.md "T-45 power armor (Fallout 4)"), as well as introducing the [T-60 power armor](T-60%20power%20armor%20Fallout%204.md "T-60 power armor (Fallout 4)") as the new standard issued power armor for the East Coast Brotherhood of Steel. Also introduced was the [X-01 power armor](X-01%20power%20armor%20Fallout%204.md "X-01 power armor (Fallout 4)"), which was an experimental prototype power armor created right before the bombs dropped. The [raiders](Raiders%20Fallout%204.md "Raiders (Fallout 4)") of [the Commonwealth](The%20Commonwealth.md "The Commonwealth") had also created salvaged power armor, introducing [raider power armor](Raider%20power%20armor%20Fallout%204.md "Raider power armor (Fallout 4)"). *[Fallout 76](Fallout%2076.md "Fallout 76")* features the same power armor as *Fallout 4*, but also introduces the [Excavator power armor](Excavator%20power%20armor.md "Excavator power armor"), which was developed by [Garrahan Mining](Garrahan%20Mining%20Company.md "Garrahan Mining Company"), and the [Ultracite power armor](Ultracite%20power%20armor.md "Ultracite power armor"), which was an armor created with ultracite rather than poly-laminate composite. Updates for *Fallout 76* have introduced a variety of power armor variants used exclusively by certain factions, such as the [Secret Service](Secret%20Service%20Fallout%2076.md "Secret Service (Fallout 76)")'s [T-65 power armor](T-65%20power%20armor.md "T-65 power armor"), the [Hellcat Company](Hellcat%20Company.md "Hellcat Company")'s [Hellcat power armor](Hellcat%20power%20armor.md "Hellcat power armor"), and the [Pittsburgh Union](Pittsburgh%20Union.md "Pittsburgh Union")'s [Union power armor](Union%20power%20armor.md "Union power armor").

Using a much different art style in general, *[Fallout Tactics](Fallout%20Tactics%20Brotherhood%20of%20Steel.md "Fallout Tactics: Brotherhood of Steel")* shows the T-51b model only once in the introduction of the game, and the [Midwestern Brotherhood](Brotherhood%20of%20Steel%20Fallout%20Tactics.md "Brotherhood of Steel (Fallout Tactics)") power armor is used instead in the game, including an advanced version standing in for the [Enclave](Enclave.md "Enclave")'s power armor. In *[Fallout: Brotherhood of Steel](Fallout%20Brotherhood%20of%20Steel.md "Fallout: Brotherhood of Steel")*, two versions ([regular](Power%20armor%20Fallout%20Brotherhood%20of%20Steel.md "Power armor (Fallout: Brotherhood of Steel)") and [advanced](Advanced%20power%20armor%20Fallout%20Brotherhood%20of%20Steel.md "Advanced power armor (Fallout: Brotherhood of Steel)")) based on the T-51b with a similar appearance and a blue color appear in the [Corporate Vault](Corporate%20Vault.md "Corporate Vault") and are created by [Vault-Tec](Vault-Tec%20Corporation.md "Vault-Tec Corporation"). The [*Fallout* demo](Fallout%20Demo.md "Fallout Demo")'s item description references power armor.

### 

* Power armor is endgame armor, with just four suits available (with only two being capable of being obtained peacefully, and only one with a low Speech skill), exclusively from the [Brotherhood of Steel](Brotherhood%20of%20Steel.md "Brotherhood of Steel") at [Lost Hills](Lost%20Hills.md "Lost Hills"): One from [Talus](Talus.md "Talus") for rescuing the initiate, the other from the maintenance bay, where the player can put together their own suit.

* Power armor remains the pinnacle of personal protection, now available in virtually unlimited quantities thanks to the [San Francisco](San%20Francisco.md "San Francisco") vendors. Several other suits are also found in the wild. The player can harden one of the T-51 suits in San Francisco as well.
* Edging the T-51 out is the [advanced power armor](Advanced%20power%20armor.md "Advanced power armor"), available as one unit: The [standard advanced power armo](Advanced%20power%20armor%20Fallout%202.md "Advanced power armor (Fallout 2)")r at [Navarro](Navarro.md "Navarro") and an experimental [Mk II](Advanced%20power%20armor%20Mk%20II%20Fallout%202.md "Advanced power armor Mk II (Fallout 2)") suit at the [Enclave oil rig](Enclave%20Oil%20Rig.md "Enclave Oil Rig").

* The Lone Wanderer must receive [training](Power%20Armor%20Training.md "Power Armor Training") to be able to equip power armor. Non-player characters do not have this restriction.
  + Training may be received at [the Citadel](The%20Citadel.md "The Citadel") from [Paladin Gunny](Gunny.md "Gunny") or by completing the *[Operation: Anchorage](Operation%20Anchorage%20add-on.md "Operation: Anchorage (add-on)")* add-on mission before they can equip power armor. While being given the training, the paladin tells the Wanderer to "relax their muscles" and "let the suit do the work."
* Any weapon that is holstered on a character's back is normally hanging off the right shoulder diagonally with the barrel downwards. However, when wearing any form of power armor the gun is hanging diagonally off the left shoulder with the barrel pointing up. This is because power armor prevents the arm movements necessary to unholster the gun when it is in its normal position. Characters retrieve them under the arm, rather than over it. This was done to prevent clipping.
* Shooting at someone wearing power armor produces metallic sounds, decals, and particles (a metallic \*clang\* and sparks instead of flesh hit sound and blood spurts), despite still causing damage to the wearer. This is also true of the player character.

### 

* Power armor has undergone substantial changes in both size and gameplay mechanics as opposed to *Fallout 3* and *Fallout: New Vegas*. Instead of being treated as a suit of armor that is equipped from the Pip-Boy, it is instead treated as a separate object, a vehicle more than a garment. In order to equip the armor, it must be entered from the outside by pressing the action button, which then causes the suit to unfold to accept an occupant, then reseals itself. In order to operate properly, power armor requires the use of a [fusion core](Fusion%20core%20Fallout%204.md "Fusion core (Fallout 4)").
* For playability and practicality, power armor in *Fallout 4* requires no [Power Armor Training](Power%20Armor%20Training.md "Power Armor Training") perk to use as it did in previous titles (the Sole Survivor, specifically Nate, served in the pre-War U.S. military and thus he may have had access to this technology and training). But in universe, it would seem that some form of training in power armor is still required for the safe or efficient use of the equipment.
* Power armor allows its wearer to not only have superior strength bonuses but its armor components have their own hit points and actively absorb a substantial percentage of incoming damage until they are destroyed. The armor allows the wearer to fall from great heights without suffering any damage and simultaneously causes damaging shock waves to anything close by.
* Companions who enter power armor don't require fusion cores. Power armor increases the carrying capacity of companions much like it would the [Sole Survivor](Sole%20Survivor.md "Sole Survivor"), even more so if it is left bare.

* *Fallout 4*s vehicular power armor returns. However, it can now be deployed in front of the character from the Pip-Boy. In order to equip the armor, it must be entered from the outside by pressing the action button, which then causes the suit to unfold to accept an occupant, then reseals itself. In order to operate properly, power armor requires the use of a [fusion core](Fusion%20core%20Fallout%2076.md "Fusion core (Fallout 76)").
* For playability and practicality, power armor in *Fallout 76* requires no Power Armor Training perk to use as it did in prior titles. In universe, power armor training still exists. Dialogue from terminals at [Camp Venture](Camp%20Venture.md "Camp Venture") indicate that the [Appalachian Brotherhood of Steel](Brotherhood%20of%20Steel%20Fallout%2076.md "Brotherhood of Steel (Fallout 76)") trained new recruits in the use of power armor.
* Power armor allows its wearer to not only have superior strength bonuses but its armor components have their own hit points and actively absorb a substantial percentage of incoming damage until they are destroyed. The armor allows the wearer to fall from great heights without suffering any damage and simultaneously causes damaging shock waves to anything close by.
* Power armor is exclusively owned by the player character after entering it, thus not allowing any other player characters to utilize it. Ownership of the chassis and any associated armor pieces can be released by dropping the chassis, thus abandoning it in place.
* The power armor chassis can be stored in the inventory with all pieces of armor remaining on it, as well as the fusion core.
* Power armor will be recalled to one's inventory after a period of time unless dropped.
