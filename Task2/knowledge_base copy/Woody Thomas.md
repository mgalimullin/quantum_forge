# Woody Thomas

**Woody Thomas** is a resident of [Vault 33](Vault%2033.md "Vault 33") who later moves into [Vault 32](Vault%2032.md "Vault 32") in the [*Fallout* TV series](Fallout%20TV%20series.md "Fallout (TV series)").

## Contents

## 

Woody is a native resident of Vault 33. By 2296, he had become a member of the the Vault's three-person governing council alongside [Betty Pearson](Betty%20Pearson.md "Betty Pearson") and [Reg McPhee](Reg%20McPhee.md "Reg McPhee"), under Overseer [Hank MacLean](Hank%20MacLean.md "Hank MacLean").

However, following the attack on the Vault by [Vault 32 raiders](Vault%2032%20raider.md "Vault 32 raider") that year, Hank was abducted, and a crisis erupted when the Vault's [water chip](Water%20chip%20TV%20series.md "Water chip (TV series)") broke. The governing council was disbanded, and Betty was elected overseer in Hank's absence, beating Reg and Woody. Woody was subsequently reassigned as part of the group sent to resettle [Vault 32](Vault%2032.md "Vault 32"), and was coerced into joining the group despite his initial objections.

## 

### 

#### 

Woody, alongside his Vault 33 council associates Betty and Reg, listens to [Lucy](Lucy%20MacLean.md "Lucy MacLean") give her speech request to be allowed to participate in the Triennial Trade with [Vault 32](Vault%2032.md "Vault 32"). The council unanimously agrees to approve Lucy's marriage application.

Sometime later, Woody is seen greeting the [Vault 32 dwellers](Vault%2032%20raider.md "Vault 32 raider") and their new overseer, [Lee Moldaver](Lee%20Moldaver.md "Lee Moldaver"), who introduces Lucy's new husband, [Monty](Monty%20TV%20series.md "Monty (TV series)"), to her. After the wedding ceremony is held, he and Reg are seen setting up the podium for Overseer Hank, who gives a poignant speech about his daughter's marriage. The two then stand and watch as the [Vault 33 dwellers](Vault%2033%20dweller.md "Vault 33 dweller") dance with their Vault 32 counterparts.

When the Vault 32 dwellers are revealed to have been [raiders from the surface](Vault%2032%20raider.md "Vault 32 raider"), Woody hides behind a doorframe. Upon seeing two people hauling in more food for the party, Woody tells them to take it away, but he is too late; he soon witnesses a [shirtless raider](Shirtless%20raider.md "Shirtless raider") kill one dweller and then shoot the two who were bringing food; Woody himself manages to avoid being shot.

Rushing into a nearby classroom, Woody runs past some raiders who are beating up a Vault 33 dweller, and then hides behind another doorframe when another dweller in the distant corridor is shot and killed by one of [Moldaver's elite guards](Moldavers%20elite%20guard.md "Moldaver's elite guard").

With Hank having been kidnapped by the raiders, Reg, Woody, and Betty hold an emergency assembly with the surviving Vault 33 dwellers in their authority as the governing council. During the assembly, Lucy proposes sending a search team to the surface to save her father. However, none of the council members agree with her; Reg questions her plan, Woody tries to mediate, and finally, Betty stresses the importance of the Vault's security, which both Reg and Woody agree with. Lucy leaves, disconcerted, while Woody and the others continue the assembly. Lucy's brother [Norm](Norm%20MacLean.md "Norm MacLean") tells her that the three council members may have refused out of a desire to preserve their newfound authority. Soon after, Lucy leaves the Vault on her own to search for her father, aided by Norm and [Chet](Chet%20TV%20series.md "Chet (TV series)").

#### 

In the wake of Lucy's departure from Vault 33, Woody and the other two councilmembers hold disciplinary sessions to punish Chet and Norm, firing Chet from his role as gatekeeper. Woody introduces Norm to his session, during which he and Reg alternately lead the conversation, discussing Norm's involvement and the dangers of opening the Vault door. Woody points out that Norm's performance reviews for his various assignments have all listed him as "lacking enthusiasm," and his confirmation notably displeases Woody. However, Betty seems to get the final say in Norm's punishment, and in response to a comment he makes, she reassigns him to deliver food to sixteen surviving raiders who have been imprisoned in the Vault.

Afterward, an assembly is held in the Vault to brainstorm what to do with the surviving raiders. After Woody announces their ethical dilemma, Reg chimes in, claiming that they have been given a prime opportunity to prove to the surface world the Vault's goal of repopulating and building a better world on the surface, though Woody calls this a "positive spin" on the situation. He asks for a collective decision, though Betty tells them to promote an idea for discussion. Thanks to a "conversation" that Woody had with one of the raiders, Reg suggests that the Vault 33 dwellers rehabilitate the raiders to integrate them into their society. A few ideas are then proposed by some Vault 33 dwellers, including [Davey](Davey%20TV%20series.md "Davey (TV series)") and [Marianne](Marianne%20TV%20series.md "Marianne (TV series)"). Norm also raises a suggestion to kill the raiders as retribution, though this is shot down.

At that moment, the [Vault 33 engineer](Vault%2033%20engineer.md "Vault 33 engineer") appears and tries to privately talk with Reg and Woody. However, the two insist that they talk in public, despite the engineer's objections. Reluctantly, the engineer reveals that the Vault's [water chip](Water%20chip%20TV%20series.md "Water chip (TV series)") was destroyed, leaving only two months of water for the Vault 33 residents to survive. Before the engineer could finish, Reg cut off the engineer, and the meeting was adjourned.

#### 

With Hank gone, an election is set to be held for the Vault's new overseer. Woody, Reg, and Betty are all running in the election, and Woody and Reg are seen hanging up posters for their electoral campaign. Later, they meet outside a cell where a semi-sedated Vault 32 raider is being held for interrogation about his background and the kidnapping of Hank.

As they continued talking, the semi-sedated Vault 32 raider regained consciousness and tried to gnaw off his restraints, though Woody advises him against it. The Vault 32 raider curses at Woody and remains uncooperative, so Woody decides to retranquilize the raider and retry the interrogation tomorrow.

#### 

Betty ends up winning the overseer election with 98% of the vote. During the ceremony for Betty's victory, Woody and Reg are in the back, also clapping for Betty. Woody comments "better luck next time" to Reg, and he softly agrees.

Later, Woody stands in front of one of his campaign posters with a forlorn look. Davey walks by and compliments Woody for running a "great" campaign, though Woody notes that he only hung ten posters up. Davey sympathizes, admitting he once ran and lost an election against Hank, but understands that "When things look glum, vote for someone from [Vault 31](Vault%2031.md "Vault 31")." Before leaving, Davey admits to Woody that he voted for Betty.

Soon after, Betty announces that she and the [Vault 31 overseer](Bud%20Askins.md "Bud Askins") have formulated a plan to resettle Vault 32, which will involve transferring many of Vault 33's dwellers into the new Vault. Woody tours Vault 32 along with many other Vault 33 dwellers, unaware that its newly-cleaned facade hides the violence and death that had occurred in the Vault.

#### 

Woody and Betty are present when Norm tries to deliver food for the Vault 32 raiders only to discover they have all been poisoned to death, with [Veronica](Veronica%20TV%20series.md "Veronica (TV series)") taking the blame. Immediately after Betty leaves scene, she sends out the Vault 32 reassignment notices. Woody learns that he is being sent to Vault 32, much to his dismay.

On the day when the new Vault 32 dwellers are to be sent to Vault 32, Woody tries to back out but is stopped by [security guards](Vault%2033%20guard.md "Vault 33 guard"). Unable to bypass them, Woody shrinks back and decides to get back in line for his transfer. Before he leaves for Vault 32, Reg hugs him goodbye, and he's last seen as the Vault 32 door closes with him and his fellow new Vault 32 dwellers behind it.

## 

Woody is naive and portrays himself as optimistic and agreeable. While he attempts to assert himself as a governing voice, he is overshadowed by his governing peers, Betty and Reg, which causes his fellow Vault dwellers to not take him seriously. In truth, he is a coward, and his cowardice occasionally overcomes his positive and agreeable demeanor, such as when he objected to being reassigned to Vault 32. However, when he tries to back out of being reassigned to Vault 32, the vault security guards intimidate him into compliance, which causes him to capitulate and meekly obey the orders of his Overseer.
