# Davey (TV series)

**Davey** is a resident of [Vault 33](Vault%2033.md "Vault 33") who moves into [Vault 32](Vault%2032.md "Vault 32") in [2296](/wiki/Timeline#2296 "Timeline") in the [*Fallout* TV series](Fallout%20TV%20series.md "Fallout (TV series)").

## Contents

## 

Davey is known to have lost to [Hank MacLean](Hank%20MacLean.md "Hank MacLean") in a previous [Overseer](Overseer.md "Overseer") election in Vault 33.

He voted for [Betty Pearson](Betty%20Pearson.md "Betty Pearson") in the 2296 Overseer election.

## 

### 

#### 

Davey and [Reg](Reg%20McPhee.md "Reg McPhee") witness [Norm](Norm%20MacLean.md "Norm MacLean") and [Chet](Chet%20TV%20series.md "Chet (TV series)") help [Lucy MacLean](Lucy%20MacLean.md "Lucy MacLean") leave [Vault 33](Vault%2033.md "Vault 33").

#### 

Davey offers to teach the captured prisoners from [Lee Moldaver](Lee%20Moldaver.md "Lee Moldaver")'s raiders about [William Shakespeare](William%20Shakespeare.md "William Shakespeare"), as well as [Christopher Marlowe](Christopher%20Marlowe.md "Christopher Marlowe"), "when they are ready."

#### 

Davey participates in voting for a new Vault 33 overseer, and while in line, he apologizes to Reg and initially alludes to him that he's voting for [Betty](Betty%20Pearson.md "Betty Pearson") instead of him. On the other hand, Reg understands, and when Davey informs Reg that he's voting for Betty, Reg states that he had that impression.

Afterward, Davey would console a forlorn [Woody](Woody%20Thomas.md "Woody Thomas"), who was reeling over his election loss to Betty. He'd relate with Woody over his election loss, detailing he ran once, but lost against [Hank](Hank%20MacLean.md "Hank MacLean"). Davey would then recite the [Vault 31](Vault%2031.md "Vault 31") phrase, "When things look glum, vote for someone from Vault 31". Before leaving, Davey again confesses that he voted for Betty to Woody.

#### 

Davey discovers he will be relocating to Vault 32, which he positively reacts to. He moves into it with [Steph](Steph%20Harper.md "Steph Harper"), Chet and Woody.

### 

#### 

In contrast to his initial optimism, Davey reveals in a meeting with Steph, the new overseer, that he has been having difficulties navigating through the Vault due to its layout being a mirror of 33's, such as now having to take two left turns instead of two rights to reach the elevator. Despite Steph's attempt to end the meeting quickly, Davey continues by suggesting hanging some signage, then begins rambling about avoiding controversy by instead commissioning a feasibility study on signage, which the overseer frustratedly agrees to.

As he leaves the overseer's office and makes small chat with [Chet](Chet%20TV%20series.md "Chet (TV series)") holding [Chet Jr.](Chet%20Jr.md "Chet Jr."), Davey remarks on his "good fortune" to have found a wife and a child to raise despite Chet's protests against both of those facts. Davey exits the office foyer but turns to face a wall where he thought there was a hallway and has to turn around.

#### 

## 

Davey presents himself as an educated man who is well-versed in the theatrical arts of Shakespeare and Marlowe, but is also shown to have a guilty conscience too. This is displayed when he confesses that he voted for Betty to Reg and Woody instead of them.

## 

Davey appears only in the [*Fallout* TV series](Fallout%20TV%20series.md "Fallout (TV series)").
