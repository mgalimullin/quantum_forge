# Overseer

An **overseer** is the leader of a [Vault-Tec](Vault-Tec%20Corporation.md "Vault-Tec Corporation") [vault](Vault.md "Vault").

## Contents

## 

The overseers were often aware of their vault's purpose as an experiment and helped coordinate it. Overseers were chosen in a variety of ways, with initial leaders chosen before the war, and subsequent overseers afterward. In [Vault 11](Vault%2011.md "Vault 11"), elections were held each year to choose a new overseer, who in turn would be killed as that year's "sacrifice."

The overseer's role and extent of their authority varied from vault to vault; in one vault an overseer might serve in a purely advisory capacity, while another vault might have an overseer with absolute control of every aspect of its residents' lives. [Dr. Stanislaus Braun](Stanislaus%20Braun.md "Stanislaus Braun") distributed sealed orders to each vault's overseer in the weeks before the [Great War](Great%20War.md "Great War"). According to a letter addressed by Dr. Braun to the original overseer of [Vault 101](Vault%20101.md "Vault 101"), disclosing the contents of those sealed orders to the vault's residents or deviating from the experiment were grounds for termination of employment from Vault-Tec.
