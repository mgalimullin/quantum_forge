# Norm MacLean

**Norman "Norm" MacLean** is a resident of [Vault 33](Vault%2033.md "Vault 33") in the [*Fallout* TV series](Fallout%20TV%20series.md "Fallout (TV series)"). He is the inquisitive older brother of [Lucy MacLean](Lucy%20MacLean.md "Lucy MacLean") and the son of [Overseer](Overseer.md "Overseer") [Hank MacLean](Hank%20MacLean.md "Hank MacLean").

He may also be considered another protagonist or deuteragonist of the TV series since after Lucy leaves the Vault, the aftermath is shown through Norm's perspective as he investigates the truth of [Vault 31](Vault%2031.md "Vault 31") and [Vault 32](Vault%2032.md "Vault 32").

## Contents

## 

Norm was born to [Rose](Rose%20MacLean.md "Rose MacLean") and [Hank MacLean](Hank%20MacLean.md "Hank MacLean") of Vault 33 and has one sister, [Lucy](Lucy%20MacLean.md "Lucy MacLean"). Rose suspected the surface world was hospitable and fled Vault 33, angering her husband because he viewed this as dangerous. Rose took Norm and his older sister to live in [Shady Sands](Shady%20Sands.md "Shady Sands"), although the two children seemed too young to have memories of being on the surface. At some point, Hank finds Rose on the surface, but she refuses to return with him. Hank took his children back from Rose, moving them back to Vault 33, where they believed Rose had died of natural causes during their early childhood.

Growing up in Vault 33, Norm became best friends with his cousin [Chet](Chet%20TV%20series.md "Chet (TV series)"). However, he is a black sheep in the Vault's community due to his introversion and irreverent personality. As he came to working age, Norm went from job to job in the Vault, receiving a performance grade of "lacks enthusiasm" at each one. Despite his general apathy, he does care about the community to some degree, especially the people who are close to him.

## 

### 

#### 

Norm discovers the residents of [Vault 32](Vault%2032.md "Vault 32") are all dead just before an attack by [raiders](Vault%2032%20raider.md "Vault 32 raider") posing as [residents](Vault%2032%20dweller.md "Vault 32 dweller"). During the attack, he hides in a storage space under the floor.

After the attack, Norm's sister Lucy proposes they send a search party to the surface to find their kidnapped dad. However, the interim council rejects her proposal based on keeping the Vault safe, although Norm speculates they did so because they don't want to recover their father, and since then, they wouldn't be in charge anymore. He later helps his sister Lucy leave the vault and they share a brief hug.

#### 

Due to helping his sister escape, the Vault council comprised of [Woody Thomas](Woody%20Thomas.md "Woody Thomas"), [Reg McPhee](Reg%20McPhee.md "Reg McPhee"), and [Betty Pearson](Betty%20Pearson.md "Betty Pearson") decide to punish Norm with a demotion. The council scolds Norm, saying he and Chet put their Vault in incredible danger since the radiation levels could have killed everyone and the last vestiges of human civilization. Norm attempts to defend himself saying it was Lucy's idea. When the council points out he could have tried to stop her, Norm replies it was not on his mind. He is reassigned from computer duty to delivering food to the surviving raiders, even after he suggests the raiders deserve punishment.

He later suggested executing the imprisoned raiders in the Vault during an assembly, causing many of the dwellers to be disgusted at him and his idea. Betty rejects the idea, saying murdering them should be out of the question. Norm then, perhaps snarkily, apologizes to Betty for questioning her leadership. After the meeting, [Steph Harper](Steph%20Harper.md "Steph Harper") approaches Norm to tell him she agrees with his idea of executing the raiders.

#### 

Betty brings some pie she made herself to Norm. She comes to talk to him about how his vocalizing his desire to kill the raiders upset the Vault dwellers and that what he says carries weight because he is the only MacLean left in the Vault. Norm expresses how he feels guilty about hiding during the raid, and Betty notices how he feels angry about it. Betty comments that because Norm is clever, how he channels his anger can lead to dark places, and asks him to tread lightly before she leaves.

While delivering food to the raiders, Norm has a small talk with [Veronica](Veronica%20TV%20series.md "Veronica (TV series)") about how she feels the raiders do not deserve cake. Veronica wants some of the cake herself, so Norm secretly gives her the cake instead of the raiders. Once Veronica has left, Norm talks to one raider, [Dervin](Dervin.md "Dervin"), who claims that the raiders did not kill the dwellers of Vault 32, and that the dwellers were not as innocent as Norm thinks.

Afterward, Norm tries to look into files related to Vault 32, but his access is denied. He goes to Chet for help getting into Vault 32, whose door had been damaged in an explosion and was accessible if they moved some rubble. The pair explore Vault 32 and find that the dwellers had all died at least two years ago in what appeared to be internal conflict, meaning the raiders did not kill the residents. They eventually reach the overseer's office, finding the [deceased overseer](Ian%20Jackson.md "Ian Jackson")'s corpse; Norm accesses the overseer's terminal and finds that his mother [Rose](Rose%20MacLean.md "Rose MacLean")'s Pip-Boy was used to access Vault 32 from the surface.

#### 

Exploring Vault 32, still confused as to how the Vault 32 raiders had his mother's Pip-Boy, Norm and Chet discover that many of the Vault 32 dwellers died trying to get into [Vault 31](Vault%2031.md "Vault 31"). They hurriedly return to Vault 33 to try to avoid suspicion, but are seen not far away from the Vault 31 door by Betty, offering only a weak lie for what they were doing.

Later, Norm investigates Vault 33's Inter-Vault trade records and discovers that all of the personnel traded have been from Vault 31 to Vault 33, and all of them have ended up being elected as overseer, with Hank and Betty being the latest ones in this trend. Norm congratulates Betty at the Vault's celebration for her electoral victory. He later visits Chet in [Steph Harper](Steph%20Harper.md "Steph Harper")'s apartment to discuss his findings, but Chet dismisses them, in part because Steph herself is from Vault 31. Norm directly asks Steph about how Vault 31 is different from Vault 33; she only says that its mashed potatoes were better, causing Norm to remark that Hank said the same thing.

They are later confused when Betty takes everyone to a now-cleaned Vault 32, completely devoid of any traces of the violence and death that previously occurred and announces a project to resettle the vault. Norm returns to the now-refurbished overseer's office and is joined soon after by Betty. Knowingly praising her for the successful "cleanup" of the vault, he asks Betty what happened to his mother's Pip-Boy, and is left confused when Betty claims to have buried it with Rose when she died.

#### 

Back in Vault 33, Norm continues to deliver food to the imprisoned Vault 32 raiders. He arrives one day to find all of the raiders dead by poisoning, and Veronica accused of the crime. Later, when the Vault reassignment decisions are released, Norm discovers he will be staying in Vault 33. Soon, the dwellers assigned to Vault 32 prepare to leave, and Norm takes advantage of Betty's preoccupation with the departing dwellers to sneak into her office. There, he hacks into Betty's terminal and communicates with Vault 31's overseer, fooling him into opening the door to Vault 31.

#### 

Norm secretly enters Vault 31 and discovers its overseer, [Bud Askins](Bud%20Askins.md "Bud Askins"), who has had his brain removed and preserved in a rudimentary robotic chassis, described as a "[brain-on-a-Roomba](Robobrain.md "Robobrain")." He soon walks deeper into the Vault and discovers the truth: that all of its residents are cryonically frozen Vault-Tec employees from before the Great War, including his father, intended to reshape the world in the image of Vault-Tec's and Bud's own plans.

Bud refuses to let Norm leave and traps him inside the cryo chamber bay, as he knows too much. Bud implores Norm to enter one of the cryo chambers so that Norm will not starve.

### 

#### 

In [Vault 31](Vault%2031.md "Vault 31"), Norm is still locked in the cryo facility with Bud Askins, having continually refused to enter a cryopod. Bud begins to panic upon discovering that Norm had disabled inter-Vault communications and goes to check on him. Bud is unable to reach Norm as he has sequestered himself down the stairs into the cryopod area, now littered with emptied ration cases. Bud tries to persuade Norm with his "two rational choices": either enter Hank's cryo-chamber or starve to death. Norm asks Bud if he would ever be released from cryonic suspension; Bud says he would, but only upon achieving , which, according to Bud, will occur when everyone on the surface who could challenge Vault-Tec's monopoly on life has died.

Norm stubbornly refuses to be Bud's prisoner and insists that someone would come for him, but Bud asserts that Vault-Tec had prepared for every contingency, including when a vault dweller goes missing, promising that Betty will cover up his disappearance. To further demoralize Norm, Bud shuts off the lights, making it harder to find more food, certain that he'd be more "reasonable" when he grows hungrier.

Bud later checks on Norm again as the young man was lying on the floor. As Bud tried once again to encourage him to take Hank's pod, Norm ignored him, and asked what his father was like and why he was frozen in the first place. Bud boasted that Hank was an ambitious man and that becoming one of Bud's Buds was a highly coveted position in the pre-war era. Then noticing Norm spotted a two-headed [centipede](Centipede.md "Centipede"), he cautioned that consuming the mutated insect would cause him a slow, painful death, and instead offered to inject him with an unnamed poison that will give Norm a quick and painless death, as an alternative to cryo, and that when he dies, his body will dissolve and be filtered from 31 through the ventilation system into the crops for the other Vaults.

Norm refuses Bud's false dichotomy of "two rational choices" and asserts there are plenty of "irrational" ones. Norm approaches Bud and kicks off the syringe limb Bud had threatened him with. Norm tells Bud that he is releasing the Vault-Tec executives. Bud tries to protest the disruption of the Bud's Buds' plan to create a group of super managers, but is kept back with Norm's foot as Norm releases all Vault-Tec executives from their cryo-chambers.

## 

Norm is a clever young man who begins the series as a somewhat timid and apathetic individual but gradually becomes more confident as the series progresses. He seems to enjoy teasing others, such as when he tells Lucy her future husband might be a cannibal. Betty also figures out that Norm likes to upset people, although it is possible Norm simply prefers speaking his mind, even if it makes him seem rude to others. Nevertheless, he maintains a sense of innocence that is common among Vault dwellers who have never been to the surface.

Norm is also a perceptive individual. He appears to be one of the first to figure out that something is wrong with the "dwellers" from Vault 32, later revealed to be raiders.

Over time, Norm is shown to exhibit a great amount of bravery, having the courage to let his sister out of the Vault and exploring the decrepit Vaults 31 and 32. This stems from his regret over hiding in a duct while raiders attacked Vault 33, which spurs him to become more active. He is also shown to be somewhat vindictive, particularly towards the raiders, wishing them dead. The raiders are mysteriously killed because someone slipped rat poison into their food, although it is unclear if Norm is the culprit.

He does not seem to enjoy his family's book club; instead, he prefers to play Pip-Boy games.

Norm MacLean appears in the [*Fallout* TV series](Fallout%20TV%20series.md "Fallout (TV series)"). He was also added to *[Fallout Shelter](Fallout%20Shelter.md "Fallout Shelter")* in update 1.17.0.

## 

* Norm appears in six out of eight episodes in the first season and is the most frequently reoccurring character outside of Lucy, who appears in all eight episodes, and [Maximus](Maximus.md "Maximus") and [the Ghoul](The%20Ghoul.md "The Ghoul"), who appear in seven each.
  + Although Norm can considered a major character of the show, he was not explicitly called a protagonist in any Amazon descriptions for season 1, nor was he featured in promotional marketing that focused on Lucy, Maximus, and the Ghoul. However, Moisés Arias is also credited as part of the main cast in episodes in which he appears, and Norm does play a leading role outside of the three main protagonists and thus could be considered a deuteragonist of the series.
* Norm's actor, [Moisés Arias](Mois%C3%A9s%20Arias.md "Moisés Arias"), is 4 feet and 11 inches (1.50m) tall. Compared to the rest of his family, Hank's actor, [Kyle MacLachlan](Kyle%20MacLachlan.md "Kyle MacLachlan"), is 6 feet (1.83m) tall, and Lucy and Chet are of average height, although it is unclear how tall Rose's actress, [Elle Vertes](Elle%20Vertes.md "Elle Vertes"), is.
* [**^**](#ref_(Age))  Moisés Arias is older than Lucy's actress, [Ella Purnell](Ella%20Purnell.md "Ella Purnell"). However, he is referred to as both older and younger than Lucy in various places:
  + In the [script](The%20End%20script.md "The End script") for "[The End](The%20End%20episode.md "The End (episode)")," Norm is referenced as being 22, while Lucy is said to be 20. However, Norm's official [character description for *Fallout* season 1 on Amazon Prime](Fallout%20season%201%20Amazon%20overview.md "Fallout season 1 Amazon overview") calls him Lucy's younger brother, while the descriptions for *Fallout* season 2 only refers to him as her brother. Despite appearing in the episode script notes, no definitive age is given for either character in the show itself.
